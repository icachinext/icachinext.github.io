## 会议简介

世界华人华侨人机交互协会（International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction，简称“ICACHI”）举办的 **International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing** （ICHEC 2025）会议将于 **2025年11月21日至23日** 在新加坡， **新加坡管理大学** 举办。本次会议主题为： **包容** （Inclusion）、 **智能** （Intelligence）、 **连接** （Connection）。

ICHEC 2025 （前身为Chinese CHI）由世界华人华侨人机交互协会（ICACHI）举办，这一学术会议已成为连接世界范围内的HCI学者、学术界和产业界的重要会议。从2012年起，Chinese CHI 已分别在巴黎、多伦多、首尔、硅谷、广州、蒙特利尔、厦门、巴厘岛以及深圳成功举办。

**往年历届会议发表论文均会收录于ACM Digital Library，并提交EI检索。** 2025年经 ICACHI 理事会商讨，为使会议更具包容性并且吸引全球人机交互领域的研究者参与，会议正式更名为 ICHEC。我们诚邀全球范围内人机交互领域相关的学者、研究人员和业界领袖，分享他们的最新研究成果和实践经验，共同探讨未来人机交互发展的无限可能性。

### 投稿长度

- 投稿的写作应简洁明了，并保证论文长度与论文实际贡献成正比; 论文的长度计算只计算正文内容，不计参考文献、附录、图表的标题等内容的字数。
- **长文长度：** 长文的字数应在5000和12000字之间，建议 **平均字数在7000到8000字** 左右。
- **短文长度：** 短文长度 **应不超过5000字** （单栏，不包括参考文献）。
- **超出长度的长文投稿（大于12000字）和短文投稿（超过5000字）会被直接拒稿，** 除非作者提供有力依据说明超出投稿字数限制是必要的。
- **投稿阶段请使用单栏格式，** 并保证使用会议官方提供的标准投稿模版。使用其他模版格式会导致论文直接拒稿。注意LaTeX和Overleaf模版默认使用双栏模式，需要通过使用 “\documentclass[manuscript, screen, review]{acmart}” 设定改成单栏模式。

### 生成式人工智能使用

- 根据ACM的政策，允许使用生成式人工智能工具创建论文内容。 **当作者使用AI辅助写作或润色，或使用目的超出编辑自身文本的范畴时，必须在论文的致谢部分充分说明。** 注意LaTeX和Overleaf 模版默认不显示致谢章节，如果需要说明生成式人工智能工具使用情况，请确保提交版本在提交论文里面致谢章节已显示。此外，即使生成式人工智能软件工具被用于论文的准备工作，也不应将其列为作者。作者需对论文中的所有内容承担全部责任，包括可能由人工智能生成的任何内容。
- 自2024年起，ACM规定所有拟发表论文 **均需接受AI内容检测。** 为帮助作者顺利完成检测流程，ACM指定了 **官方检测机构 Morressier** （www.morressier.com）以确保检测结果的可靠性。投稿作者可选择在投稿前或论文录用后进行检测并根据建议进行修改，直至符合发表标准。

### Paper Length

Concise and succinct writing is strongly encouraged, and the paper length should be proportional to its contribution.

The following paper length requirements exclude references, figure/table captions, and appendices.

- **Full Paper:**
- Submissions should be between 5,000 and 12,000 words (about 7,000-8,000 words on average).
- **Late-breaking Work:**
- Submissions should be up to 5000 words (single column, excluding references).
- **Submissions with Excessive Length:**
- Full paper submissions of more than 12,000 words and late-breaking work submissions of more than 5000 words **will be desk-rejected,** unless a clear and strong justification is provided.

**A single-column format must be used for the reviewing phase,** and the authors are requested to use the correct template. Use of different templates or formats may result in desk rejection. Note that the LaTeX and Overleaf templates are double-column by default; you can change it to a single-column format by using “\documentclass[manuscript, screen, review]{acmart}”.

### Use of Generative AI Tools

Following the policy of ACM, **the use of generative AI tools to create content is permitted, but it must be fully disclosed in the acknowledgements section of the paper when such tools are used for purposes beyond editing the author’s own text.**

Note that the LaTeX template defaults to hiding the acknowledgements section while in review mode – please make sure that any LLM disclosure is available in your submitted draft. Generative AI software tools should not be listed as authors even if they are used in the preparation of a paper. Authors will take all responsibility for all content in their paper, including any content that may have been generated with AI.

**For more details of the ACM policy, please refer to the official integrity check criteria and frequently asked questions:**

[https://www.acm.org/publications/icps/integrity-check-criteria](https://www.acm.org/publications/icps/integrity-check-criteria)

[https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/frequently-asked-questions](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/frequently-asked-questions)

**To ensure the publication quality and integrity, ACM has requested that all the accepted papers will undergo LLM-generated text detection by the designated AI-check platform Morressier** (www.morressier.com) before they can be officially published. The authors can decide whether they want to do an AI check first by themselves before the submission.
