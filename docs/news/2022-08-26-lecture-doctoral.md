## 【讲座简介】

在这个全球链接的时代，源自中国案例的HCI研究不仅仅是本土的， 更需要用跨文化的全球视角来观照和解读。近年来去殖民主义（decolonial）思潮和反种族主义（anti-racist）思潮在欧美学界方兴未艾， 来自第三世界的中国理工学者该如何理解这些思潮，用什么样的思辨框架呈现中国本土HCI科研创新的精彩，从而和国际学术思潮高效对话？孙华彤博士在美国多年研究跨文化用户体验设计，全球设计，多元文化设计基础上，介绍如何跨越文化差异，把差异转化为设计资源， 从而在全球化语境下更好地做赋能设计，讲更有感染力的中国故事。

HCI design practices are not local any more in this globally linked world. All the design we are working on today is connected with global design. Global design expertise and vision is required for every design researcher. This talk will introduce a decolonial design rhetoric that celebrates cultural differences against the single-world ideology out of the Euro-American historical experiences. Based on the interconnectedness—a core concept of global studies (Giddens; Appadurai; Ong), this framework links micro with macro, traces global mobilities, and advocates situated positionality in this increasingly globalized world. It articulates how “discursive affordances” mediates between structure and agency to redress asymmetrical relations of everyday life in the global power hierarchy, turns differences into design resources for “togetherness-in-difference” (Mao), and co-creates culturally sustaining value propositions for local empowerment and global innovations. With a close look at the development ideology behind the dominant Western design paradigms, it provides suggestions for articulating China-themed HCI research amidst the international movement of social justice-oriented design.
