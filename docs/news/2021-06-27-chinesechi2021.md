**由世界华人华侨人机交互协会（ICACHI）举办的Chinese CHI 2021 会议将于2021年10月16日至17日在线上举办，并免费开放同步直播。**

Chinese CHI 大会是连接世界范围内华人人机交互学者、学术界和产业界的重要会议，自2012年起，已分别在法国巴黎，加拿大多伦多，韩国首尔，美国硅谷，中国广州，加拿大蒙特利尔，中国厦门以及线上成功举办了八届。

作为华人世界人机交互领域的重要会议， **Chinese CHI 大会邀请到了国际知名院校及企业的资深学者担任本次的组委会成员。**

### GENERAL CHAIR

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-02.jpg)

## 翟树民 Shumin Zhai

Google Research

**Shumin Zhai** is Principal Scientist at Google, where he leads and directs research, design, and development of human-device input methods and haptics systems. He was named one of ACM's inaugural class of Distinguished Scientists in 2006 and Member of the CHI Academy and Fellow of the ACM in 2010. His research career has contributed to both foundational models and understandings of human-computer interaction (HCI) and practical user interface designs, inventions, and flagship products.

### PROGRAM CHAIRS

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-03.jpg)

## 刘灿 Can Liu

City University of Hong Kong

**Can Liu** is an assistant professor at the School of Creative Media, City University of Hong Kong. Her research is about designing future interfaces with ubiquitous technologies based on empirical user studies. She works with heterogeneous displays, voice interfaces and physical computing to augment our interaction with the physical world.

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-04.jpg)

## 胡军 Jun Hu

Eindhoven University of Technology

**Jun Hu** is an Associate Professor in Design Research on Social Computing, at the Department of Industrial Design, Eindhoven university of Technology. He is a Distinguished Adjunct Professor at Jiangnan University and a Guest Professor at Zhejiang University. His research interests include social computing, cyber-physical systems, IoT, HCI, and design education.

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-05.jpg)

## 冯桂焕 Guihuan Feng

Nanjing University

**Guihuan Feng** is an associate professor of Nanjing University, China. Her research interests include natural human-computer Interaction, multimodal interaction, software usability and software experience design, especially in pen/sketch-based interaction. She is a committee member of the International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction.

### PAPER CHAIRS

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-06.jpg)

## 涂华伟 Huawei Tu

La Trobe University

**Huawei Tu** is a lecturer at La Trobe University, Australia. His research area is Human-computer Interaction, with special interests in multimodal interaction, user interface design and user experience design. He is a committee member and the secretary general of International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction.

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-07.jpg)

## 赵健 Jian Zhao

University of Waterloo

**Jian Zhao** is an assistant professor in the Cheriton School of Computer Science at the University of Waterloo, where he directs the WatVis (Waterloo Visualization) group. His research interests include information visualization, human-computer interaction, and data science. His work contributes to the development of advanced interactive visualizations that promote the interplay of human, machine, and data.

## LATE BREAKING WORK CHAIRS

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-08.png)

## 范明明 Mingming Fan

The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

**Mingming Fan** is an Assistant Professor in the Thrust of Computational Media and Arts and an Affiliated Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology starting in July 2021. He has been an Assistant Professor at Rochester Institute of Technology since 2019. His research interests are in Human-Computer Interaction and Accessibility. His research contributes to Assistive Technology, AI-Assisted UX Analysis Methods, and novel VR & Sensing Techniques.

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-09.jpg)

## 王冠云 Guanyun Wang

Zhejiang University

**Guanyun Wang** is an Assistant Professor at Zhejiang University, College of Computer Science and Technology. His research revolves around design and fabrication. As a designer, he has sought emerging interaction methods from science and nature. As a researcher, he has worked on designing and fabricating smart materials that are responsive, adaptive, and tunable. His works have been published and exhibited across science, engineering, design, and art.

### PUBLICATION CHAIR

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-10.jpg)

## 朱克宁 Kening Zhu

City University of Hong Kong

**Kening Zhu** is an Associate Professor jointly appointed by the School of Creative Media and the Department of Computer Science, City University of Hong Kong. Kening Zhu received his PhD degree from the National University of Singapore, and his bachelor degree in Computer Science from Huazhong University of Science and Technology, China. His research mainly focuses on multimodal and embodied human-computer interaction, including gesture-based interfaces, haptic interfaces, and tangible user interfaces.

### TECHNICAL CHAIR

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-11.jpg)

## 王晨 Chen Wang

Kochi University of Technology

**Chen Wang** is a third-year Ph.D. student of human-computer interaction from Kochi University of Technology in Japan. He is a student board member of the International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction. His main research directions are Human-Engaged Computing and computational aesthetics. His supervisor is Prof. Xiangshi Ren (xiangshiren.com).

### PUBLICITY CHAIRS

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-12.jpg)

## 林旭 Xu Lin

Eindhoven University of Technology

**Xu Lin** is a PhD Candidate of Industrial Design from Eindhoven University of Technology. She received an M.A. from Academy of Arts & Design, Tsinghua University. Her current research looks into the specific context of long-term institutional care, investigating the possibilities of interaction design to support elderly people’s social connectedness through facilitating connections between the inside and outside world.

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-13.jpg)

## 安鹏铖 Pengcheng An

University of Waterloo

**Pengcheng An** is a postdoctoral researcher in the Cheriton School of Computer Science at the University of Waterloo. He received his Ph.D. from Eindhoven University of Technology on Peripheral Interaction and Ambient Intelligence, and M.Sc. from Sichuan University. His research encompasses the design and development of Calm Technology, Seamless Interaction, and emerging interactive systems for augmenting the future of work.

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-06-27-chinesechi2021-14.jpg)

## 刘伟 Wei Liu

Beijing Normal University

**Wei Liu** , PhD, associate professor, is director of User eXperience (UX) Master at Faculty of Psychology at Beijing Normal University. His research interests include developing methods, techniques, and tools to optimize the design process for user centered interaction design.

Guangdong University of Technology
