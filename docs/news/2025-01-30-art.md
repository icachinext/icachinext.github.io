2024年11月22日至25日，由世界华人华侨人机交互协会（ICACHI）与南方科技大学联合主办的第十二届国际华人人机交互会议在深圳南方科技大学成功举行。本次大会以“创新·创造·整合”为主题，吸引了来自全球各地的500余名专家学者参会，共同探讨人机交互领域的前沿发展。

其中，Chinese CHI2024数字艺术展书写了浓墨重彩一笔，通过跨学科融合的创新视角表现了本次大会的主题。展览包含了四个单元：改善生活、模糊边界、情感界面、文化融合，旨在鼓励创作者打破传统界限，将创新思维与技术融合，探索突破常规的人机交互模式。

### BROKEN: A Multi-Modal Interactive Experience to Support Psychological Capital through Stress Breaking

**作者：**

Mirna Zordan\
Yuting Xue\
Yong Zhao\
Luwen Zhang\
Southern University of Science and Technology

**简介：**

本互动体验以心理资本（PsyCap）和自我效能概念为基础。通过利用生物数据检测和处理以及动作捕捉系统，压力水平可通过脑电图实时可视化，而随后的互动过程可触发个人对压力水平的反应。

This interactive experience builds upon the concept of psychological capital (PsyCap) and self-efficacy. By utilizing bio-data detection and processing, together with a motion capture system, stress levels are visualized in real-time through EEG, while a following interaction process can trigger individuals' responses to it.

### EMBERS - Improve survival chances beyond critical 72-hour period for those trapped in rubble from conflicts and disasters by maintaining a positive mental attitude through RF communication and HCI method

**作者：**

Mengze Zou\
Imperial College London & Royal College of Art\
Zhejun Zhang\
China Academy of Art\
Xinhang Guo\
The University of Manchester\
Gareth Loudon\
Supervisor, Royal College of Art\
Stephen Green\
Supervisor, Imperial College London

**简介：**

EMBERS 通过建立双向通信，帮助那些被困在废墟中的人保持求生意志，直到救援人员到来。它还能让救援队利用低成本设备找到他们，这在缺乏穿透雷达的欠发达地区至关重要。EMBERS 硬件由微控制器、低功率射频收发器、振动电机、压电陶瓷和电池组成，体积小巧。该装置可通过手动或外部按压激活。研究表明，模仿深呼吸频率的振动可以在潜意识中诱导深呼吸，帮助被困者减轻焦虑和恐慌。当他们激活自己的设备时，设备就会振动并发出信号，远程激活附近的其他 EMBERS 设备。彼此设备的持续振动会让被困者意识到自己并不孤单，从而减少孤独感。如果被困者的双手受到限制，远程激活可以确保设备仍然能够工作。救援人员也可以通过相同的无线电频率远程激活设备。不同的预设信息允许设备切换到快速振动模式，向被困者发出救援队就在附近的信号，帮助他们保持求生意志。

EMBERS helps those trapped under rubble maintain their will to survive by establishing two-way communication until rescue arrives. It also enables rescue teams to locate them using low-cost devices, crucial in less developed areas lacking penetrating radar. The EMBERS hardware consists of a microcontroller, low-power RF transceiver, vibration motor, piezoelectric ceramic, and battery, integrated into a compact size. The device can be activated by manual or external presses. Research shows that vibrations mimicking deep breathing rates can subconsciously induce deep breathing, helping the trapped individuals reduce anxiety and panic. When they activate their device, it vibrates and sends a signal to remotely activate other nearby EMBERS devices. Continuous vibrations from each other's devices reduce loneliness by making trapped individuals aware they are not alone. If a person's hands are restricted, remote activation ensures the device still works. Rescue personnel can also remotely activate devices on the same radio frequency. Different preset information allows the device to switch to a rapid vibration mode, signalling the trapped person that the rescue team is nearby, helping maintain their will to survive.

**简介：**

这件作品将风景画分为虚、实两部分，用户和人工智能各创作一部分。然后，两部分合并，虚拟部分动态演化，形成一幅完整的动画山水画。通过在人机交互中分配绘画任务，该作品展示了美学和谐。

This artwork divides landscape paintings into Xu and Shi parts, where the user and AI each create one. The two parts are then merged, with the virtual part dynamically evolving, resulting in a complete animated landscape. Through the allocation of painting tasks in human-AI interaction, the artwork showcases aesthetic harmony.
