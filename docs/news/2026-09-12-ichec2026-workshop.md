## 会议简介

世界华人华侨人机交互协会（ International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction，简称 ICACHI）举办的 International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing **（ICHEC 2026）** 将由 **澳门城市大学** 承办，于 **2026 年 11 月 23 - 26 日在中国澳门，永利澳门（Macau Wynn）举行。** 本次会议主题为：共融（Inclusivity）、体验（Experience）、创新（Innovation）。

## 征稿范围与形式

我们鼓励 **涉及 HCI 当代和新兴领域** 的提案，支持结构化讨论、制作、批评或社群建设的活动形式。 **主题包括但不限于：**

- 新兴交互范式与用户体验设计
- 新兴 HCI 方法与设计实践
- 新兴研究社群、跨学科 HCI 主题，以及区域或文化视角
- 负责任 AI、无障碍、健康、教育、创意工具与社会影响

## Conference Overview

The International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction ( ICACHI ) is delighted to announce that the **International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing ( ICHEC 2026 )** will be hosted by the City University of Macao and held at **Wynn Macau, Macao, China,** from **November 23rd to 26th,** 2026. The conference theme is **“Inclusivity, Experience, Innovation”.**

## Topics and Scope

We invite proposals that address **contemporary** and **emerging areas** within HCI, supporting activities that foster structured discussion, making, critique, or community building. Topics of interest include, **but are not limited to:**

- Emerging interaction paradigms and user experience design
- Emerging HCI methods and design practices
- Emerging research communities, interdisciplinary HCI themes, and regional or cultural perspectives
- Responsible AI, accessibility, health, education, creative tools, and social impact
