## 项目介绍

2022年第二届HCI·X暑期研究项目将继续由香港城市大学，威斯康星大学-麦迪逊，马里兰大学-巴尔的摩，圣母大学，滑铁卢大学，昆山杜克大学，西北工业大学等国内外人机交互领域知名教授联合主办。

本项目旨在帮助优秀学生了解人机交互领域前沿研究课题，通过实践参与研究，熟悉人机交互的研究方法和思路，并尝试利用新兴人机交互技术解决重要的社会、科技、人文问题。项目将涉及的主题涵盖人机交互领域的多个方面，包括新兴交互系统、交互设计、语音交互、社会计算、AR/VR、无障碍设计、隐私与安全、数据可视化、交互式人工智能等课题。

学生通过该项目可以：

1）学习人机交互的设计及研究方法论，了解并实践定量、定性、及各种人机交互课题的研究和设计方法，了解人机交互领域的论文写作方法；

2）根据实际表现，优秀学生可以获得教授推荐信；

3）有机会和相关导师进一步深入交流关于博士项目的申请，甚至拿到导师的预录取；

4）在导师的带领下将科研成果发表顶级学术期刊/会议论文（作者顺序按照贡献排序)；

5) 参与来自国内外知名院校导师主办的研讨会、工作坊、研究生和博士申请答疑等活动中，与其他有志人机交互领域研究的同学合作交流；

6）获得海外研究生申请与科研合作的社交机会。

2022年，HCI·X 暑期项目将招募若干远程工作的实习生参与到各位导师的多个人机交互课题研究中。申请者须全职参与到项目中至少三个月，每周至30个小时。第一批申请于3月31日截止，第二批申请于4月30日截止。项目导师将会于4-5月开始面试，申请结果将由导师团队综合决定。

## Can Liu

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2022-03-14-call-01.png)

Dr. Can Liu (https://sweb.cityu.edu.hk/canliu/index.html) is an assistant professor at the School of Creative Media, City University of Hong Kong. Before joining CityU, she was a postdoc researcher at the University College London, and obtained her PhD in HCI from INRIA Saclay and the University of Paris-11. Her research is about developing future interfaces based on studies of human natural behavior in areas including voice interfaces, cross-display and spatial interfaces. Can has 10 years of track record of publishing at top HCI conferences and journals, is a winner of Google Faculty Research Award, SIGCHI Best Paper and Honourable Mention award. She has been a program committee member and reviewer for CHI conferences and serves in the organizational committees of CHI, UIST, ISS and Chinese CHI.

**Research areas:** Natural User Interface, Voice/Multimodal interface, Cross-device Interaction, 3D Manipulation, Remote Work/Life in Metaverse

## Zhicong Lu

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2022-03-14-call-02.png)

Dr. Zhicong Lu (https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/~zhiconlu/ ) is an assistant professor at the Department of Computer Science, City University of Hong Kong. ## Xin Tong

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2022-03-14-call-04.png)

Dr. Xin Tong (https://xintong.ca/) is an Assistant Professor of Computation and Design at Duke Kunshan University. Previously, Xin worked as a postdoc fellow at Stanford University with funding from the Canadian NSERC postdoc fellowship. She received her Ph.D. in School of Interactive Arts and Technology, Simon Fraser University, Canada. Her work largely centers on understanding, designing, developing, and evaluating interactive and intelligent technologies in the field of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), such as VR/AR, games, social computing, and AI. She deeply embeds a design research approach, working in partnership with parents/children, older adults, clinicians, caregivers, and patients frequently to solve their problems and reach their goals through a user-centered framework. Xin’s work has been published at top-tier conferences and journals, including CHI, CSCW, JMIR, Frontiers, PervasiveHealth, and so on. Further, she has been committee chairs for CSCW and CHI and an expert reviewer for CHI, CSCW, DIS, JMIR, Frontiers, Nature’s Scientific Reports, etc. Recently, Xin has been working on AI-generated virtual agents, pets, and environments for personalized learning and wellbeing (e.g., autistic children, older adults, and general stress-management), plant(nature)-human interaction, VR rehab, and AR co-creation art project.

**Research areas:** XR, Games and Play, Virtual Agents (Human and Pet), Wellbeing, Aging and Health, Social Computing, NFT and Blockchain, Design, Human-Nature Interaction, Tangible Computing

## 项目内容

本届HCI·X 暑期项目将针对人机交互领域多个前沿课题开展研究工作，通过双向选择的方式匹配每位指导教师，研究方向以及实习生。请申请者报名时在统计表格中填写最希望合作的1-3位导师姓名，及不超过3个感兴趣的研究方向。

## 可期成果

所有项目的学术研究成果将在指导老师的指导下完成学术论文写作，并提交到人机交互或者其他相关领域的国际顶级学术会议或期刊进行评审和发表，例如ACM CHI, CSCW, UIST, Ubicomp, WWW, DIS, IUI, CHI Play, TEI, ISS, SOUPS 等。

## 申请要求与说明

1. 申请者须具有较高的英语听说、阅读及写作水平，如有相关成绩证明（雅思、托福、GRE等语言测试）请在简历文件中提供；

2. 申请者须为高校、研究机构在校生或毕业生，攻读计算机科学与技术、电子工程、工业工程、信息科学、交互设计、数字媒体、工业设计、信息管理学、传播学、心理学等相关专业，或者拥有等同的专业技术能力；

3. 有人机交互、人因工程、人工智能等方向的研究经历的申请者优先考虑；
