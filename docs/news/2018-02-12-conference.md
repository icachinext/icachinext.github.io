“2019年度国际华人华侨人机交互学术会议（Chinese CHI 2019）现征集承办方。本次会议预计于2019年5月中旬至8月之间召开，届时将吸引海内外众多人机交互领域学生和学者，以及企业界人士参会。作为华人华侨界的人机交互顶尖研究论坛，会议主题涵盖人机交互的各个领域，包括用户体验设计、软件工程、人因工程、信息系统、人工智能、娱乐科技、社会科学和艺术创意等多个学科。历届会议分别在法国巴黎（2013）、加拿大多伦多（2014）、韩国首尔（2015）、美国硅谷圣何塞（2016）以及中国广州（2017）成功举办，并即将于今年4月加拿大蒙特利尔举办新一届会议。2019年承办方征集说明详见下文。征集截止时间为2018年3月1日。”

International Symposium of Chinese CHI(ChineseCHI) is the premier conference organized by the International Chinese Association of Computer-Human Interaction (ICACHI), held annually as the principal international forumfor reporting outstanding research and development of computing, design,behavioral science and engineering in major world centers in China, USA, Canadaand Europe since 2013. In the recent past, ChineseCHI 2017 was held in Guangzhou, China, the world’s largest urban area. The language used in the conference is English and the conference proceedings is published to ACM Digital Library. The conference series is sponsored by Huawei, Google, Autodesk, HTC and other organizations. Please see [http://ICACHI.org](http://ICACHI.org) for more information.

ICACHI hereby invites bids to host the 7th ChineseCHI conference. The received bids will be evaluated by the steering committee and the ICACHI Board. The final selection of the ChineseCHI 2019 site will be made at a board meeting in 2018 based on the merits of the proposals and when the proposalsare submitted. The finalist will be announced during ChineseCHI 2018 in Montreal, Canada.

## BID REQUIREMENTS

All proposals must be written in English or Chinese with a readable font not smaller than 12 point and margins not less than one inch on all sides. The bid should address each aspect outlined below but not exceed 15.000 characters (approx. 6 pages). The narrative should be concise but provide sufficient details on the following items:

## Organizers

Conference organizers should have knowledge of the CHI community(e.g., having attended or published regularly at CHI or ChineseCHI) and have experience with conference organization. The diversity of the organizing committee is highly encouraged and preferred. Please attach short bios of the key hosts/organizers and roles of individuals, including the conference chair(s) and program chairs to the proposal.

## Location

We regularly rotate the conference locations to engage with communities around the globe. For ChineseCHI 2019, p riority will be given for proposals hosted in China. Since ChineseCHI is an international forum, we expect that attendees travel from different parts of the world to the conference location. It is important that the proposed location is conveniently located for all attendees.

## Time

ChineseCHI 2019 will be held between mid-May 2019 and the end of August 2019. A bid should include a proposed time within this range with key dates (e.g., paper submission and notification) and innovative events/sessions. The choice of time should also take into account the weather conditions of the proposed location to avoid potential weather-caused travel delays and potential conflicts with other conferences and their submission deadlines in 2019 and 2020.

## Conference Venue

Please describe the proposed conference venue from the following aspects.

- **Capacity.** The main conference was traditionally a single-track, typically with around 250 attendees. The conference also includes workshops, forums and break-out sessions that need to be hosted in separate rooms in parallel. To encourage interaction among the attendees, during the main conference, it is also desirable to have additional space at the venue to put up posters and demonstrations for the attendees to visit during designate sessions and coffee breaks.
- **Facilities** . The venue should be centrally located for attendees to reach quickly by ground transportation and have access to other amenities (e.g.,restaurants). The venue should provide basic conference facilities, such as internet access and projectors.
- **Banquet and ICACHI meeting** . Traditionally, ChineseCHI will host a banquet dinner on the first day of the conference and an ICACHI dinner meeting before the night of the conference. While the location(s) of the reception and dinner are not required to be co-located with the venue, the above location criterion still should be used to minimize the travel for the attendees.

## Accommodation

Most attendees will need accommodation during the conference period. One of the responsibilities of the conference hosts/organizers is to locate and reserve a bulk of affordable lodging for attendees. Please describe the proposed accommodation options, including the price range of the hotel rooms and the distance to the main venue.

## Accounting & Finance

The proposal should include a provisional budget plan outlining the cost of major items including the rental of the conference venue, cost/per person for banquet and meetings, and the estimated conference registration fee and accounting.

## Sponsorships & Industry Involvement

The proposal should also include a plan to attract sponsorship and engage with industries surrounding the ChineseCHI community. Target audience, potential gains a sponsor could receive and advertising should also be identified in this section.

This call is open to the all societies/groups worldwide. Proposals should be sent to the ICACHI Secretariat (office@icachi.org) no later than **March 1, 2018** . Please allow 24 hours for electronic receipt confirmation from ICACHI.
