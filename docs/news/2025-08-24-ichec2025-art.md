## 会议简介

世界华人华侨人机交互协会（International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction，简称“ICACHI”）举办的 **International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing** （ICHEC 2025，前身为Chinese CHI）会议将于 **2025年11月21日至23日** 在新加坡， **新加坡管理大学** 举办。本次会议主题为： **包容** （Inclusion）、 **智能** （Intelligence）、 **连接** （Connection）。

ICHEC 2025 的 **交互与艺术作品（Interactivity & Artwork）** 单元诚邀能够批判性或创造性呼应大会主题 “Human-Engaged Computing” 的互动演示、系统/界面原型、创新制品、艺术构想与其他互动体验。 **我们尤其鼓励能让参会者亲身参与与互动的投稿。**

## 投稿范围与形式

欢迎包括但不限于以下形式的作品：

- 系统与界面原型
- 创新交互装置与制品
- 艺术构想与多媒体作品
- 其他创造性互动体验

**ICHEC 2025**

## Conference Overview

The International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI) is delighted to announce that the **2025 International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing** （ICHEC 2025) will take place in **Singapore Management University,** Singapore, from **November 21st to 23th, 2025.** The conference theme for this year is **"Inclusion. Intelligence. Connection".**

**The Interactivity & Artwork track of ICHEC 2025** invites interactive demos, system/interface prototypes, innovative artifacts, artistic visions, and other creative experiences that critically or creatively reflect the conference theme “Human-Engaged Computing”. **We especially encourage submissions that actively engage conference participants.**

## Submission Guidelines

Submit via EasyChair:

![配图](https://media.icachi.org/news/2025-08-24-ichec2025-art-01.png)

**[https://easychair.org/account2/signin?l=4690469135012500472](https://easychair.org/account2/signin?l=4690469135012500472)**

Each submission must include:

- Extended abstract (up to 6 pages, single-column, ACM Primary Article Templates)
- Technical specifications & requirements document (no page limit)
- One still image (min. 800×600 pixels)
- A short video (under 2 minutes)

**At least one author must register and present the work during the interactivity session.**
