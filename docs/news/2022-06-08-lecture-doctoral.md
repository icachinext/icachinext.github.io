## 【讲座嘉宾】

周雪

Dr. Michelle Zhou

周雪(Michelle Zhou)博士是美国人工智能初创企业“聚集”（Juji）的创始人和首席执行官。聚集旨在以无代码的方式支持大众化打造和运作认知人工智能助手。在创立聚集之前，周博士曾在 IBM 研究院和 IBM 沃森业务集团工作了15年，领导团队从事以人为中心的人工智能相关技术和解决方案的研究与开发。迄今为止，周博士已经发表了100余篇论文，申请专利45余项，以及十多个被广泛应用的产品和服务,包括 IBM 沃森的 Personality Insights 产品，所有这些都是在以人为中心的人工智能，一个融合人工智能和人机交互的交叉领域。目前，周博士担任国际期刊 Transactions on Interactive Intelligent Systems (TiiS) 的主编。她在哥伦比亚大学获得计算机科学博士学位，也是ACM杰出科学家。

Dr. Michelle Zhou is a co-founder and CEO of Juji, Inc., the maker of the world’s only accessible, cognitive artificial intelligence (AI) assistants, which enable businesses to automate human-interaction tasks empathetically and responsibly with no code. Prior to starting Juji, Michelle led and managed the research and development of cutting-edge, interactive intelligent technologies and solutions at IBM Research/Watson, including IBM RealHunter and Watson Personality Insights. Michelle's work has resulted in a dozen widely used products or solutions, over 100 scientific publications, and 45 issued/filed patents, all in the interdisciplinary field of Human-Centered AI that intersects of AI and Human-Computer Interaction. Currently, Michelle also serves as the Editor-in-Chief for ACM Transactions on Interactive Intelligent Systems (TiiS). She received a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Columbia University and is an ACM Distinguished Scientist.

## 【讲座简介】

人工智能（AI）旨在通过将众多人类不擅长或不喜欢做的任务自动化，来增强或拓展人类的稀缺资源（例如时间以及精力）。随着人工智能技术和其应用的迅速发展，人工智能不可避免地会成为我们个人和职业生活的重要组成部分。然而，创造人工智能并非易事，需要人工智能的专业知识、精密的软件工程设计，更需要大量的计算资源。虽然个人和组织希望获得AI的协助，但是并非每个个人或组织都拥有创建他独有AI系统的能力或资金。在本次演讲中，周雪博士将以开发认知AI助手为例，介绍无代码、可重复使用的AI的挑战和机遇。她将具体分享AI在现实世界的挑战，强调目前前沿的技术进步，并解释不同领域的人们在人工智能民主化的道路上可以发挥的作用。

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is meant to augment humankind and scale out scarce resources (e.g., human time and effort) by automating many tasks that humans are not good at doing or do not like to do. With the rapid advances of AI technologies and their applications, it is inevitable AI would become a big part of our personal and professional lives. However, creation of AI is non-trivial as it requires AI expertise, sophisticated software engineering, not to mention extensive computational resources. While individuals and organizations wish to enlist AI’s help, not every individual or organization has the required skills or financial resources to create their own AI solutions. In this talk, Michelle will use the development of Cognitive AI Assistants as an example to present the challenges and opportunities of enabling no-code, reusable AI. Specifically, she will share real-world challenges, highlight the state-of-the-art advances, and explain the roles that people in different fields could play on the road to democratizing AI.
