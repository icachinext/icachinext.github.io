斯坦福大学中国史教授、古根海姆研究员、美国国会图书馆技术与社会克鲁格主席。他是六本书的作者及主编，包括《中文打字机》、《你的电脑着火了》和即将出版的《中文计算机》——第一部中文计算综合史。

## 【讲座简介】

中文打字机，可能看起来像是昙花一现、微不足道的技术奇观，却是中国计算机“真实”故事的缩影。事实上，这些机器构成了中国当代数字计算和新媒体成就的基础，为当今中国 IT 巨头奠定了基础。我们通常会将许多东西与中文计算相关联——预测文本、文本语料库、自适应数据库，甚至数字图形——都是在机械中文打字领域首次开创的。最重要的是，中文打字机——许多人认为不可能制造的机器——证明了中国的现代化并不像鲁迅和其他人所假设的那样，依赖于旧有汉字的废除。正如一位早期工程师所说，汉字无罪。

Chinese Typewriters might seem like short-lived, insignificant, technological curiosities--a side-show to the "real" story of Chinese computing. In fact, these machines form the basis of China's contemporary achievements in digital computing and new media, and laid the foundation for today's Chinese IT juggernaut. So many things we typically associate with Chinese computing--predictive text, text corpora, adaptive databases, and even digital graphics--were first pioneered in the domain of mechanical Chinese typewriting. Above all, Chinese typewriters--machines that many assumed would be impossible to build--served as proof that Chinese modernization did not, as Lu Xun and others assume, depend upon the abolition of character-based Chinese script. That "Chinese characters are innocent" (汉字无罪), as one early engineer phrased it.

## 【对谈嘉宾】

### 翟树民

## Shumin Zhai

翟树民博士现任谷歌首席科学家 (Principal Scientist)。1996至2010 在 IBM 阿尔莫丹研究中心任研究员 (Research Staff Member)。2001至2002年在瑞典皇家工学院、瑞典Linkoping大学，法国巴黎11大学做访问教授。2004年任清华大学计算机系客座讲席教授。2005 年担任 ACM人机交互大会（ACM CHI）论文委员会联席主席。2009至2015任ACM人机交互学报 （ACM Transactions on Computer-Human Interaction） 主编。学术论文曾获得 ACM UIST 持久影响奖和 IEEE 计算机学会最佳论文奖。2006年入选ACM首届杰出科学家（ACM Distinguished Scientist)。2010 年入选人机交互学院院士 （Member of the CHI Academy）和 ACM Fellow。2020 入选多伦多大学工程学院荣誉堂 （Engineering Hall of Distinction)。目前（2021年）学术指标：H-Index 76, I10-Index 186, Citations 18658; 美国专利 103 项。

Shumin Zhai is a human-computer interaction research scientist at Google where he leads and directs research, design, and development of manual and text input methods and haptics systems. His research career has contributed to both fundamental models and understandings of human-computer interaction and practical user interface designs, inventions and mass-products. He originated and led the SHARK/ShapeWriter project and a start-up company that pioneered the touchscreen swiping-gesture keyboard paradigm, filing the first patents of this paradigm, publishing the first generation of scientific papers and dissertations (by his former Ph.D. student Per Ola Kristensson) of the field, releasing the first word-gesture keyboard in 2004 through IBM Alphaworks, and a top ranked (6th) iPhone app called ShapeWriter WritingPad in 2008. His publications have won the ACM UIST Lasting Impact Award and a IEEE Computer Society Best Paper Award, among others. He served as the 4th Editor-in-Chief of ACM Transactions on Computer-Human Interaction, and frequently contributes to other academic boards and program committees. He received his Ph.D. degree at the University of Toronto in 1995. In 2006, he was named one of ACM's inaugural class of Distinguished Scientists. In 2010 he was named Member of the CHI Academy and Fellow of the ACM.

## 【对谈嘉宾】

### 李维

## Wei Li

李维博士是华为人机交互实验室首席科学家，世界华人人机交互协会（ICACHI）名誉会长。加入华为前，曾在Autodesk Research UI Group 和Computational Science Group 担任资深科学家。李维在多模态交互、移动计算、推荐系统、约束推理、计算视觉、计算生成式设计等领域有 40 余件发明专利；曾获得 AAAI/IAAI, ACM MobileHCI, IDETC / CIE 等多个最佳研究论文奖和提名。

华为人机交互实验室是华为公司研究机构，担负在人机交互领域科研探索的责任。实验室汇集了跨学科领域的研究员、工程师和设计师，致力于交互系统创新、人因、感知反馈技术、智能座舱等方向的研究创新。

Dr. Wei Li is the Chief Scientist of Huawei Human-machine Interaction Laboratory and the Honorary President of the International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI). Before joining Huawei, he was a principal scientist in Autodesk Research UI Group and Computational Science Group. He received his PhD in Computer Science from the University of Waterloo, Canada, and his BS in Computer Science from the University of Science and Technology of China. Wei Li has over 40 patents in the fields of multimodal interaction, mobile computing, recommender system, constraint reasoning, computer vision, computational generative design, etc.; His research work in software learning, multimodal interaction, and generative design have won AAAI/IAAI, ACM MobileHCI, and IDETC/CIE Best Research Paper awards and nominations.

Huawei Human-machine Interaction Lab focuses on developing cutting-edge technology and exploring future product directions.
