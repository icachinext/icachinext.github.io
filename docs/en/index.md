## Introduction

The International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI) is a non-profit academic organization aimed at promoting communication and cooperation among Chinese HCI scholars, researchers, and professionals globally.

We are committed to:
- Promoting research and education in the HCI field
- Organizing high-level academic conferences and workshops
- Providing career development and networking opportunities for members
