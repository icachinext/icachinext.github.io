## Members of the Sixth ICACHI Board of Directors

The election of the sixth ICACHI Board of Directors has concluded successfully. Thank you to all members for your strong support. Your willingness to contribute is a key force behind the association's development. We also thank the fifth Board for its important contributions to the advancement of Chinese human–computer interaction research worldwide.

The new Board comprises the following 30 members.
