Inclusion . Resilience . New Normal

Dear Participants,

Given Guangzhou's current pandemic situation, the committee has decided that **the 2022 International human–computer interaction Conference (CHINESE CHI 2022) will be held online on November 5–6, 2022. Design workshops will retain a hybrid format on November 4, with the principal physical venue at the Dongfeng Road campus of Guangdong University of Technology in Yuexiu District, Guangzhou.**

**We will process registration fee differences collectively after the conference.** Participants do not need to take any action.

Further information will be announced on the conference website:

**[http://chchi2022.icachi.org](http://chchi2022.icachi.org)**

and the WeChat official account.

Chinese CHI 2022 Secretariat
