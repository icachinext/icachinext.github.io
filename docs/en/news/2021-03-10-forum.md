## · IWHEC ·

The Chinese-language online forum Humanity, Technology, and the Future: Reflections on the Post-AI Era **will take place on March 21, 2021, at 16:45 Beijing Time (17:45 Tokyo, 08:45 London, 04:45 US Eastern, and 01:45 US Pacific).**

The forum is jointly initiated by the Young Scholars Committee of International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI) and the Innovation Promotion Committee of the Chinese Professors Association in Japan. Considering humanity and technology over the longer term, it opens discussion of the post-AI era. **It will be held on Zoom and livestreamed worldwide, open to all audiences.**

## For speakers and the detailed program, see:

**[http://iw2021.xrenlab.com/day3.html](http://iw2021.xrenlab.com/day3.html)**

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-03-10-forum-01.png)

**Humanity created technology, but technology also enables people and subtly reshapes thought.** As technology transforms modern life, pervasive data and information blur reality and illusion and test spiritual and individual boundaries. The pandemic's disruption of social structures and emotions reveals humanity's vulnerability where technology falls short. We enjoy technology's benefits but must accept its costs. At this unusual moment, when good and bad need redefining and past innovations and values require reassessment, we ask early- and mid-career frontier researchers: beyond better living and efficiency, what does technology mean, and where is it going? How can we establish universal values in a rapidly changing world, and what technologies do we need? We ask students whether they have the ability and courage to offer new interpretations when established experience is challenged. These questions converge on one concern: **what future relationship should we have with technology, and where should humanity's future lead?**

**The forum has three parts. The first two feature discussion: seven senior university and industry researchers will offer broad perspectives on current social and technological issues and future directions, while fourteen doctoral students present frontier research. Both groups will then discuss the post-AI era. The third part brings concluding discussion.**

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-03-10-forum-02.png)

We hope the forum opens dialogue between mid-career scholars and young people, generating new insights and finding needs and meaning for future technologies. As inheritors of Chinese culture, thought, and awareness, can we offer Eastern perspectives beyond established industrial and information-era paradigms?

Everyone is welcome online. **Register through Zoom and follow the ICACHI WeChat account for conference updates.**
