## Call for Papers

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), **Chinese CHI 2024 will take place from November 22 to 25, 2024, at Southern University of Science and Technology in Shenzhen, China.** The conference themes are **Innovation**, **Creativity**, and **Integration**.

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), this academic conference has become an important meeting point connecting Chinese HCI researchers, academia, and industry worldwide. Since 2012, Chinese CHI has been held successfully in Paris, Toronto, Seoul, Silicon Valley, Guangzhou, Montreal, Xiamen, and Bali, Indonesia. Conference papers are included in the ACM Digital Library and submitted for EI indexing.

In an era of emerging AI, the conference will explore how technological innovation can transform interaction, stimulate creativity, shape more intelligent and human-centered interactive systems, and integrate multidisciplinary research to address complex real-world applications. We warmly invite scholars, researchers, and industry leaders worldwide to share their latest findings and practical experience, exchange ideas, and explore diverse ways technology can empower humanity's future.

## Topics

Topics Include, but Are Not Limited To:

- User Experience and Usability
- Health, Accessibility and Aging
- Games and Play
- Generative AI and HCI
- Human Robot Interaction
- Affective Computing & Intelligent Interaction
- Visualization
- Design
- Privacy, Security, Ethics and Critical Computing
- Interaction Techniques, Devices and Modalities
- Understanding People: Theory, Concepts, Methods
- Engineering Interactive Systems and Technologies
- Specific Application Areas

The conference also welcomes human–computer interaction research in Chinese cultural contexts. Topics include, but are not limited to:

- Chinese Traditional Culture Oriented HCI
- Chinese Cultural Heritage Oriented HCI
- Technology Use or Development in the Chinese Context
- Understanding Chinese Users
- Community Engagement in China

## Submission Information

Submissions are accepted from the publication of this call. The submission deadline for full papers (Papers) is:

- **Abstract:**
- **August 31, 2024, 23:59 AOE**
- **Full paper:**
- **September 20, 2024, 23:59 AOE**

### (II) Submission Requirements

1. Only submissions in English are accepted.

2. All authors must remain anonymous, and the manuscript must not contain information identifying them.

3. Submissions must use the ACM Conference Proceedings Primary Article Template:

- acm_submission_template.docx
- Download the LaTeX template:
- [https://portalparts.acm.org/hippo/](https://portalparts.acm.org/hippo/)
- latex_templates/acmart-primary.zip
- Download the Overleaf template:
- [https://www.overleaf.com/latex/](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/)
- templates/acm-conference-proceedings-primary-article-template/wbvnghjbzwpc

4. Full papers must not exceed 20 pages, excluding references.

5. Papers submitted to Chinese CHI 2024 must be unpublished and must not simultaneously be under review at another conference or journal.

6. Authors must observe academic integrity. The committee will uphold rigorous quality standards and investigate misconduct. Authors bear responsibility for violations, and the association reserves the right to hold them accountable.

## Call for Papers

The International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI) is delighted to announce the upcoming Chinese CHI 2024 conference **will occur in the Southern University of Science and Technology, Shenzhen, China, from November 22th to 25th, 2024.** The conference theme for this year is **"Innovation. Creativity. Integration."**

Chinese CHI is the premier venue for connecting Chinese HCI researchers, practitioners, and industry leaders worldwide. Since its inception in 2012, Chinese CHI has successfully been held in Paris, Toronto, Seoul, Silicon Valley, Guangzhou, Montreal, Xiamen, and Bali. Accepted papers at the conference are published annually in ACM Digital Library and EI Compendex.

In the era of emerging technologies, Chinese CHI 2024 will focus on driving transformations in interaction paradigms through technological innovation, stimulating creativity to shape more intelligent and human-centered interactive systems, and integrating multidisciplinary research outcomes to address complex real-world application scenarios. We cordially invite leading scholars, researchers, and industry leaders from around the globe to share their latest findings and practical experiences, to foster intellectual exchange and collaborative dialogue, and to explore diverse pathways through which technology can empower the future of humanity.

We invite contributions to Chinese CHI 2024 across a variety of research domains, including but not limited to:

- User Experience and Usability

On top of that, we particularly welcome submissions in the Chinese cultural context, with example topics including but not limited to:

## Submission Guidelines

All submissions for Chinese CHI are to be:

- Written in English.
- Original and not simultaneously submitted to another journal or conference.
- Anonymized and without any information identifying the authors in the main text.

**Formatting:** All submissions for Chinese CHI are to be written in English. Instructions for Word, LaTex, and Overleap authors are given below.

**Length:** The following length limit is imposed for papers: 20 pages max, excluding references.

**Review Process:** Submissions are subject to blinded peer reviewing. Authors submitting papers and late-break works must comply with the Chinese CHI reviewing policy including, but not limited to:

- To ensure the integrity of the peer review process, we ask that no authors publicize the work until that process is complete.
- The work submitted is not currently under review at any other publication venue.
- The submission is original and is an honest representation of the underlying work.

**Registration:** Upon acceptance, at least one author of each accepted paper must be registered to attend the conference.
