The **Human-Engaged ComputingOnline International Workshop** (International Workshop on Human-Engaged Computing 2021, IWHEC 2021) **will take place on March 19–20, 2021.** It is initiated by the Human-Engaged Computing institute at Japan's Kochi University of Technology (Center for Human-Engaged Computing, CHEC) and International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), with support from the Engineering Academy of Japan, HCI Korea, ACM SIGCHI China Chapter, and ACM SIGCHI Japan Chapter. By rethinking human–computer relations, it seeks inspiration for new goals, methods, and technologies for future human–computer interaction. **The workshop will take place on Zoom and be livestreamed worldwide, open to all audiences.**

For speakers and the program, see [http://iw2021.xrenlab.com/index.html](http://iw2021.xrenlab.com/index.html)

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-03-12-conference-02.png)

Human-Engaged Computing was introduced in Rethinking the Relationship between Humans and Computers (Ren, 2016), published in IEEE Computer, and Human-Engaged Computing: The Future of Human–Computer Interaction (Ren, Silpasuwanchai, and Cahill, 2019), published in CCF Transactions on Pervasive Computing and Interaction. Human-Engaged Computing argues that technology should begin by developing human mental capabilities and potential, enabling humans and computers to improve together through organic synergy guided by balanced moderation. Its framework contains four concepts: Engaged Humans, Engaging Computers, Synergized Interaction, and Antibiosis.

**For a fuller explanation of Human-Engaged Computing, see [https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/5Fh68A76rLkAwFPSnqdlhA](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/5Fh68A76rLkAwFPSnqdlhA)**

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-03-12-conference-03.png)

Exploring future technological paradigms, the IWHEC series has held three editions: IWHEC 2017 (http://forum.chec.ren), IWHEC 2019 (http://iw2019.xrenlab.com), and IWHEC 2020 (http://iw2020.xrenlab.com). It brings together active scholars from different cultures and backgrounds to examine higher-level technological ideas and directions and encourage academia and industry to rethink human–computer relations. This edition again invites 12 outstanding speakers worldwide, spanning human–computer interaction, neuroscience, computer science, AI, and design. As COVID-19 profoundly affects everyone, we hope online exchange can share their future perspectives and renew understanding of technological development. The workshop also seeks to build a research community for exchanging ideas and exploring better technological paths centered on human well-being.

**Everyone is welcome online. Please register through Zoom and follow ICACHI's WeChat account for updates.**

**For the Chinese-language forum on the third day, see [http://iw2021.xrenlab.com/day3.html](http://iw2021.xrenlab.com/day3.html)**
