Elections for the third ICACHI Board of Directors are approaching. We sincerely thank the second board for its important contributions to human–computer interaction research and look forward to your active participation in nominating and electing the next board.

Important dates and requirements are listed below.

March 31: nomination website announced

April 6: deadline for nominations and self-nominations

April 11: deadline for statements of willingness to serve, for candidates nominated by others

April 12: candidate list announced

April 19: open question-and-answer period ends

April 20: voting website announced

April 25: voting closes

April 30: new board announced

Please note that only full members who have paid their 2016 dues may nominate candidates or vote. Anyone wishing to nominate, stand for election, or vote must register and pay before 5 p.m. Eastern Time on March 31. The nomination website will be sent only to paid members that day. ICACHI's dues payment system is available at [https://www.regonline.com/icachimembership](https://www.regonline.com/icachimembership). Members registered for Chinese CHI 2016 need not pay again because their registration already includes 2016-2017 membership dues.

ICACHI 2016 Board Election Committee:

Chair: Yu Chen, University of California, Irvine

Members: Lu Liu (Shanghai Jiao Tong University), Zuoyun Yang (University of Science and Technology Beijing), and Yubo Kou (University of California, Irvine)

## Their Oath of Office

As members of the International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction 2016 Board Election Committee, we solemnly pledge to perform our duties faithfully, keep internal election materials and candidate information strictly confidential, and do everything possible to uphold a fair and impartial election.

For questions, email the election committee: committee-for-election@icachi.org
