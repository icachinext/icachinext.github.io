## 01 | Background

International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), the first global professional scientific association of overseas Chinese scholars, has long contributed to the future of human–computer interaction in China. Its 2019 Blue Book on human–computer interaction Development, China's first such publication on human–computer interaction, received an enthusiastic response from academic and industry readers. As members' academic achievements accumulate, preparation of the 2022 Blue Book on human–computer interaction Education Development is about to begin.

## 02 | Purpose and Responsibilities

To produce the blue book efficiently and to a high standard, we seek an organizing team passionate about human–computer interaction research and development to bring the work to fruition. Applications are now open for the 2022 Blue Book on human–computer interaction Education Development working committee. Join us!

Committee Members Will:

- Organize and coordinate preparation of the blue book
- Collect and manage source materials
- Publicize and promote the project

## 03 | Applicants

**Master's students, doctoral students, young scholars, and professionals** in human–computer interaction and related fields.

## 04 | Number of Positions

5–8 people

## 05 | Requirements

**Communication and collaboration:** strong teamwork and communication skills

**Time availability:** sufficient discretionary time

**Execution:** ability to attend online team meetings punctually and complete assigned tasks

## 06 | Organizer

International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction Young Scholars Committee

International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction

## 07 | Application Method

**Email your CV to xiaq.5.0@gmail.com with the subject "Blue Book Working Committee Application + Name."**

You may optionally include a personal statement describing relevant abilities.

## 08 | Application Deadline

After the application deadline, shortlisted candidates will be contacted by email. Applicants not passing the CV screening will not receive separate notifications.

We look forward to welcoming you!
