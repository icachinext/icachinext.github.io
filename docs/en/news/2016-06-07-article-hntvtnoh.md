## Background

I am a fourth-year computer science and technology student at Jilin University's Zhuhai College. I was fortunate to be admitted to Kochi University of Technology as a Special Research Student, studying in Professor Ren's CHEC laboratory from November 1, 2015, to May 31, 2016.

Time seems like a thief, stealing joyful moments and leaving sweet memories in the heart. Every plant and tree here carries a special memory, recording the days that passed so quickly.

## Feelings

My seven-month exchange at Kochi University of Technology in Japan has ended. The campus is beautiful and refreshing, and walking to the laboratory each morning was a pleasure. I saw falling snow, blooming cherry blossoms, and heard bullfrogs calling. Initially, curiosity and nervous excitement marked the beginning of this adventure. Professor Ren greeted me warmly, shaking my hand and chatting. He cared for exchange students and often invited us home for meals. Chinese senior students supported me in both study and everyday life. The laboratory was an international family of postdoctoral researchers, doctoral and master's students, and undergraduates. As a junior undergraduate, I received everyone's care. We spoke English and combined serious research with small activities that strengthened friendships, creating a responsible yet relaxed and enthusiastic academic atmosphere.

## Learning

I joined the programming group, which provided technical support for laboratory projects. My background was game programming, and the human–computer interaction laboratory had suitable game-interaction work. In one week, I learned the basics of WFP and Kinect, then quickly developed motion-controlled games through practical projects, which became my main activity over seven months. I also learned to program the Vicon motion-capture system in C# and conduct VR experiments using Unity and Oculus Rift. My programming skills improved substantially.

## Daily Life

Three of us cooked in the residence hall's shared kitchen, improving our cooking skills and meeting international students for greetings and conversation. On weekends, we explored the city center and relaxed. We also played table tennis, badminton, and shuttlecock and used fitness equipment. Such simple routines made life enjoyable.

Leaving brings many regrets and more gratitude than a few words can express. I can only say once more: keep going, everyone!
