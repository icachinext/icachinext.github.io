International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI) invites bids to host the fifth Chinese CHI academic conference. ICACHI is an international, multidisciplinary academic association whose members conduct research in computing, design, behavioral science, and engineering.

Since 2012, Chinese CHI has been held in major cities including Toronto and Paris, supported by leading companies such as Google, Autodesk, Huawei, and HTC. English is its official language, and accepted papers are included in the ACM Digital Library. The most recent edition, Chinese CHI 2015, took place in Seoul. Details: [http://ICACHI.org](http://ICACHI.org).

Bids will be reviewed by the association's executive committee and board. The final decision will be discussed at Chinese CHI 2016 in San Jose, USA.

Interested host cities are warmly invited to submit concise proposals containing the following information:

1. Letter of Intent
1. Hosting Team and Personnel
1. Proposed Conference Chairs
1. Conference Dates
1. Proposals for an Innovative Program
1. Description of the Location
1. a. Local weather and travel routes
1. b. Accommodation
1. Description of the Venue
1. a. Poster, demonstration, and reception spaces
1. b. Security arrangements
1. Sponsorship and Industry Participation
1. Draft Budget

This call is open to HCI communities worldwide. Send proposals to the ICACHI Secretariat at mailto:office@icachi.org by **May 1, 2016**. Electronic confirmation of receipt will be sent within 24 hours.
