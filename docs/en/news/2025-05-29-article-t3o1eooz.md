**International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction** (International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction, ICACHI) was founded in Austin, USA, on May 10, 2012, during ACM CHI 2012. It is the first purely professional scientific organization founded by Chinese scholars across geographical and regional boundaries worldwide.

The association supports members' development and standing in their countries and worldwide, contributing to humanity through human–computer interaction research.
