## Conference Overview

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), the **International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing** (ICHEC 2025, formerly Chinese CHI) will take place **November 21–23, 2025** at **Singapore Management University** in Singapore. The themes are **Inclusion**, **Intelligence**, and **Connection**.

The ICHEC 2025 **Interactivity & Artwork** track invites interactive demonstrations, system and interface prototypes, innovative artifacts, artistic concepts, and other experiences that critically or creatively engage with the conference theme, Human-Engaged Computing. **We particularly encourage submissions enabling hands-on audience participation and interaction.**

## Scope and Formats

We welcome, but are not limited to, the following formats:

- System and interface prototypes
- Innovative interactive installations and artifacts
- Artistic concepts and multimedia works
- Other creative interactive experiences

## Submission Guidelines

Submit through the following portal:

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2025-08-24-ichec2025-art-01.png)

**[https://easychair.org/account2/signin?l=4690469135012500472](https://easychair.org/account2/signin?l=4690469135012500472)**

Each submission must include:

- An extended abstract PDF, single-column, up to six pages, following the ACM Primary Article Templates
- A technical specifications and requirements PDF, without a page limit; illustrations may be included
- At least one static image of 800 × 600 pixels
- A video of no more than two minutes or a demonstration link

**Submissions are non-anonymous** and reviewed by a panel of track chairs. **At least one author must register and demonstrate the work in person during the exhibition.**

## Important Dates

- **10.20** — Submission deadline

- **11.01** — Notification

- **11.15** — Final version due, optionally for inclusion in the adjunct proceedings

- **11.21** — Interactive exhibition

All deadlines are **23:59 AOE**.

**Conference: November 21–23, 2025**

## Assessment Criteria

- Alignment with the **Human-Engaged Computing** theme and academic or artistic quality
- Interactivity: creative or critical participant engagement
- Feasibility: safe, convenient deployment at the conference venue

## Conference Overview

The International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI) is delighted to announce that the **2025 International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing** （ICHEC 2025) will take place in **Singapore Management University,** Singapore, from **November 21st to 23th, 2025.** The conference theme for this year is **"Inclusion. Intelligence. Connection".**

**The Interactivity & Artwork track of ICHEC 2025** invites interactive demos, system/interface prototypes, innovative artifacts, artistic visions, and other creative experiences that critically or creatively reflect the conference theme “Human-Engaged Computing”. **We especially encourage submissions that actively engage conference participants.**
