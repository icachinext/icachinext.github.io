## Call for Student Competition Entries

Chinese CHI 2022, organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), **will be held in Guangzhou, China, on October 22–23, 2022, in a hybrid online and in-person format.** The theme is **Inclusion, Resilience, and New Normal.**

We invite students from diverse design backgrounds, including human–computer interaction, industrial, product, visual, user experience, and interaction design, to enter this student competition on-site or online.

The competition showcases students' ideas and skills in human–computer interaction design and user experience, providing opportunities to raise their work's visibility and receive expert feedback.

Submissions will be assessed by a committee of experienced experts in relevant fields, to be announced later, and through an online audience vote. The final award recipients will be invited to exhibit their award-winning work at the Digital Art Exhibition and receive an ICACHI competition award certificate.

## Important Dates

- Proposal deadline: August 30, 2022, 23:59
- Review notification: September 30, 2022, 23:59

## Submission Guidelines

Submission Guidelines

1. **Poster:** Prepare a **standard letter-sized poster as a PDF no larger than 10 MB. It should include:**
1. - Proposed solution title, team and author names, and institution
1. - Your perspective on the design challenge
1. - A brief description of the solution
1. - A clear presentation of the proposed solution's key information
1. **Video:** Teams should provide a video **of up to five minutes**, no larger than 100 MB. **Explain how your proposal addresses a design challenge in an interaction scenario. You may also illustrate interface details and related information.**
1. **Supplementary video content includes:**
1. - Important background data and examples of its analysis
1. - Major creative inspirations, including existing designs and systems
1. - Sketches of the solution
1. - Scenarios explaining how the solution addresses the design challenge
1. - Relevant interaction and design information
1. **Proof of student status:** Submit **a statement signed by your academic supervisor verifying all of the following**:
1. - Your institution
1. - Whether you were an undergraduate or graduate student when the work was completed
1. - Proof of student status for every student
1. Compress the materials above into a **.ZIP file and send it to the following email address:**
1. **competition_chichi@163.com**
1. **Note:** In your submission email, state whether you wish to participate on-site or online only.

## Competition Assessment Criteria

**Ensure that submissions contain no infringing or confidential materials and do not cite infringing or confidential publications.**

Poster and video submissions **will be reviewed according to:**

- A brief explanation of how the design addresses this year's conference theme
- The clarity and credibility of the design focus, objectives, and solution in relation to the challenge
- The originality and quality of the design solution, including its claims and supporting literature
- Innovation, artistry, and human-centered considerations in the design process

## Call for Student Competition

**We invite practitioners, researchers, and students in art and design-related fields to submit proposals for on-site or online exhibitions at the Chinese CHI 2022 Art Gallery track.** The theme of this year's hybrid Art Gallery is Inclusion, Resilience, and New Normal.

**We invite students from a variety of design backgrounds** (HCI, industrial design, product design, visual design, user experience and interaction design, etc.) **to participate in this student competition (onsite or online).**

**This track aims to demonstrate their ideas and skills in HCI Design and User Experience, and provides a chance to expand the visibility of your design and get feedback from experts.**

**The submissions will be juried by a committee** of experienced experts in relevant domains (to be announced later) and vote online from audience. The final winners will get invited to demonstrate their work in the Art Gallery track, and receive a Chinese CHI Competition Award Certificate from ICACHI.

## The Competition Selection Criteria

- Short description of how your proposed design fits with this year’s conference theme
- Clarity and credibility of design focus, purpose and solution relative to the posed challenge
- Originality and quality of the design solution, including claims and their supporting evidence
