## Conference Overview

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), the **International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing** (ICHEC 2025, formerly Chinese CHI) will take place **November 21–23, 2025** at **Singapore Management University** in Singapore. The themes are **Inclusion**, **Intelligence**, and **Connection**.

We warmly invite innovative workshop proposals. **Workshops will take place on Friday, November 21, 2025, in Singapore, the opening day of ICHEC 2025.** They offer an excellent platform for exploring frontier human–computer interaction topics and building meaningful connections across the global HCI community.

## Scope and Topics

We encourage proposals addressing contemporary and emerging HCI areas, **including but not limited to:**

- Emerging interaction paradigms and user experience design
- Human–AI collaboration, fairness, agency, and explainability
- User experience, social computing, digital well-being, and ethics
- AR/VR/XR, ubiquitous computing, and embodied interaction
- Sustainability, accessibility, and inclusive HCI
- New HCI research and evaluation methods
- Application areas: health, education, civic technology, and workplace technology

- A dedicated half-day (3.5-hour) or full-day (2 × 3.5-hour) slot
- Standard meeting facilities, including a projector, microphones, and Wi-Fi
- A conference-promoted workshop webpage and inclusion in the proceedings

- Networking opportunities with international participants

## Participation Requirements

- Workshop organizers and participants must register for both the workshop and main conference
- No additional workshop fee; participation is included in the conference registration fee
- At least ten on-site participants are required
- Preference will be given to workshops with strong international appeal

## Conference Overview

The International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI) is delighted to announce that the **2025 International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing** （ICHEC 2025) will take place in **Singapore Management University,** Singapore, from **November 21st to 23th, 2025.** The conference theme for this year is **"Inclusion. Intelligence. Connection".**

We warmly invite submissions for innovative **workshops that will take place on Friday, November 21, 2025, the opening day of ICHEC 2025 in Singapore.** These workshops provide an excellent platform for exploring cutting-edge topics in Human-Computer Interaction and fostering meaningful connections within our global HCI community.

## Scope & Topics

We encourage proposals that address contemporary and emerging areas within HCI, **including but not limited to:**

- Emerging Interaction Paradigms & UX Design
- Human-AI Collaboration, Fairness, Agency & Interpretability
- UX and Social Computing, Digital Wellbeing & Ethics
- AR/VR/XR, Ubiquitous Computing & Embodied Interaction
- HCI for Sustainability, Accessibility & Inclusion
- Novel Methodologies in HCI Research & Evaluation
- Application Domains: Health, Education, Civic Tech, Workplace Tech
