## ICACHI Young Scholars Committee

## Yuzhi Wan

Yuzhi Wan earned a bachelor's degree from Tsinghua University's Department of Automotive Engineering, now the School of Vehicle and Mobility, in 2014 and a PhD in industrial and operations engineering from the University of Michigan in 2019. Previously a user researcher at Google AR, he now conducts user research and data analysis at Tencent.

## THInC at the University of Michigan

**The Human-automation Interaction and Cognition Lab (THInC), a human–computer interaction and cognition laboratory, is part of the University of Michigan's Center for Ergonomics.** It is led by Professor Nadine Sarter, a member of the US National Academy of Engineering and director of the center. **The center studies cognitive ergonomics, biomechanics, work physiology and safety,** and related areas.

THInC studies highly complex, event-driven systems in aviation, defense, healthcare, automotive, and other sectors. Main topics include:

- **Designing and evaluating multimodal interfaces combining vision, hearing, and touch to support human–computer interaction and computer-assisted teamwork;**
- **Supporting attention and interruption management through adaptive alerts and preattentive reference;**
- **Designing decision aids for trust calibration and dynamic function allocation;**
- **Human error and error/disturbance management**
