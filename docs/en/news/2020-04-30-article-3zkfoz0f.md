## ICACHI's Recommendations to China Association for Science and Technology

## 2012.08

In August 2012, **ICACHI signed a cooperation agreement with the Hefei base of China Association for Science and Technology's Overseas Expertise Program.** Supporting national strategies for development through science, education, and talent, the agreement established regular, close, accessible communication with China Association for Science and Technology and an effective working mechanism to mobilize overseas scientists and engineers. It aimed to contribute expertise to scientific innovation, economic and social development, and China's rejuvenation.

## 2018.07

In July 2018, ICACHI lifetime honorary president Xiangshi Ren and vice president Zhiyong Fu **visited the Overseas Expertise Office of the international liaison department on the 17th floor of Building B, China Hall of Science and Technology.** They met department director Jiansheng Zhang, office director Aijun Qiu, deputy director Heqing Yan, and secretary-general Xin Ma.

ICACHI meets with China Association for Science and Technology (image source: ICACHI)

During the meeting, Xiangshi Ren shared his understanding of human–computer interaction, reflections on frontier ideas, and plans for ICACHI's future platform. Director Jiansheng Zhang proposed establishing a human–computer interaction research institute in China. **The institute would have three functions: frontier research, talent development, and research commercialization.**

## Frontier Research

Xiangshi Ren suggested that human–computer interaction would become the next major area after AI and introduced the theory of Human-Engaged Computing (HEC). These frontier ideas could form the institute's theoretical and academic foundation. It could also publish research reports, consistent with the association's earlier plans to develop a think tank.

## Talent Development

The institute could organize student exchanges, such as overseas internships for young students and researchers, using board members' and members' laboratories as an internship network with some financial support. It could also organize technology competitions through China Association for Science and Technology and use international resources to hold workshops in China.

## Research Commercialization

The institute could provide a platform to bring overseas research into application in China. It could also help students form teams and turn human–computer interaction ideas into start-ups supported by innovation funding.

The institute could be established locally in cooperation with municipal governments and universities, seeking local government funding. It could contain several laboratories, with ICACHI directors or members responsible for different research directions. **The institute would be a permanent organization with a dedicated management team.**

## 2020.03

The unexpected outbreak of COVID-19 in early 2020 posed considerable challenges to science and technology in China and worldwide. **ICACHI therefore submitted Recommendations on Scientific and Technological Development After the Pandemic to China Association for Science and Technology,** advocating stronger applied human–computer interaction research for epidemic prevention and disaster mitigation. It sought mechanisms connecting overseas and domestic research and industry in human–computer interaction to support these efforts in China. ICACHI supports China's pandemic response and will continue contributing to China Association for Science and Technology's Overseas Expertise Program and offering constructive recommendations in the field.
