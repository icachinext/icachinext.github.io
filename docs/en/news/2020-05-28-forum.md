## Chinese CHI 2020

The eighth International Chinese human–computer interaction Conference, Chinese CHI, will be held online on May 30, 2020. Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), it has become an important link among Chinese HCI scholars, academia, and industry worldwide. Since 2012, seven editions have been successfully held in Paris, Toronto, Seoul, Silicon Valley, Guangzhou, Montreal, and Xiamen.

## Keynote Speakers

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2020-05-28-forum-02.jpg)

**Fifty Years of Reflection: What Exactly Is human–computer interaction?**

Guozhong Dai, born in September 1944 in Wuxi, Jiangsu, is a Han Chinese researcher and doctoral supervisor at Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences and chief scientist of its human–computer interaction Technology and Intelligent Information Processing Laboratory. He has led and participated in National Natural Science Foundation, National High-Tech R&D, and National Basic Research projects. His interests include human–computer interaction, computer graphics, and virtual reality, including pen-based interaction, natural interfaces, and 3D interfaces. He has published more than 100 influential conference and journal papers and two monographs. He has received four National Science and Technology Progress Awards, multiple academy and ministerial awards, and the Chinese Academy of Sciences' 1993 award for outstanding young and middle-aged experts. A pioneer of computer user interfaces in China, he has made major contributions to software and graphics application engineering, the national CAD application initiative, and the 863/CIMS program.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2020-05-28-forum-03.png)

## Walking Through Large Virtual Environments

Ligang Liu is a professor at the University of Science and Technology of China. He received his PhD in applied mathematics from Zhejiang University in 2001 and has worked or visited Microsoft Research Asia, Zhejiang University, and Harvard University. His computer graphics and virtual reality research includes 30 papers in ACM Transactions on Graphics. Awards include the first prize of the Lu Zengyong CAD&CG High-Tech Award in 2010 and a second-class National Natural Science Award in 2013. He received support from Chinese Academy of Sciences's Hundred Talents Program and the National Natural Science Foundation's Excellent Young Scientists program. He co-chaired GMP 2017 and served as paper co-chair for SPM 2014, SGP 2015, CVM 2016, CAD/Graphics 2017, GMP 2018, SIAM GD 2019, Chinagraph 2020, and ChinaVR 2020. He serves on the editorial boards of IEEE TVCG, IEEE CG&A, CGF, CAGD, and C&G. Website: [http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~lgliu](http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~lgliu)
