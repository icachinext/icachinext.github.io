## Conference Overview

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), the **International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing** (ICHEC 2025, formerly Chinese CHI) will take place **November 21–23, 2025** at **Singapore Management University** in Singapore. The themes are **Inclusion**, **Intelligence**, and **Connection**.

## Keynote Speaker

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2025-09-28-ichec2025-keynote-01.png)

Karrie Karahalios is a leading researcher in human–computer interaction (HCI) and computer-supported cooperative work (CSCW), focusing on algorithmic accountability and social computing. She is widely recognized for introducing algorithm auditing, a systematic method for exposing hidden algorithmic behavior, and has received a Sloan Fellowship and multiple CHI/CSCW best paper awards.
