## Pokémon Go Launches

On your way to school, a wild Charmander appears. Throw a few Poké Balls, and it could be yours! Would you stop to catch it? The major AR game Pokémon Go debuted in Australia and New Zealand on July 7.

Worlds are merging. A game uses AR to bring the virtual world into reality, signaling the beginning of large-scale commercial adoption of augmented reality.

## SoftBank Buys ARM to Pursue Its AI Ambitions?

On July 18, Japan's SoftBank acquired ARM for US$32.9 billion. ARM does not manufacture chips itself; it designs processor technology and licenses it to companies such as Qualcomm and NVIDIA. These processors are used primarily in mobile devices, including Apple's.

In 2015, SoftBank and Aldebaran officially launched Pepper, an emotional robot whose core technology responds to emotional signals, representing a breakthrough in AI. SoftBank's Masayoshi Son is said to have long dreamed of artificial intelligence. ARM may make it easier to create a smarter Pepper.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2016-07-19-article-2mjco0un-02.jpg)

## Thomson Reuters Sells SCI; Google Scholar Metrics Released

On July 11, Thomson Reuters sold its intellectual property and science business for US$3.55 billion, including the renowned Science Citation Index (SCI). The Institute for Scientific Information first published the 1961 SCI report in 1964, making SCI an unavoidable term for researchers. Meanwhile, the American Society for Microbiology announced that its journals would no longer promote impact factors.

Google Scholar has released its metrics. Launched in late 2004, the service has gained researchers' approval and growing influence. Its h5-index measures the largest number h5 such that a publication has at least h5 articles from the past five years, each cited at least h5 times.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2016-07-19-article-2mjco0un-03.jpg)

## A New VR Application: Vehicle Testing

A series of Tesla crashes has brought criticism of Autopilot. According to The Economist, the University of Warwick is combining VR and autonomous driving in a new vehicle-testing approach.

A real car sits inside a simulator projecting high-definition, 360-degree surroundings based on digital maps of 48 kilometers of roads around Coventry, UK, including buildings and scenery. The simulator adds controllable traffic lights, cyclists, pedestrians, and even dogs running into the road. Sound and motion systems respond to acceleration, braking, and turning, even reproducing the sound of potholes.

## IxAP 2016 Submission Deadline Approaches

IxAP 2016—Interactive Technology and Ageing Populations—will be held in Kochi, Japan, on October 20, 2016, focusing on coordinated development of human–computer interaction technologies for older adults. Submissions close on the 20th of this month. Researchers and experts from all fields are welcome to submit and attend.
