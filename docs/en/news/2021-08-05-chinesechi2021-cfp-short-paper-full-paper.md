Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), **Chinese CHI 2021's submission period is about to close. We welcome your papers and participation!** Outstanding submissions will be considered for **Best Paper Awards** and **Honorable Mention Awards. Please note the deadlines:**

- **Full Papers Deadline:**
- **July 29, 2021, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth**
- **August 5, 2021, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth**
- **Late-Breaking Work Deadline:**
- **August 10, 2021, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth**
- **August 20, 2021, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth**

**Chinese CHI's theme this year is Identity, Value, and Trust amid technological progress.** Connecting Chinese HCI scholars, academia, and industry worldwide, it has held eight successful editions since 2012 in Paris, Toronto, Seoul, Silicon Valley, Guangzhou, Montreal, and Xiamen. **All accepted Full Papers and Late-Breaking Work papers will be included in the ACM Digital Library and submitted for EI indexing.**

The conference accepts Full Papers and Late-Breaking Work. **Only English submissions are accepted.**

All Submissions Must Use the ACM Master Article Template

Template Download:

**[https://www.acm.org/publications/taps/word-template-workflow](https://www.acm.org/publications/taps/word-template-workflow)**

**Full papers must not exceed 20 pages, and Late-Breaking Work papers eight pages, excluding references.**

All Manuscripts Must Be Submitted Online Through EasyChair

Link:

**[https://easychair.org/account/signin?l=mcIv4hzTjQIXERTVIdRiV4#](https://easychair.org/account/signin?l=mcIv4hzTjQIXERTVIdRiV4#)**

Both tracks use blinded peer review organized by experienced human–computer interaction scholars. **For further details and formatting requirements, see the conference website's call for papers ([http://chchi2021.icachi.org/](http://chchi2021.icachi.org/)）。**

**This year's Chinese CHI seeks deeper discussion of how human–computer interaction can develop in China's cultural context. We warmly welcome submissions in this interdisciplinary area!**

Topics Include, but Are Not Limited To:

- User Experience and Usability
- Specific Application Areas
- Health, Accessibility and Aging
- Privacy, Security and Visualization
- Interaction Beyond the Individual
- Games and Play
- Design
- Interaction Techniques, Devices and Modalities
- Understanding People: Theory, Concepts, Methods
- Engineering Interactive Systems and Technologies
- **Technology Use or Development in the Chinese Context**
- **Understanding Chinese Users Interaction Techniques Tailored to Chinese Users**
- **Community Engagement in China**

Acceptance Notification Dates:

- Full Papers notification: September 1, 2021
- Late-Breaking Work notification: September 10, 2021

**The conference will take place online on October 16–17, 2021, with free public livestreams.** Supported by academic and industry partners, it features senior scholars from internationally renowned institutions and companies as speakers and panelists. Accepted papers will be presented by their authors online or through video introductions, offering exchanges with human–computer interaction researchers worldwide. **General attendance is free. At least one author of each accepted, peer-reviewed paper must pay the US$100 registration fee.**
