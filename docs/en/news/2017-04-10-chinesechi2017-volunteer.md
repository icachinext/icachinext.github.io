Chinese CHI, the International Chinese HCI Conference, is the flagship conference of International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI). It is a leading research forum for the global Chinese community, addressing all aspects of human–computer interaction (Human Computer Interaction, HCI) across user experience design, software engineering, human factors, information systems, social science, and creative arts.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-04-10-chinesechi2017-volunteer-02.jpg)

The conference's success depends on the collaboration and support of our volunteers. Chinese CHI 2017, hosted by Guangdong University of Technology, will take place on June 7–9, 2017, in Guangzhou, China.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-04-10-chinesechi2017-volunteer-03.png)

**The conference is recruiting 20 volunteers.**

## 1. What do volunteers do?

Answer: Each volunteer serves for one day, consisting of a morning and an afternoon. Duties include registration and reception; room assistance, such as checking badges, connecting equipment, and timing presentations; guiding visitors and maintaining order in exhibition areas; and other small tasks.

## 2. What benefits do volunteers receive?

Accepted volunteers receive complimentary conference registration and meal vouchers for daytime meals.

## 3. Who can apply?

- Anyone with good Chinese or bilingual Chinese–English communication skills may apply;
- Preference will be given to students;
- Among student applicants, preference will be given to Student Forum submitters;

## 4. How do I apply?

- Before April 30, email your name, occupation, and affiliation to Haipeng Mi (mhp@mail.tsinghua.edu.cn).
- Students who have submitted to the Student Forum should include their submission number.
