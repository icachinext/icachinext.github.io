## Organizers

**The Huawei Smart Mobility Innovation Competition** is organized by Huawei Technologies and International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction, supported by China Computer Federation's human–computer interaction Technical Committee, the Shanghai Institute of Communications' Intelligent Connectivity Committee, and the China Video Industry Association's Intelligent Interaction Technology Working Committee, and hosted by Guangdong University of Technology.

## I Organization

Huawei Smart Mobility Innovation Competition is hosted by Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd and International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction, supported by The Committee of Human Computer Interaction of China Computer Federation, Intelligent Networking Committee of Shanghai Institute Communication and Intelligent Interaction Technology Working Committee of China Video Industry Association, and organised by Guangdong University of Technology.

## Competition Content

### 1. Theme Requirements:

Entries should **integrate software and hardware across intelligent devices inside and outside vehicles to provide safe, comfortable, efficient driving, mobile entertainment and working, and intelligent experiences**.

## II Competition Contents

### 1 Theme Requirements

Entrants are asked to design their projects around the following theme: Focus on hardware and software integration of smart devices inside and outside the car to provide a safe, comfortable and efficient driving, audio-visual entertainment and mobile office experience.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2023-01-20-registration-01.jpg)

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2023-01-20-registration-02.jpg)

The competition has three themes: **Intelligent Driving**, **The Third Living Space**, and **All-Scenario Connectivity**, detailed below:

**1. Intelligent Driving:** Connect phones, vehicles, and more smart devices to provide drivers with safer, more convenient, and more efficient driving experiences.

**2. The Third Living Space:** How can additional smart devices inside vehicles provide passengers with comfortable, immersive mobile audiovisual entertainment or mobile working experiences?

**3. All-Scenario Connectivity:** Connect smart devices inside and outside vehicles, placing users at the center and enriching intelligent travel experiences.

Details are as follows.

(1) Smart driving: connecting mobile phones, cars and more intelligent devices to provide drivers with a safer, more convenient and more efficient driving experience.

(2) Third living space: how to combine more intelligent devices in the car to provide passengers with a comfortable, immersive audio-visual entertainment or an efficient and comfortable mobile office experience.

(3) All-scene connectivity: connecting various intelligent devices inside and outside the car, providing a richer intelligent experience around the travel scenario with the user as the centre.

## Eligibility and Registration

### 1. Eligibility

The Smart Mobility Innovation Competition is open to **students at universities in China and abroad**, including associate, undergraduate, master's, and doctoral students, and **working professionals**, excluding employees of Huawei Technologies and its affiliates.

Participants must bring valid government-issued identification to the competition.

### 2. Registration

(1) Applicants may compete individually or in teams and must register themselves and their teams through the official competition website.

(2) University student entries must have at least one supervisor. Student teams may have no more than eight students and three supervisors.

(3) Working professionals must not be employed by Huawei Technologies or its affiliates. Teams may have no more than eight members and need not have a supervisor.

(4) Teams may include members from different universities or organizations. Participants may enter multiple projects.

(5) Individuals and teams must sign the Smart Mobility Innovation Competition Participant Declaration when registering. Successful registration constitutes full acceptance of the declaration; see Attachment 1.

(6) No registration fee is charged.

## III Eligibility Statements and Team Entry Methods

### 1 Eligibility Statements

Huawei Smart Mobility Innovation Competition is open to entries from domestic and international university students (including college, undergraduate, master's and doctoral students) and active members of society (except employees of Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. and the related). Participants must use official and valid government-issued ID card to join the competition.

### 2 Team Registration Instructions

(1)Applicants may choose to enter individually or as a team, and must complete registration and team entry by visiting the official website of Huawei Smart Mobility Innovation Competition.

(2)University students are required to have at least one supervisor for each project. In principle, it is recommended to enter in teams, with the number of students in each team not exceeding eight and the number of supervisors not exceeding three.

(3)The unit of the social worker cannot be Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd and the related. In participating in a team, the number of students in each team should not exceed eight, and there can be no supervisor.

(4)Teams are allowed to be formed across schools and units , and participants may register for more than one project.

(5)Individuals or teams are required to sign the Smart Mobility Innovation Competition Participant Declaration at the time of registration, and upon successful registration, the individual or team will be deemed to have fully accepted the Participant Declaration (see Annex 1 for details).

(6) No entry fee will be charged for the competition.

## Entry Submission Guidelines

### Preliminary Round:

1) Design proposal

Clearly and thoroughly explain the work's design approach, concept, and meaning, including its principal design and implementation plans.

2) Electronic images

Submit no more than five landscape-format design images. One should show the entire work; the remaining four should show different angles with reference objects and represent the original appearance as faithfully as possible. Images must be JPG files at 300 dpi, A3 size, and no larger than 20 MB each.

3) Demonstration video

Record the creative process and key production stages in a landscape-format video, with appropriate narration and explanation if helpful. Upload a single MP4 file with clear, stable images and clear audio, no longer than three minutes and under 300 MB.

4) Project presentation materials (PowerPoint)

The presentation must include at least a title page, an introduction to the work, and its implementation results, within ten slides.

Submission method: use PDF for all documents and MP4 for videos. Compress the work into a package and upload it as an attachment through the official submission page.

5) Presentation requirements: complete the design in the preliminary round, create a demonstrable prototype in the second round, and improve, upgrade, and test the prototype in the final round.

**Note:** Provide accurate personal and project information. Plagiarism is prohibited and results in immediate disqualification. Participants bear all consequences of copyright disputes.

Design proposals, images, videos, and presentations must not show institution names, author names, or logos of other competitions. Such information must be obscured; unmasked identifying information will incur scoring deductions.

## IV Entry Submission Guidelines

### 1 Submission Guidelines for Preliminary Round

(1)Requirements for submission of design proposals: You will need to set out clearly and in detail the design ideas, concept and meaning of your work, including the main design and implementation options.

(2)Guidelines for submitting electronic images: No more than 5 images should be submitted electronically (1 image should reflect the whole work; the other 4 images should be from multiple angles with references and reflect the original work as far as possible). The electronic file should be in JPG format, 300dpi, A3 paper size, and the size of a single image should not exceed 20M.

(3)Guidelines for submitting demo videos:You are required to make a video of the creative process and key points in the production process, with some appropriate narration and explanation. (Technical requirements for the video: the video file should be combined into one file and uploaded in MP4 format, with clear and stable images and clear sound, not exceeding 3 minutes in length and less than 300M).

(4)Guidelines for the submission of project reporting materials (PPT presentation files): The PPT should include at least 3 areas: title page, introduction to the work and presentation of the realisation. The total number of pages of the PPT should not exceed 10 and there should be at least 1 reference.

Submission: All documents are in PDF format and all videos are in MP4 format. Package and compress your work and submit as an attachment on the submission page in the official website.

(5)Requirements for the presentation of your work: Participants should complete the design of your work in the preliminary round, produce a prototype of the product for demonstration in the semi-final round, and complete the improvement, upgrading and testing of the prototype in the final round.

**Note:** Please fill in the personal and relevant information of your work. Plagiarism is strictly prohibited, once found, the entry is immediately disqualified. If the work generates copyright disputes, all consequences will be borne by the participant.

School/unit, author's name and other event logos are not allowed to appear on the artwork, and points will be deducted if found.

## Competition Schedule and Process

### 1. Schedule

1) Preliminary registration and submission: December 20, 2022–February 19, 2023

2) Second-round shortlist announcement: March 1, 2023

3) Software and hardware resources distributed: March 5, 2023

4) Second-round submission: April 5–10, 2023

5) Finals and awards: April 2023

### 2. Process:

The 2023 Smart Mobility Innovation Competition has three stages:

Stage 1: Preliminary round. Competition launch, workshop guidance and Q&A, completion of designs, and selection of second-round entries.

Stage 2: Second round. Distribution of software and hardware resources, prototype development, and selection of finalists.

Stage 3: Final round. Final assessment and selection of award-winning works.

The detailed process is as follows:

**1) Preliminary Round**

**December 20, 2022–February 19, 2023: Preliminary Registration and Submission**

Teams submit written design proposals addressing the themes. Experts select no more than 30 projects for the second round.

**March 1, 2023: Second-Round Shortlist Announcement**

## 2) Second Round

**March 5, 2023: Software and Hardware Resources Distributed**

Second-round teams receive software and hardware from the competition working group, which provides free design technology briefings and collective Q&A through a kickoff meeting. Teams can discuss technical questions at any time through the designated email address.

**April 5–10, 2023: Second-Round Submission**

Innovative Design Awards are selected among second-round entries that received resources. No more than ten works advance to final assessment.

## 3) Final Round

**April 2023: Finals and Awards**

Final assessment will consist of on-site presentations and defenses, moving online if required by the pandemic or force majeure. Finalists are assessed against the criteria, and prizes and award certificates are presented at the event.

### 2 Competition Process

Huawei Smart Mobility Innovation Competition will be conducted in three phases.

(1)Stage 1: Preliminary round, competition launch, workshop guidance and Q&A, preliminary phase.

(2)Stage 2: Semi-final round, distribution of hardware and software resources, final stage.

(3)Stage 3: Final round, judging and awarding of winning entries.

**The process will be as follows.**

(1) Preliminary Stage

①Time for submission of preliminary competition entries (2022.12.20-2023.02.19)：Individual or teams will be required to submit text design proposals on relevant themes. No more than 30 project entries will be selected by the expert panel to enter the semi-final stage.

②Announcement of the nominees to the semi-final round (2023.03.01)

(2) Semi-final Round

①Distribution of hardware and software resources (2023.03.05)：Nominees are eligible to enter the semi-final round and receive hardware and software resources from the competition committee, who is to provide free technical presentations and Q&A sessions on the design of the solution through kick off meetings, and the nominees to the semi-final round can discuss and exchange technical issues at any time through the designated email address.

②Submission of the work (2023.04.05 - 2023.04.10)：Ten finalists will be selected for final judging among the project entries that have been awarded hardware and software resources.

(3) Final Round

Final judging and award presentation (2023.04)：The final judging will be held online, or offline in case of force majeure factor, with a centralised speech and defence presentation on site . The 10 finalists will be judged according to the unified selection criteria and the results of each award will be produced, and the awards and certificates will be presented in the presence of the winners.
