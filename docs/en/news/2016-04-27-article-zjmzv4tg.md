The Cooperative Information and Systems Lab (CISL) at Fudan University was founded in 2000 by Professor Ning Gu, who has served as its director ever since.

The laboratory focuses on:

- Cooperative Computing
- Computer-Supported Cooperative Work (CSCW)
- Social Computing
- human–computer interaction(HCI)

The laboratory currently has three associate professors or associate research fellows, including Tun Lu and Xianghua Ding; three senior engineers, engineers, or lecturers; and more than 30 doctoral and master's students, forming a well-balanced team with strong research capabilities.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2016-04-27-article-zjmzv4tg-01.png)

More than 60 former and current laboratory students reunited for Fudan University's 110th anniversary

Guided by **Human-Centered Computing**, the laboratory integrates computer science and social science. Its research includes models and environments integrating multiple forms of collaboration, social computing theories and methods for social order, qualitative and quantitative analysis of collaborative interaction, and hardware and software for mobile-cloud multimodal interaction. It has achieved notable results in consistency maintenance for large distributed collaborative systems, big-data analysis and prediction of online community behavior, and interpretation of interactions involving disadvantaged or special populations. This work has appeared at leading venues in CSCW, human–computer interaction, ubiquitous computing, web research, and distributed computing, including ACM CSCW, CHI, UbiComp, GROUP, WWW, and ICDCS.

Building on these results, the laboratory combines collaborative interaction theory, qualitative and quantitative analysis, and interaction design to address social problems. Its large-scale deployed systems include the Shanghai Disabled Persons' Federation's cloud platform for independent living and support, combining big data with intelligent interaction and control devices for older and disabled people; and a Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development energy-monitoring private-cloud platform for green-campus management at Fudan University. The Shanghai platform attracted favorable public attention through coverage by Dragon TV, Shanghai TV, Phoenix, People's Daily Online, China Daily, Guangming Daily, and other major media.

The laboratory combines independent research and innovation with strong practical skills. Regular high-quality discussions and collaboration with leading international universities and research institutes give students opportunities to learn directly from distinguished scholars.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2016-04-27-article-zjmzv4tg-02.jpg)

Students mainly come from leading Chinese universities, including Fudan University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, the University of Science and Technology of China, Shandong University, and Jilin University. Graduates have established careers at major universities, leading IT companies and research institutes, and in finance. The laboratory welcomes applications from students interested in research, willing to learn and challenge themselves, and visits from scholars in China and abroad.
