## Scan the QR Code to Visit the Chinese CHI 2021 Website

**Chinese CHI connects Chinese human–computer interaction scholars, academia, and industry worldwide.** Since 2012, eight editions have been held in Paris, Toronto, Seoul, Silicon Valley, Guangzhou, Montreal, Xiamen, and online. This year's theme is Identity, Value, and Trust amid technological progress. The conference encourages human–computer interaction research grounded in Chinese culture and contexts and supports human–computer interaction's local development. Supported by academic and industry partners, it features senior scholars from internationally renowned universities and companies as speakers and panelists. Accepted papers will be presented by their authors online or through video introductions.
