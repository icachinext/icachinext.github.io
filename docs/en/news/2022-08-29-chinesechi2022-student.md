Inclusion . Resilience . New Normal

Chinese CHI 2022, organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), **will take place in Guangzhou, China, on October 22–23, 2022, in a hybrid online and in-person format.** Its theme is **Inclusion, Resilience, and New Normal.**

The conference **invites practitioners, researchers, and students in art and design** to **submit digital artwork proposals** to the Chinese CHI 2022 human–computer interaction conference's Digital Art Exhibition, which will take place on-site and online.

The conference also **invites students from diverse design backgrounds**, including human–computer interaction, industrial, product, visual, user experience, and interaction design, **to enter the Student Competition** on-site or online.

**One day remains until the Digital Art Exhibition and Student Competition proposal deadline.** We welcome your applications.

## Digital Art Exhibition

Digital Arts Gallery

- **The Chinese CHI 2022 Digital Art Exhibition aims to create a highly inclusive interdisciplinary space** for new media art, novel interaction technologies, original conceptual designs, smart product demonstrations, experimental audiovisual works, explorations of materiality, games, and other interactive experiences. Within its thematic framework, the exhibition fosters meaningful exchanges among exhibitors **to enrich, extend, or reframe relationships between art, design, and technology.**
- A committee of experienced experts in relevant fields, to be announced later, will review all submissions. **Selected works will be archived in the Chinese CHI 2022 Digital Art Exhibition yearbook, and authors will receive an ICACHI exhibition award certificate**.

## Student Competition

- **The competition showcases students' ideas and skills in human–computer interaction design and user experience**, raising the visibility of their work and providing expert feedback.
- Submissions will be assessed by a committee of experienced experts in relevant fields, to be announced later, and through an online audience vote. **Final award recipients will be invited to exhibit their award-winning work at the Digital Art Exhibition and receive an ICACHI competition award certificate.**
- **For proposal requirements, see:**
