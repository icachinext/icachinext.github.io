## IWHEC 2024

## International Workshop on Human-Engaged Computing

## About IWHEC

The International Workshop on Human-Engaged Computing (International Workshop on Human-Engaged Computing, IWHEC) rethinks the relationship between people and computers. By inviting experts in human–computer interaction, neuroscience, humanities, philosophy, social sciences, design, computer science, and AI to share their insights, we aim to create a platform for collective intelligence and global intellectual exchange.

This workshop and the launch of Human-Engaged Computing are part of Chinese CHI 2024 and will be held in a hybrid format. You may attend in person at Southern University of Science and Technology or watch the livestream.

## Workshop Theme

Over the past few years, you may have experienced both ICT's support for people and its potential negative effects, alongside milestone advances in AI. What do these developments mean for us? Drawing on our disciplinary knowledge and thorough discussion of human–technology relationships, we hope to identify insights, common ground, and practical directions for future research and technological development.

### Guozhong Dai

Chinese Academy of Sciences

Formerly chief engineer at Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences, he is now chief researcher and doctoral supervisor at the Laboratory of human–computer interaction Technology and Intelligent Information Processing. A graduate of the University of Science and Technology of China, he visited the University of Maryland's computer science department during 1982-1985. He served on the automation expert group and expert committee of China's 863 Program and advised the information division of the 973 Program. He is honorary chair of ACM SIGCHI China and honorary director of China Computer Federation's human–computer interaction committee. Researcher Guozhong Dai has led and participated in National Natural Science Foundation, National High-Tech R&D, and National Basic Research projects. His main areas are computer graphics, human–computer interaction, and software engineering. He has published over one hundred influential papers and two monographs, won four National Science and Technology Progress Awards, and received multiple Chinese Academy of Sciences and ministerial awards.

### Zhiyong Fu

Tsinghua University

Zhiyong Fu is president of International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction, deputy head and tenured associate professor of Information Art and Design at Tsinghua University's Academy of Arts and Design, and a doctoral supervisor. He directs Tsinghua University's Art and Science Innovation Base, is deputy director of the China Innovation and Entrepreneurship Education Research Center, secretary-general of the China Industrial Design Association's Information and Interaction Design Committee, and co-founder of the Global Design Futures Network. His research addresses smart products and services, human-centered cities, and design futures.

## Shengdong Zhao

City University of Hong Kong

Shengdong Zhao is a professor in the School of Creative Media and Department of Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong. In 2009 he founded the Synteraction research laboratory, formerly NUS-HCI, at National University of Singapore. He earned his PhD in computer science from the University of Toronto and master's degree in information management and systems from UC Berkeley. He has extensive experience developing innovative interface tools and applications and publishes regularly at CHI, ToCHI, Ubicomp/IMWUT, CSCW, UIST, and IUI. In 2017 he served as a senior adviser to Huawei's Consumer Business Group. An active HCI community member and frequent program committee member, he co-chaired papers for ACM SIGCHI in 2019 and 2020 and will co-chair papers for ACM UIST 2025. In 2017 he introduced the concept of Heads-Up Computing and has contributed key projects and publications, including a September Communications of the ACM feature exploring wearable intelligent assistants as a new interaction paradigm. His work develops interfaces that improve everyday life. Learn more at www.shengdongzhao.com.
