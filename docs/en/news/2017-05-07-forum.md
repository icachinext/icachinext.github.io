Chinese CHI 2017, hosted by Guangdong University of Technology, will take place on June 7–9, 2017, in Guangzhou, China.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-05-07-forum-02.jpg)

Increasingly diverse relationships between people, vehicles, and roads will give rise to groundbreaking driving and passenger experiences. More complex future transport scenarios and easier access to driving will depend on innovative forms of human–computer interaction enabled by intelligent transportation technologies.

The Intelligent Transportation and Internet of Things forum at CHCI 2017 invites leading figures from businesses, universities, and research communities to share recent developments and discuss ubiquitous intelligent transportation and human–computer interaction in the IoT era.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-05-07-forum-03.jpg)

## Speakers

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-05-07-forum-04.jpg)

**Keiichi Sato** is Charles Owen Professor and doctoral supervisor at the Institute of Design, Illinois Institute of Technology, where he chairs the academic committee and directs the doctoral program. He serves on the academic boards of Design Studies and the International Journal of Design and is a visiting professor in the School of Art and Design at Guangdong University of Technology. His research focuses on human-centered product, system, and service design and integration, and interaction design methodology. He has led major research projects in the United States and Japan, published more than 70 high-quality papers, and received several best paper awards.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-05-07-forum-05.jpg)

**Xiangyang Xin** is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Jiangnan University's School of Design. With a PhD in design from Carnegie Mellon University, he is among the few Chinese scholars to have earned this degree in the United States and the first to return to mainland China. His education and professional experience span several disciplines. He developed influential interaction design theories and methods, including the Five Elements of Interaction Design and Behavioral Logic, and established China's first interaction design program at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. As conference chair or vice chair, he has helped organize major international events advancing interaction design in China, including the International Conference on Interaction Design, China Interaction Design Experience Week, and Redesigning Design Education.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-05-07-forum-06.jpg)

**Fan Zhang** is vice president of the GAC Automotive Engineering Institute. He has worked in automotive styling for more than a decade. At Daimler AG in Germany, he designed exterior styling for vehicle development across Mercedes-Benz, Maybach, and Smart. His Concept A-Class embodied Mercedes-Benz's new youthful, dynamic, and compelling design language and became a benchmark for its next generation of vehicles. Since joining GAC R&D in 2011, he has strengthened his team's capabilities in innovation methods, design tools, collaboration, resource allocation, and quality control. The team independently completed high-quality concept designs for E-Linker, E-Jet, WitSTAR, i-Lounge, EV Coupe, EnLight, and Enspirit, as well as industrial designs for production models including GS4, GA6, GA8, GS8, and GS7.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-05-07-forum-07.jpg)

**Ting Han** is vice dean of the School of Media and Design at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, an associate professor with a PhD, and head of industrial design. His honors include Shanghai Jiao Tong University's Outstanding Teacher and Candlelight awards, recognition in Shanghai Jiao Tong University's first Morning Star Young Scholars program, support from the China Scholarship Council's university faculty development scheme, and selection for the Shanghai Pujiang Talent Program in 2013. He has led or participated in projects under China's 973 and 863 programs, major Chinese Academy of Engineering advisory studies, National Natural Science Foundation and National Social Science Fund projects, and Shanghai's 12th Five-Year Plan for philosophy and social sciences, including subprojects of 973 and engineering advisory programs. His interests include design management and strategy, user research and experience design, innovative design, and interaction design.
