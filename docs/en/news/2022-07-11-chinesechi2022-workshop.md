## Call for Interaction Design Workshops

Chinese CHI 2022, organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), **will be held in Guangzhou, China, on October 22–23, 2022, in a hybrid online and in-person format.** The theme is **Inclusion, Resilience, and New Normal.**

**We invite corporate design and development teams, advanced doctoral students, and university research groups to use the Chinese CHI 2022 human–computer interaction conference workshops to share recent human–computer interaction technologies and research methods, exchange experience and findings, and inform future research.**

Chinese CHI 2022 particularly encourages two types of workshop proposal:

- **Interaction Platform Workshops:** Companies or individuals provide development platforms, open-source tools, or design tools, such as Google's tensorflow.js, Huawei's Data Lake Visualization platform, or the CARLA open-source driving simulator. Participants develop ideas and rapidly create interactive prototypes or design proposals for discussion. The outcomes can inform the organizers' research.
- **Co-Design Workshops:** Companies or individuals propose discussion topics based on their research methods or projects, such as data-driven design, data visualization, explainable AI, human–AI collaboration, or smart glasses. Organizers guide participants through brainstorming and rapid sketches and proposals for cross-discussion. Outcomes can help companies or individuals assess their research methods.

**Chinese CHI 2022 workshops may last half a day or a full day and are expected to take place before the main conference on October 22–23, 2022, in a hybrid format. Each workshop is expected to have 8–15 participants.**

## Proposal Guidelines

Submission Guidelines

1. Organizers should prepare their workshop proposals using **the attached template**. **Send the proposal document to:**
1. **workshop.chichi@gmail.com**
1. **After acceptance, workshop organizers must issue a call for participants.** We recommend promotion through a dedicated website, social media, email, and other channels. The Chinese CHI website will also list accepted workshops' publicity materials. **Organizers decide the number of workshop participants.**
1. **Organizers may request necessary equipment and support from the Chinese CHI committee,** including video equipment and hardware materials. In-person workshops must comply with pandemic prevention requirements.

## Call for Workshop

**We invite you to submit proposals for workshops at Chinese CHI 2022.** The workshops can focus on **any area related to the field of human-computer interaction, industrial design, user experience and user interface design.** In particular, workshops on challenging, emerging areas related to this year's conference topics are especially sought. The workshops might address basic research, applied research, HCI practice, HCI education, new methodologies, emerging application areas, or design innovations.

**We highly encourage the enterprise research, design or engineering team in the company to organize a workshop.** The enterprise organizers could provide their developer tools/design platform for participants, and testing the newly designed technologies in the workshop and collect feedback. It might also be a good place to brainstorm new applications and usage context for the newly developed HCI technologies.

**We also highly encourage PhD students in the area of HCI and design, to consider organizing a workshop** and leverage it as a place to share your research results or evaluate your design methods. It might be a good chance to find researchers who care about the same challenges, questions, and research agendas as you.

We particularly encourage proposals for:

1. **Highly interactive workshops.** If you are working on developing new technologies or modalities for HCI, you could bring your interactive prototypes to the workshop, where the attendees could work together to think about the potential application areas, use cases, research topic or that suggest promising directions for future work.
1. **Highly collaborative workshops.** If you are working on developing new design methods, you could apply your design methods in organizing a co-creation workshop on relevant design topics. The outcomes of the workshops can be used to evaluate your methods.

**The expected dates of the workshops would be October 22-23, 2022,** before the main track of the conference. **Workshops are scheduled for either half day or one day long. A typical workshop will have 8 to 15 participants. The workshops will take place in physical and/or virtual form, depending on the situation with COVID-19.**

## Submission Guidelines

The following is an outline of the submission and selection process of Workshop.

1. **Workshop organizers submit a workshop proposal (see below for content and format) to Chinese CHI:**
1. **workshop.chichi@gmail.com**
1. The submitted workshop proposals will be by reviewed by members of the program committee and peer experts.
1. **Once a workshop is accepted, workshop organizers are responsible for publicizing the workshop and soliciting potential participants.** Workshop organizers solicit participants for their workshop through a Call for Participation. Chinese CHI will also place a link to the workshop’s website on a page listing accepted workshops.
1. **Upon acceptance, the workshop organizers are required to create a sharable booklet, or a website with workshop specific information.** The workshop organizers may decide to cap the number of attendees for the workshop.
1. Organize the workshop with attendees

**Attachment: Workshop Proposal Template**

1. **Organizers**
1. **- Name:** Organizers’ full names
1. **- Contact information:** Affiliations, job titles, postal addresses, e-mail addresses, URLs, and phone
1. **- Brief biography:** 100-200 words, focusing on the organizers’ expertise in the field and experience as workshop organizers
1. **Workshop content**
1. **- Title:** Workshop title
1. **- Abstract:** Max 150 words describing the workshop (suitable for the conference’s website)
1. **- Topics and motivation:** What are the topics, themes, and areas of interest of the workshop? How is the workshop relevant to this year’s Chinese CHI topic? How does the workshop connect HCI, UX research communities?
1. **- Goals and expected results:** Explicitly state the goals of the workshop and how you intend to reach them. What are the expected results of the workshop?
1. **- Format:** What is the planned workshop format (paper presentations, working sessions, lightning talks, demonstrations, etc.)? If the workshop takes place in virtual form, what is the virtual plan? What are the planned pre- and post-workshop activities?
1. **- Participants:** What is the expected number of submissions and participants? Provide a plan for attracting sufficient submissions and promoting attendanceIf applicable, please provide information from previous or related workshops. Have there been previous workshops on the same or a closely related topic? When, where and with how many participants.
1. **- Required equipment** (if the conference remains physical): Overhead projector, PC projector, whiteboard, flip charts, microphone, etc.
