NUS-HCI Lab *welcome*

The human–computer interaction laboratory at National University of Singapore (NUS-HCI Lab) is a leading research laboratory in human–computer interaction in Asia and worldwide.

NUS-HCI Lab group photograph

Founded in 2009 by Dr. Shengdong Zhao, the laboratory has been operating for more than seven years. It has produced substantial high-quality research, including nearly 20 papers at ACM SIGCHI, the leading human–computer interaction conference, placing it among Asia's foremost laboratories.

The laboratory takes a people-centered approach to nurturing individual talent. It aims to produce world-class research and researchers while contributing to society. Personal interests and abilities guide individualized study and work plans, and students' interests are a key consideration when choosing projects.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2016-04-17-article-zk2udokb-02.jpg)

Laboratory members presenting projects at the ArtScience Museum

To address challenges often faced by Asian students in English writing, public speaking, and communication, the laboratory offers weekly training designed to produce substantial improvement within six to twelve months.

The laboratory fosters a warm, harmonious working environment and encourages mutual support and collaboration. Members' views are heard, and an annual retreat brings everyone together to discuss laboratory operations and improvements.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2016-04-17-article-zk2udokb-03.jpg)

Laboratory barbecue night

Beyond work, the laboratory encourages healthy, varied lives. Members organize social, sporting, and recreational activities while maintaining effective research. Except around a few submission deadlines, weekend and late-night overtime are generally discouraged to support work–life balance.

Visitors are welcome, and internship opportunities are often available. Those interested in joining can apply for graduate study in the Department of Computer Science at National University of Singapore; application information is available from the School of Computing. An internship can also provide an opportunity to experience the laboratory before applying.

Application email: join-nus-hci@googlegroups.com
