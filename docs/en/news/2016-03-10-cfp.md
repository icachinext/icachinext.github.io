Both developed and developing countries face population aging. By 2050, people over 65 are projected to account for 27% of the population in developed countries and 15% in developing countries. The digital revolution has arrived rapidly but has not adequately benefited older adults. Despite their growing share of the population, mainstream HCI research remains focused on younger people, leaving older adults increasingly excluded from the benefits of the digital age.

ITAP 2016 will be held in Kochi, Japan, on October 21, 2016, focusing on coordinated development of human–computer interaction technologies for older adults. More than 100 world-class researchers will be invited to exchange ideas and advance HCI.

**Submissions are now invited**. Accepted papers will appear in a special issue of *Interacting with Computers*.

## Submission Categories

ITAP 2016 welcomes contributions in Human Computer Interaction, Human Factors, Interaction Design, and related fields.
