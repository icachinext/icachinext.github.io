**The Chinese CHI 2023 Program Is Available!**

**Early-Bird Registration Countdown!**

Program Ready! Early bird ticket registration countdown for Chinese CHI 2023!

With the theme Generative, Reflective, and Envisioning, **Chinese CHI 2023** will take place **November 13–16, 2023**, on **Kura Kura Island, Bali**, **Indonesia**.

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), this academic conference has become an important connection among Chinese HCI scholars, academia, and industry worldwide. Since 2012, ten editions have been held in Paris, Toronto, Seoul, Silicon Valley, Guangzhou, Montreal, Xiamen, and online. Conference papers are included in the ACM Digital Library and submitted for EI indexing.

The Eleventh International Symposium of Chinese CHI (Chinese CHI 2023) will be held from November 13–16 in Kurakura Island, Bali, Indonesia. Supported by the International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), Chinese CHI is steered by a group of globally distributed HCI researchers, practitioners, academics, and students concerning about the development of HCI as a thriving field in the global Chinese communities.

Chinese CHI serves as a primary forum for connecting the international HCI community to the booming Chinese computing industry. The theme of Chinese CHI 2023 is Generative, Reflective, and Envisioning. As the first time we can gather globally again at Chinese CHI after the pandemic, We welcome discussions on the value, significance, and social contribution of human-computer interaction in a wide context. We plan to have all accepted papers included in the conference proceedings and published through the ACM Digital Library and EI Compendex.
