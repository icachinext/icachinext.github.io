## Professor Xiangshi Ren

## Attends the 2023 China Association for Science and Technology Overseas Talent Forum

The China Association for Science and Technology Overseas Talent Forum opened in Changchun, Jilin Province. Organized by China Association for Science and Technology, it was hosted by the Jilin Association for Science and Technology, China Centre for International Science and Technology Exchange, Changchun New Area Administrative Committee, and Changchun Association for Science and Technology. At President Zhiyong Fu's request, lifetime honorary president Professor Xiangshi Ren represented ICACHI. The forum shared practical experience in connecting with and serving overseas scientific talent, explored effective ways to support world-class talent centers and innovation hubs, released assessments of overseas talent program bases, and held base designation, distinguished expert appointment, partnership signing, and overseas talent innovation and entrepreneurship roadshow events.

ICACHI was founded in Austin, USA, in May 2012. That summer, then-president Professor Xiangshi Ren and vice president Professor Henry Duh met Dr. Jiansheng Zhang, then director of China Association for Science and Technology's international department, in Beijing. ICACHI signed an agreement with China Association for Science and Technology's overseas talent office, becoming one of over 90 overseas scientific organizations partnered with China Association for Science and Technology. ICACHI was distinctive as the only professional Chinese scientific organization spanning the entire world.

On the afternoon of April 25, 2023, Hui Luo, director of China Association for Science and Technology's international department, met overseas delegates at the Jilin Provincial Hotel. Professor Xiangshi Ren introduced ICACHI and the concepts of human–computer interaction and Human-Engaged Computing, making a strong impression on Director Luo and the delegates. That evening, Jilin's Party secretary, governor, organization department head, and other officials met overseas representatives. Secretary Jing encouraged investment, entrepreneurship, and international exchanges in Jilin. Professor Xiangshi Ren introduced ICACHI and its annual conference, emphasizing their international influence and importance.

The opening ceremony on April 26 was chaired by Professor Qinglin Wang, deputy director of China Association for Science and Technology's international department, with addresses by Hui Luo and Jilin's vice governor. Ceremonies followed for overseas talent base designations, expert appointments, partnership signings, and project launches. Afternoon keynotes and discussions explored advancing overseas talent work under new conditions, building high-level recruitment platforms, proactively recruiting abroad, and promoting innovation in talent bases. April 27 featured visits to high-tech zones and science parks and events including an automotive microelectronics development forum.

Before and after the forum, Professor Xiangshi Ren also visited Jilin University, exchanging ideas with faculty from the computer science and communications schools and international office.
