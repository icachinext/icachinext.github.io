Associate Professor Dakuo Wang at Northeastern University, USA, seeks several PhD students in human–computer interaction, machine learning, psychology, and design, with full scholarships and entry in fall 2023 or spring 2024. Professor Wang holds joint appointments in the College of Computer Sciences and College of Arts, Media and Design. His main area is Human-Centered AI, an emerging interdisciplinary field, alongside human–computer interaction and user experience, natural language processing, computer-supported cooperative work, and social computing. He focuses on linking real-world user needs with AI research questions and designing better systems toward a future of Human–AI Collaboration, where ordinary people benefit from AI in daily life and work. Dakuo Wang's team regularly publishes at leading human–computer interaction venues such as ACM CHI and CSCW and AI venues such as ACL and EMNLP, with multiple best paper awards and honorable mentions and more than 60 patents. Before Northeastern, Dakuo Wang worked at IBM T. J. Watson Research and the IBM–MIT AI Lab, leading IBM's human-centered AI strategy and IBM–Stanford collaboration, and earning the IBM Master Inventor title. He is an ACM and IEEE Distinguished Speaker and senior member, vice chair of ACM SIGCHI China, and member of China Computer Federation's human–computer interaction and cooperative computing committees, and has served for years as an area chair on the organizing committees of leading human–computer interaction conferences, including CHI and CSCW. Professor Dakuo Wang also co-translated the Chinese edition of Ways of Knowing in human–computer interaction.

## Admission Requirements

Professor Wang recruits through two PhD programs, in computer sciences and in arts and design. (1) Computer sciences applicants should have a background in computing, software, AI, NLP, graphics, or visualization; research or enterprise-level full-stack development experience is preferred. (2) Arts, Media and Design applicants should have a background in art and design, psychology, cognitive science, communication, or organizational behavior; research or industry experience is preferred.
The two programs have different fall 2023 application deadlines. Those interested in Arts, Media and Design must apply through the CAMD website before December 1, 2022: [https://camd.northeastern.edu/program/interdisciplinary-design-and-media-phd/](https://camd.northeastern.edu/program/interdisciplinary-design-and-media-phd/)

Those interested in computer sciences must apply through the college website before December 15, 2022: [https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/apply/phd-apply/](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/apply/phd-apply/)

For questions or to introduce yourself, send your CV and transcripts to Professor Dakuo Wang at: d.wang@northeastern.edu

For information about the supervisor, see: [https://www.dakuowang.com/](https://www.dakuowang.com/)

## University Profile

Northeastern University (NEU) is a private research university in central Boston, Massachusetts, in the northeastern United States. In recent years, it has established more than ten campuses, including locations in London and Silicon Valley, offering students a choice of study locations. It ranks 44th in U.S. News, and the Khoury College of Computer Science, home to Professor Dakuo Wang's laboratory, has remained among the top 20 in CSRankings for ten consecutive years ([http://csrankings.org/](http://csrankings.org/)). Northeastern has the country's top-ranked co-op and internship program. Professor Wang's laboratory works closely with major IT companies and strongly supports students' industry internships and career planning. For students aspiring to academic careers, he can support selected doctoral researchers in visiting laboratories at institutions such as MIT and Stanford.
NEU is in central Boston, in New England, known for its scenery and European cultural heritage, across the river from MIT and Harvard. The region has an exceptionally dense concentration of universities and research institutes, outstanding healthcare and educational resources, and a reputation as a life-sciences Silicon Valley. Technology companies such as Google, Amazon, Microsoft, and IBM, and biopharmaceutical companies including Biogen and Moderna, support the university and graduate employment. The university values ethnic diversity, and both campus and city welcome international students. Boston offers shopping, museums, concert halls, and theaters, with beaches, hiking trails, and ski destinations nearby. New York is three hours away by direct train; the airport is 15 minutes from campus and offers direct international flights to most parts of the world. Asian supermarkets including 99 Ranch and H-Mart, and Chinese restaurants such as Liuyishou, Little Sheep, and Meet Fresh, provide plentiful familiar food.
