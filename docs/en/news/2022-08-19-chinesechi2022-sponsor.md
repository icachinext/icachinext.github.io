## Invitation to Sponsors

**International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction** (International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction, abbreviated ICACHI) **will hold Chinese CHI 2022 in Guangzhou, China, on October 22–23, 2022, in a hybrid online and in-person format.**

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), this academic conference has become an important connection among Chinese HCI scholars, academia, and industry worldwide. Since 2012, nine editions have been held in Paris, Toronto, Seoul, Silicon Valley, Guangzhou, Montreal, and Xiamen. **Chinese CHI 2017 and 2019, hosted in China by Guangdong University of Technology and Xiamen University, attracted more than 2,000 HCI practitioners and researchers,** including professionals from over 50 technology companies such as Huawei, Baidu, Tencent, Microsoft, and Google, and professors from over 60 universities including Tsinghua University, Tongji University, Beijing Normal University, and Jiangnan University. This year's conference is expected to attract even more academic and industry participants.

**On behalf of the organizing committee, we warmly invite your company to participate in and sponsor Chinese CHI 2022.** Sponsorship will strengthen your connections with the global human–computer interaction research community, enhance your brand, and open new markets.
