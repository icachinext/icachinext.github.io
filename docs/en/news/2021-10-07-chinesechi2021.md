**Chinese CHI 2021, organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), will take place online on October 16–17, 2021, with free livestreams for participants.**

Two renowned Chinese HCI scholars will join the conference as **dialogue guests: Shumin Zhai from Google and Professor Xiangshi Ren from Kochi University, Japan.**

**The conference theme is Identity, Value, and Trust amid technological progress.** The two scholars will discuss frontier human–computer interaction questions and respond to concerns from audiences both within and beyond the venue, offering an unmissable exchange of ideas.

**The dialogue begins on October 17, 2021, at 9:00 AM Beijing Time.**

## Dialogue Guest Introductions

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-10-07-chinesechi2021-02.jpg)

### Shumin Zhai

Google Research

**Dr. Shumin Zhai received bachelor's and master's degrees in radio remote control and telemetry and computer applications from Xidian University in 1982 and 1984, respectively,1984-1989 then taught there and earned a PhD from the University of Toronto in 1995.** He was a senior computer science researcher at IBM Almaden in California and has worked at Google since January 2011. **The ShapeWriter project and team led by Dr. Shumin Zhai pioneered gesture keyboards, introducing the world's first such product, SHARKtex, in 2004.** His work has received extensive coverage from The New York Times, The San Jose Mercury News, The Wall Street Journal, and BBC. **Dr. Shumin Zhai was elected an ACM Fellow in 2010 for his outstanding gesture-keyboard contributions.**

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-10-07-chinesechi2021-03.png)

### Xiangshi Ren

Kochi University of Technology, Japan

**Xiangshi Ren is a tenured professor in the School of Information and director of the Human-Engaged Computing institute at Japan's Kochi University of Technology. A foreign member of the Engineering Academy of Japan and fellow of IPSJ, he has conducted fundamental human–computer interaction research and education for nearly 30 years.** He has trained doctoral and postdoctoral scholars from more than ten countries, now active across Asia, Africa, Europe, and Oceania, and received paper awards from ACM, IEEE, CCF, and IPSJ-related venues. ICACHI's founding and lifetime honorary president, he has been a visiting researcher at IBM Almaden and visiting professor at Toronto and Chinese universities. **Considering humanity's future and ideal technological relations, he proposed Human-Engaged Computing (Human-Engaged Computing), grounded in Eastern balanced moderation, and is advancing the new field.**
