## ICACHI Young Scholars Committee

### Qing Xia

Qing Xia is a doctoral student in the Department of Information Art and Design at Tsinghua University's Academy of Arts and Design, supervised by Associate Professor Zhiyong Fu, researching design foresight and design methods.

Design Futures Research Group, Academy of Arts and Design, Tsinghua University

**The Design Futures group is based in the Department of Information Art and Design at Tsinghua University's Academy of Arts and Design** and works with Tsinghua University's Art and Technology Innovation Base and Tsinghua University's China–Italy Design Innovation Base. **Led by Associate Professor Zhiyong Fu, it researches futures-oriented interaction design innovation methods, developing innovative thinking frameworks and intelligent design tools to tackle wicked problems in the digital era, improve design productivity, and shape resilient futures.**

**The laboratory has three main areas: design foresight theory and methods; Innovation and New Infrastructure theories and tools; and AI City design practice.** Its process-oriented research is forward-looking, open, digital, and interdisciplinary. It studies human–machine symbiotic paradigms in the intelligent era and interdisciplinary designers' cognition and behavior, using vision-building and prototyping to research, design, validate, and iterate intelligent digital design tools.

## Laboratory Director

### Zhiyong Fu

**Associate Professor Zhiyong Fu directs the Design Futures group at Tsinghua University's Academy of Arts and Design and is a doctoral supervisor and tenured associate professor in the Department of Information Art and Design at Tsinghua University.** He is deputy director of Tsinghua University's China–Italy Design Innovation Base and the China Research Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship Education; director of Tsinghua University's Art and Technology Innovation Base; secretary-general of the China Industrial Design Association's Information and Interaction Design Committee; and president of International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction. He serves on the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs' second expert committee for Globally Important Agricultural Heritage Systems and the China–Europe Art and Humanities Education Alliance. He teaches interaction design, service design, design thinking, innovation, and entrepreneurship at undergraduate and graduate levels. His research concerns intelligent products and services, human-centered cities, and design futures. Selected for the Ministry of Education's New Century Excellent Talents program in 2006, he visited Tsukuba in 1998 and Carnegie Mellon in 2008. He was a principal organizer of the China–US Young Maker Competition from 2014 to 2021 and has served on ACM SIGCHI and HCII committees.

## Research Areas

## Future Foresight

Exploring trends, examining change, and creating future value through design present new challenges in today's rapidly changing world. Moving beyond object-, user-, society-, and nature-centered approaches, future-centered design treats all possible futures—including the dynamic development of human subjects and external objects—as new design content and criteria.

**Grounded in design, futures studies, and forecasting, this research integrates futures thinking into design thinking, moving from technology foresight to design foresight. It considers the future through a humanistic lens and brings broader social reflection on worldviews and values into products and services.** It adds time as a design variable, treats evolution and trends as integral to design, and **helps creators examine current design and technological pathways through future possibilities.** Using AI and big data, it analyzes how near- and long-term futures influence present design, combining speculation and reflective practice to develop forward-looking theories and tools. **The group collaborates with Carnegie Mellon and Politecnico di Milano's design teams on research, online courses, co-creation workshops, and international conferences.**

## Innovation and New Infrastructure: Platforms and Tools

**Innovation and New Infrastructure is a design methodology and toolkit built around digital innovation methods.** It integrates design foresight, scenario building, and design fiction into interaction design practice, creating a speculative design and futures signals resource library and digital co-creation platform. Released at the 2020 Design Futures international conference exhibition, it became a model initiative for design-driven innovation.

Innovation and New Infrastructure encompasses ideas, tools, spaces, and ecosystems empowering industry–university–research collaboration. **It comprises an innovation space station, design toolkit, and design resource library.** The space station combines online collaboration with physical spaces, enabling innovators worldwide to work on their projects and collaborate, communicate, and critique through a shared online platform. The toolkit includes futures and design tools. The resource library draws on project experience and student coursework, organizing knowledge for reuse in design projects. **Serving cities and communities, it combines online and offline activity with human–machine co-creation to build desirable futures.**

## AI City: Design and Applications

**AI CITY is a series of immersive experiences exploring and reflecting on the integration of AI with future cities.** Through modules on future living, mobility, healthcare, and education, it imagines AI in human life from the perspectives of access, understanding, interaction, co-creation, and enhancement. It examines effects on human existence, alternative trends, and visions of future cities.

From the Artificial Intelligence City project's launch in 2018 to the virtual AI City experience in 2021, the work combines design tools with a Living Lab approach. Through city-as-a-service, it reimagines urban experiences and services, offering intelligent solutions for inclusive social innovation grounded in Chinese society. **Using AI, open-source hardware, and design futures methods, it constructs reflective future urban interaction scenarios.** Further development will create authoring tools and open platforms for more designers to develop scenarios and foresight together.

**This research collaborates with Tsinghua University's Spring Breeze Fund on inclusive services for older adults in the post-pandemic era and works internationally with UNDP, FAO, and the Swiss Embassy in China on social innovation projects.**

The group develops new interaction design methods and tools and an interdisciplinary Innovation and New Infrastructure design system. It leads international discussion of designing futures and expands information art and design's boundaries. Applying foresight to smart cities and services, it creates forward-looking experiences and sustainable solutions for digital age-friendly design, community cultural exchange, intelligent learning and work, and better lifestyles.
