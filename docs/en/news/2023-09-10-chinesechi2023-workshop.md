Chinese CHI 2023, organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), **will take place November 13–16, 2023, in Bali, Indonesia, in a hybrid format.** The theme is Generative, Reflect, and Envision.

**We warmly invite conference workshop proposals** covering human–computer interaction, industrial design, user experience, and user interface design. Topics may include fundamental or applied research, human–computer interaction practice, human–computer interaction education, methodologies, emerging applications, and design innovation.

Industry teams are welcome to organize workshops, offering new development tools or design platforms and testing designs or technologies to gather feedback for new applications of human–computer interaction. Doctoral students in human–computer interaction and design are also encouraged to organize workshops to share findings, evaluate design methods, and meet researchers with common interests.

We particularly encourage the following workshop types:

1. **Highly interactive workshops.** If you are developing a new human–computer interaction technology or paradigm, bring a prototype to explore potential applications, use cases, research questions, and future directions with participants.
1. **Highly collaborative workshops.** If you are developing a new design method, organize a workshop to apply and evaluate it.

## Workshop Information

Workshops are expected to take place **November 13, 2023**, last **half a day**, and typically have **8–15 participants.**

## Review Process

- Organizers submit a workshop proposal to Chinese CHI, following the content and formatting requirements below.
- Committee members and expert peers review the proposals.
- Accepted workshops are notified.
- After acceptance, organizers are responsible for promotion and participant recruitment, with assistance from the conference. They must provide a shareable publicity poster containing detailed workshop information.
- Organize and run the workshop.

## Important Dates

- **Proposal deadline: September 20, 2023, 23:59 AoE**
- Review notification: October 10, 2023, 23:59 AoE

## Workshop Proposal Content

Submit the proposal entirely in English, including:

- Organizer Information
- Name: each organizer's full name
- Contact information: affiliation, position, address, email, telephone, and other details
- Brief biography: approximately 100–200 words, emphasizing expertise and relevant workshop-organizing experience
- Main Proposal
- Title
- Abstract, up to 150 words
- Theme and Motivation
- What are the workshop's theme and focus areas?
- How does it relate to this year's Chinese CHI theme?
- How will it connect relevant human–computer interaction and user experience communities?
- Objectives and Expected Outcomes
- What are the objectives, and how will you achieve them?
- What outcomes do you expect?
- Format
- What format will the workshop use, such as paper presentations, discussions, or demonstrations?
- What activities do you expect before and after the workshop?
- Participants
- How many participants do you expect? Provide a plan for attracting them and encouraging participation.
- If you have previously held workshops on a similar theme, provide their dates, locations, and participant numbers.
- Equipment Required
- Projector, whiteboard, microphone, sticky notes, paper, pens, and other supplies.

**Email your workshop proposal as a .doc or .pdf file to:**

**workshop.chichi@gmail.com**. Please also contact us with any questions.

## Call for Workshop

We invite you to submit proposals for workshops at Chinese CHI 2023. The workshops can focus on any area related to the field of human-computer interaction, industrial design, user experience and user interface design. In particular, workshops on challenging, emerging areas related to this year's conference topics are especially sought. The workshops might address basic research, applied research, HCI practice, HCI education, new methodologies, emerging application areas, or design innovations.

We highly encourage the enterprise research, design or engineering team in the company to organize a workshop. The enterprise organizers could provide their developer tools/design platform for participants, and testing the newly designed technologies in the workshop and collect feedback. It might also be a good place to brainstorm new applications and usage context for the newly developed HCI technologies.

We also highly encourage PhD students in the area of HCI and design, to consider organizing a workshop and leverage it as a place to share your research results or evaluate your design methods. It might be a good chance to find researchers who care about the same challenges, questions, and research agendas as you.

We particularly encourage proposals for

1. **Highly interactive workshops.** If you are working on developing new technologies or modalities for HCI, you could bring your interactive prototypes to the workshop, where the attendees could work together to think about the potential application areas, use cases, research topic or that suggest promising directions for future work.
1. **Highly collaborative workshops.** If you are working on developing new design methods, you could apply your design methods in organizing a co-creation workshop on relevant design topics. The outcomes of the workshops can be used to evaluate your methods.

## Quick Facts

The expected date of the workshops would be **November 13, 2023,** before the main track of the conference.

Workshops are scheduled for **half day** long and a typical workshop will have **8 to 15 participants.**

### Selection Process

The following is an outline of the submission and organization process of Workshop.

- Workshop organizers submit a workshop proposal (see below for content and format) to Chinese CHI.
- The submitted workshop proposals will be by reviewed by members of the program committee and peer experts.
- The Workshop Chairs will notify organizers whether their proposals have been accepted for the conference.
- Once a workshop is accepted, workshop organizers are responsible for publicizing the workshop and soliciting potential participants. Workshop organizers solicit participants for their workshop through a Call for Participation. Chinese CHI will also place a link to the workshop’s website on a page listing accepted workshops.
- Upon acceptance, the workshop organizers are required to create a sharable booklet, or a website with workshop specific information. The workshop organizers may decide to cap the number of attendees for the workshop.
- Organize the workshop with attendees.

## Preparing and Submitting Your Workshop Proposal

Workshop proposals should consist of the following components:

- Organizers
- Name: organizers’ full name
- Contact information: affiliations, job titles, postal addresses, e-mail addresses, URLs, and phone
- Brief biography: 100-200 words, focusing on the organizers’ expertise in the field and experience as workshop organizers
- Workshop content
- Title: workshop title
- Abstract: max 150 words describing the workshop (suitable for the conference’s website)
- Topics and motivation:
- What are the topics, themes, and areas of interest of the workshop?
- How is the workshop relevant to this year’s Chinese CHI topic?
- How does the workshop connect HCI, UX research communities?
- Goals and expected results:
- Explicitly state the goals of the workshop and how you intend to reach them.
- What are the expected results of the workshop?
- Format:
- What is the planned workshop format (paper presentations, working sessions, lightning talks, demonstrations, etc.)?
- What are the planned pre- and post-workshop activities?
- Participants:
- What is the expected number of submissions and participants? Provide a plan for attracting sufficient submissions and promoting attendance
- If applicable, please provide information from previous or related workshops. Have there been previous workshops on the same or a closely related topic? When, where and with how many participants?
- Required equipment:
- Overhead projector, PC projector, whiteboard, flip charts, microphone, etc.
