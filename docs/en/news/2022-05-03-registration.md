We invite participants to Huawei-sponsored summer boot camps, held this summer in a hybrid format at National University of Singapore, Zhejiang University, and City University of Hong Kong. Students worldwide are welcome. During an exciting week, participants can use the latest AR smart glasses, learn innovation with guidance from leading university faculty, and work in teams to turn imaginative ideas into tangible, interactive programs.

In the future, AI-assisted smart glasses may become everyday tools as common as smartphones. AR/VR glasses blend virtual objects with reality, sensing daily activities through cameras and other sensors. With AI, this emerging platform could transform how we connect with the world and one another.

Join expert researchers and up to fifty students from Singapore, Hangzhou, Hong Kong, and around the world, representing diverse academic backgrounds and levels. Explore the future of smart-glasses interaction and the core concepts shaping its innovation.

## Activities

Over six days, explore future interaction with smart glasses and related wearables through the following activities, and understand design requirements for lightweight next-generation glasses.

Learn core concepts in posture-compatible computing and mobile computing that frame an exciting vision for smart-glasses interaction (see the next section).

- Discuss fundamental research methods and APIs for the supporting platform.
- Participate in hands-on ideation with fellow students and instructors.
- Submit projects developed from your design concepts for evaluation in a hackathon. Selected work may be recommended for the student innovation competition finals at MobileHCI in late 2022 or for other awards.

## A Vision for Smart Glasses

Develop your own creative vision for smart-glasses interaction. Here we share existing visions currently being researched and implemented.

- Human-centered AI assistance, or another me: imagine an AI assistant living in your glasses. Through natural language it learns your goals and preferences and works with you to develop and execute solutions to everyday tasks. It senses your experience from a first-person perspective, understands the environment, and offers useful prompts. It connects with other assistants to tackle complex tasks requiring teams. The assistant thus becomes another me, extending personal thought and perception. In the near future, everyone may have such an assistant for daily life and work.
- Posture-compatible computing—human–computer interaction aligned with natural bodily activity—aims to provide seamless, timely, intelligent support for everyday activities. Using head and hand components, it supports interaction compatible with natural movement.

Rather than replacing physical space with virtual reality, posture-compatible computing augments and connects physical and virtual worlds through AR, strengthening human connections with the digital–physical environment.

## Hardware Platform

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2022-05-03-registration-02.jpg)

In-person participants will receive Huawei smart glasses during the hands-on camp. Remote students will form teams with on-site participants to complete projects together.

Huawei Smart Glasses Features Include:

- Wireless connection to existing devices such as smartphones, tablets, smartwatches, and PCs.
- Sensors include microphones for voice and ambient sound, touch sensors for gestures, and activity sensing such as neck posture. Beamforming speakers direct high-quality sound privately toward your ears.

Other AR glasses, including Nreal, will also be available for testing and comparison.

How can a simple wearable connect you to the digital world while keeping you grounded in the physical world? Join us to find out.
