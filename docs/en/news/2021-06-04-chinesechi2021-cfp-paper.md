Chinese CHI 2021, organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), is now accepting papers! We warmly welcome submissions and participation. Please note the deadlines:

- **Full Papers Deadline:**
- **July 15, 2021, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth**
- **Late-Breaking Work Deadline:**
- **August 10, 2021, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth**

**This year's Chinese CHI theme is Identity, Value, and Trust against a backdrop of technological progress.** Connecting Chinese HCI scholars, academia, and industry worldwide, Chinese CHI has held eight successful editions since 2012 in Paris, Toronto, Seoul, Silicon Valley, Guangzhou, Montreal, Xiamen, and online. **Papers from previous conferences are included in the ACM Digital Library.**

The conference accepts Full Papers and Late-Breaking Work. **Submissions must be in English.**

**All submissions must use the ACM Master Article Template.**

Download the Template:

**[https://www.acm.org/publications/taps/word-template-workflow](https://www.acm.org/publications/taps/word-template-workflow)**

**Full papers must not exceed 20 pages, and Late-Breaking Work papers eight pages, excluding references.**

**All manuscripts must be submitted online through EasyChair.**

Link:

**[https://easychair.org/account/signin?l=mcIv4hzTjQIXERTVIdRiV4#](https://easychair.org/account/signin?l=mcIv4hzTjQIXERTVIdRiV4#)**

Both tracks use blinded peer review organized by experienced human–computer interaction scholars. **For details and formatting requirements, consult the conference website's call for papers (http://chchi2021.icachi.org/).**

## Topics Include, but Are Not Limited To:

- User Experience and Usability
- Specific Application Areas
- Health, Accessibility and Aging
- Privacy, Security and Visualization
- Interaction Beyond the Individual
- Games and Play
- Design
- Interaction Techniques, Devices and Modalities
- Understanding People: Theory, Concepts, Methods
- Engineering Interactive Systems and Technologies
- Technology Use or Development in the Chinese Context
- Understanding Chinese Users
- Interaction Techniques Tailored to Chinese Users
- Community Engagement in China

Acceptance Notification Dates:

- Full Papers notification: September 1, 2021
- Late-Breaking Work notification: September 10, 2021

**The conference will take place online on October 16–17, 2021, with free public livestreams.** Supported by academic and industry partners, it features senior scholars from internationally renowned universities and companies as speakers and panelists. Authors of accepted papers will present online or through video introductions, offering participants opportunities to exchange ideas with human–computer interaction researchers worldwide. **General attendance is free throughout. At least one author of each accepted, peer-reviewed paper must pay the US$100 registration fee.**
