To All Members of International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction:

At the New Year, we extend sincere greetings and best wishes to all directors and members of International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI)!

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2025-01-14-article-pxnxaetc-02.jpg)

ICACHI Lunar New Year Greeting Image

Since 2012, ICACHI has grown into an important global platform for academic and industrial exchange in the Chinese HCI community. It brings together international expertise to inspire human–computer synergy, opens commercialization opportunities for researchers, and connects educational and academic resources across borders to support members sustainably. Twelve successful International Chinese human–computer interaction Conferences (Chinese CHI) have taken the association to Paris, Toronto, Seoul, Silicon Valley, Guangzhou, Montreal, Xiamen, and Bali. The conference first came to Shenzhen in 2024. This internationally recognized city of innovation and design offered fertile ground for academic–industry collaboration and demonstrated China's global influence in human–computer interaction and technological innovation.

**The 12th International Chinese human–computer interaction Conference** (Chinese CHI 2024) was held successfully at Southern University of Science and Technology in Shenzhen on November 22–25. It attracted over sixty experts and industry leaders from China, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, the Netherlands, the United States, Canada, and elsewhere, with more than 500 participants. Of 145 paper submissions, 48 were accepted. The International Workshop on Human-Engaged Computing (IWHEC) reconsidered human–computer relationships, bringing together experts in human–computer interaction, neuroscience, humanities, philosophy, social sciences, design, computer science, and AI. It launched **Human-Engaged Computing**, co-authored by **Xiangshi Ren, Zhiyong Fu, Xiaojuan Ma, Jianmin Wang, Huatong Sun, Xiaojun Yuan, and Chen Wang**. This new paradigm calls for attention to technology's dual effects and a sustainable human future. Thematic forums, keynotes, digital art exhibitions, competitions, and other activities showcased the latest human–computer interaction research and practice.

The International Cooperation Department of China Association for Science and Technology warmly congratulated the organizers, noting that the worldwide growth of next-generation AI is energizing economic and social development and profoundly changing how people live and work. human–computer interaction is increasingly important as a bridge between people and intelligent systems. Through its international platform, ICACHI promotes dialogue and cooperation in human–computer interaction between China and other countries and contributes to global technological innovation and development.

In 2024, the association recognized outstanding academic and industry contributions. **Yingqing Xu, Shengdong Zhao, Feng Tian, Jun Hu, and Huamin Qu** received the Leadership Award, recognizing the award recipients' lasting influence on human–computer interaction. **Mingming Fan, Haipeng Mi, Dakuo Wang, Jian Zhao, and Xianghua Ding** received the Rising Talent Award for the award recipients' innovation in human–computer interaction. **Pengcheng An, Weiwei Zhang, Guanhong Liu, Zhihao Yao, and Chen Wang** received the Emerging Star Award for the award recipients' academic potential and achievements. **Zhiyong Fu, Huawei Tu, Xiaojuan Ma, Haipeng Mi, and Chen Wang** received the Outstanding Service Award for sustained service and generosity. **Qing Xia, Boyu Gao, Yuxin Xu, Pengcheng An, and Lin Zhu** received the Distinguished Contribution Award, thanking these award recipients for exceptional work. **Can Liu, Xinyi Fu, Jiawei Li, Zijun Mai, and Yun Wang** received the Special Contribution Award for their distinctive dedication.

ICACHI continues refining its organization and now has five specialized committees to provide more targeted services and support for academia and industry:

- **Strategic Development Committee:** Led by Vice President Junfeng Yao, it defines long-term strategic goals and expands international cooperation networks.
- **Academic Activities Committee:** Led by Vice President Dakuo Wang, it develops academic exchanges, talent development, and community-building activities.
- **Specialized Conferences Committee:** Led by Vice President Haipeng Mi, it coordinates planning and delivery of the annual international conference.
- **Industry Committee:** Led by Vice President Jian Zhao, it connects academic research with industry and promotes practical applications of human–computer interaction technologies.
- **Academic Journals and Textbooks Committee:** Led by Vice President Jun Hu, it advances the publication and dissemination of high-quality journals and textbooks.

Under Vice President and Secretary-General Huawei Tu, the Secretariat improves member services and platform capabilities, providing stronger support and resource integration. The Young Scholars Committee expands the association's influence among younger human–computer interaction scholars, primarily students and early-career faculty, contributing to academic communication and the development of human–computer interaction.

**Looking Ahead to 2025, ICACHI Will Focus on:**

1. Preparing the 13th International Chinese human–computer interaction Conference (Chinese CHI 2025 and IWHEC 2025), achieving new international and interdisciplinary connections and expanding academic–industry cooperation; and hosting China Night 2025 to deepen exchanges across the global Chinese HCI community.
1. Strengthening the five committees' coordination to advance academic frontiers, translate research into applications, and increase social value.
1. Enhancing ICACHI's role as an international academic bridge and improving its cooperation network, particularly the overseas headquarters and state offices and their functions; establishing a representative office in China with support from China Association for Science and Technology.
1. The Secretariat will strengthen member services, resource integration, and platform capabilities, creating broader development and collaboration opportunities.

Thank you to every member for your support and contributions over the past year. We look forward to working together in 2025 toward an even brighter future! Wishing you success, fulfillment, and good health in the New Year!
