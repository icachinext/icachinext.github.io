## Conference Overview

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), the International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing **(ICHEC 2026)** will be hosted by **City University of Macau** and held **November 23–26, 2026, at Wynn Macau in Macau, China.** The themes are Inclusivity, Experience, and Innovation.

## Scope and Formats

We encourage proposals **addressing contemporary and emerging HCI topics** through structured discussion, making, critique, or community building. **Topics include, but are not limited to:**

- Emerging interaction paradigms and user experience design
- Emerging HCI methods and design practices
- Emerging research communities, interdisciplinary HCI topics, and regional or cultural perspectives
- Responsible AI, accessibility, health, education, creative tools, and social impact

## Submission Guidelines

Format:

**Up to three pages**, excluding references

Template:

Follow the provided proposal template and **write in English**

Submission:

Submit a PDF proposal through Google Forms, **completed in English**

[https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfqd0YCnKP056a-Gmv8luVLSnO6_CL56LuL3aFGpODmlSIgSA/viewform?usp=publish-editor](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfqd0YCnKP056a-Gmv8luVLSnO6_CL56LuL3aFGpODmlSIgSA/viewform?usp=publish-editor)

## Important Dates

- **Proposal submission deadline:** September 18, 2026
- **Acceptance notification:** September 25, 2026
- **Call for participants released:** October 1, 2026
- **Final materials due:** November 20, 2026

* All deadlines are 23:59 **AOE**

## What We Provide

- A dedicated half-day **(three-hour)** or full-day **(2 × three-hour)** slot
- Standard meeting facilities, including **a projector, microphones,** and **Wi-Fi**
- A conference-promoted workshop webpage and **inclusion in the proceedings**
- Opportunities to engage with active local and regional HCI communities
- Networking opportunities with international participants

## Participation Requirements

- Workshop **organizers** and **participants** must **register for both the workshop and main conference;** no additional workshop fee is charged, as it is included in conference registration
- Each workshop requires **at least ten on-site participants**
- Preference will be given to workshops with strong international appeal
