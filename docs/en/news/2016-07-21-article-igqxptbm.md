At researchers' request, the IxAP 2016 full paper deadline has been extended to August 20. Other deadlines will be adjusted accordingly; the conference dates remain unchanged. We welcome submissions and participation.

Both developed and developing countries face population aging. By 2050, people over 65 are projected to comprise 27% of the population in developed countries and 15% in developing countries. Yet the rapid digital revolution has not adequately benefited older adults. Although their share of the population is increasing, mainstream HCI research remains focused on young people, gradually excluding older adults from the opportunities of the digital age.

**IxAP 2016** will take place on **October 21, 2016** in **Kochi, Japan**, focusing on coordinated development of **technologies for older adults based on human–computer interaction**. More than 100 world-class researchers will exchange ideas and advance HCI, with keynote presentations by Professors Frank Vetere of the University of Melbourne and Joanna McGrenere of the University of British Columbia.
