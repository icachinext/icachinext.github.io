The **Chinese CHI 2016** workshop will take place in San Jose, USA, on May 8, 2016. It now invites **posters and special track papers**, with a submission deadline of February 22, 2016.

The poster track welcomes ongoing or incomplete research. Students are especially encouraged to submit and attend. Posters may be written in English or Chinese.

The **Special Track on AR/VR Visualization** is a new component of this year's workshop, providing a platform for researchers in augmented reality, virtual reality, and information visualization. Papers may be recommended for a special issue of Elsevier's **Journal of Visual Language and Computing (JVLC)**.

We warmly welcome submissions and participation.

[http://chchi2016.icachi.org/](http://chchi2016.icachi.org/)

For questions, please contact: chinesechi2016@easychair.org

Thank you very much for supporting Chinese CHI 2016!

Best wishes!
