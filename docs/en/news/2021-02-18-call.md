## About Us

International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI) is preparing to establish a Young Scholars Committee to expand its influence among young human–computer interaction scholars, primarily students and early-career faculty, and contribute to human–computer interaction communication and development. Serving young ICACHI members, the committee will create better opportunities for exchange and growth while developing future association talent. We warmly invite young Chinese scholars in China and overseas to join.

## ICACHI Young Scholars Committee Members

Committee members will come from human–computer interaction and related laboratories and help connect and grow membership at their institutions. Using the membership network, the committee will organize regular online and in-person workshops and other activities, share resources and research with young scholars and doctoral students worldwide, and recognize outstanding members periodically.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-02-18-call-02.jpg)

**What Do Young Scholars Committee Members Do?**

Externally, members will plan, organize, and support academic and related activities, connecting young students and faculty with senior researchers for dialogue and collaboration, strengthening professional and academic abilities, publicizing association news, and supporting other ICACHI events. Internally, they will understand young members' needs, circumstances, and ideas, communicate with the board and secretariat, and participate in development planning.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-02-18-call-03.jpg)

## Requirements for Joining the ICACHI Young Scholars Committee

1. Agree to the association's constitution ([http://www.icachi.org/col.jsp?id=104](http://www.icachi.org/col.jsp?id=104)）；

2. Be an early-career scholar within five years of the PhD or a doctoral student in human–computer interaction or related fields such as computer science, psychology, or design. Exceptional master's students and industry professionals may also be considered;

3. Have a strong commitment to advancing human–computer interaction in China and internationally;

4. Be dedicated to innovation in interaction design;

5. Have strong communication, organizational, and coordination skills and a sense of responsibility;

6. Have sufficient time to serve the association.

## What Committee Membership Offers

1. Successful applicants will exchange ideas and grow alongside outstanding board scholars and students worldwide, participate in association planning, and advance human–computer interaction;

2. Priority access to ICACHI's online and in-person activities;

3. Opportunities to engage with international frontier human–computer interaction networks, learn about emerging developments, and build academic collaborations and exchanges.

4. Use association resources to organize activities supporting your own and other young scholars' academic and professional development.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-02-18-call-04.jpg)

## How to Register

**Please send a personal statement, including a self-introduction, anticipated contributions to the association, and a CV, to youth@icachi.org to apply.**

We warmly invite you to join the ICACHI Young Scholars Committee as a founding member and help strengthen the association and the human–computer interaction community!
