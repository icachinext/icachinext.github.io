## 01 Introduction

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-05-13-chinesechi2017-02.png)

International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction was founded on May 10, 2012, in Austin, USA. Chinese CHI, ICACHI's flagship conference, will be held in China for the first time this year. HCI stands for Human–Computer Interaction and explores interactions between users and machines. Chinese CHI 2017 will provide Chinese HCI researchers worldwide with an opportunity to exchange ideas across industry and academia. We warmly invite researchers and practitioners from around the world to participate, learn from one another, and contribute to this event.

Dates:

**June 8–9, 2017, in Guangzhou, China.**

## 02 About the Host

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-05-13-chinesechi2017-03.jpg)

The conference organizer, Guangdong University of Technology, is a key provincial university emphasizing engineering alongside coordinated development in science, economics, management, humanities, law, and art, and is part of Guangdong's high-level university development initiative. The conference host, **the School of Art and Design at Guangdong University of Technology**, was established in 2004 and is a member of Cumulus, the international association of universities and colleges of art, design, and media. It offers 11 programs, including industrial design, product design, and digital media technology. The school prioritizes international collaboration and maintains stable exchange and cooperation partnerships with many leading universities outside mainland China.
