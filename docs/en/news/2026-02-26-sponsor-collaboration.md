## China Night 2026

Call for Partners and Sponsors

## About ICACHI

International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI) was founded in 2012 during ACM CHI. It is the first international professional academic organization in human–computer interaction initiated by Chinese scholars worldwide.

The association brings together experts in human–computer interaction, AI, user experience, media, and design from eighteen countries and regions, promoting cross-regional academic cooperation and research and practice in technology for good.

## About China Night

China Night is ICACHI's signature annual event during ACM CHI. It provides Chinese scholars worldwide with a platform for cross-cultural exchange and cooperation and builds a bridge for deeper dialogue between academia and industry.

At CHI 2025 in Yokohama, Japan, China Night attracted nearly 700 researchers from many countries and regions, becoming one of the conference's most influential Chinese scholarly community events.

CHI 2026 will take place in Barcelona, Spain, April 13–17, 2026. China Night will be held alongside it, strengthening connections and cooperation across the global Chinese human–computer interaction community. We invite universities, research institutes, companies, and related organizations to join China Night 2026 and expand the Chinese HCI community's international influence.

## We Are Recruiting…

We invite universities, research institutes, companies, and related organizations to help create China Night 2026 as an internationally influential Chinese HCI community event.

## Co-Organizers and Operations Team

We invite Chinese scholars and research teams worldwide to help plan and deliver China Night, including event coordination, venue arrangements, volunteer organization, on-site operations, publicity, and corporate partnerships.

## 🎯 What You Will Gain:

- Practical experience organizing activities for the global Chinese human–computer interaction scholarly community
- Opportunities to collaborate closely with leading international scholars and early-career researchers
- Experience planning and delivering events at a major international conference
- Official co-organizer recognition and public acknowledgment

## Sponsors

China Night offers universities and companies a focused setting for international academic exchange and brand presentation. Representatives from Huawei, Northeastern University, and Nanjing University, among others, have shared frontier research at past events, attracting wide attention and positive feedback.

## 🎯 What You Will Gain:

- Brand exposure within CHI's leading international academic setting
- Opportunities for on-site remarks or technical presentations
- Product or research demonstrations
- Support for connecting with top talent and recruitment publicity
- Promotion through official WeChat and online platforms
- Opportunities to build partnerships with international universities and research institutes

**See You at CHI 2026 in Barcelona!**

CHI 2026 will be held in Barcelona, Spain. We look forward to working with you to build an internationally influential Chinese HCI community event, strengthen exchanges among Chinese human–computer interaction professionals worldwide, and deepen academic–industry integration.
