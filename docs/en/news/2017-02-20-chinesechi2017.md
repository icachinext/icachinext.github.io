We invite you to submit preliminary work and work-in-progress relevant to the area of Chinese HCI to our Poster session. These may include ground-breaking work that has not been fully implemented or empirically validated, early student research and other work that are better delivered in an interactive format. Poster presenters will present their work during the Poster Session assigned by the conference committee.

## Schedule

- Submission deadline: March 10, 2017
- Review deadline: March 21, 2017
- Results announced: March 24, 2017

## Important Dates

- Submission Due Mar 10, 2017 PDT
- Review Due Mar 21, 2017 PDT
- Poster Notification Mar 24, 2017 PDT

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-02-20-chinesechi2017-03.jpg)

## Submission Outline

- Format
- Authors may submit an extended abstract of up to four pages, including text and images, using the ACM SIGCHI Extended Abstract template and PDF format.
- Submit the abstract together with a draft poster. The final submission includes both abstract and poster in electronic form through: [https://www.easychair.org/conferences/?conf=chinesechi2017](https://www.easychair.org/conferences/?conf=chinesechi2017)
- Language
- Chinese CHI 2017 accepts submissions in Chinese or English.
- Attendance
- At least one author of each accepted work must register for the conference.

## Submission Guidelines

- Format.
Authors should submit an extended abstract of the poster, including texts and images, that is no more than four pages. The abstract has to be written in the ACM SIGCHI Extended Abstract format (see below for templates). They must be submitted in PDF file format.
- Authors should also submit a letter page size poster draft to be reviewed together with the abstract.
- All work – both abstract and poster – must be submitted electronically via the [https://www.easychair.org/conferences/?conf=chinesechi2017](https://www.easychair.org/conferences/?conf=chinesechi2017) (link is external).
- Language.
Chinese CHI 2017 accepts poster submissions in Chinese or English.
- Attendance.
One author of each accepted submission must register for the conference before the early registration deadline and present the poster.
