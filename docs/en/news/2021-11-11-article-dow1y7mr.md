## ICACHI Young Scholars Committee

## Chuang Yu

Chuang Yu graduated from IP Paris's ASR Robotics Laboratory in 2021 and is now a postdoctoral researcher in the University of Manchester's Cognitive Robotics Laboratory, supported by UKRI's Trustworthy Autonomous Systems—Node on Trust project (https://trust.tas.ac.uk/home). Research interests include cognitive robotics frameworks, robot learning, human-centered intelligent robots, and perception and generation of human and robot behavior.

## Intelligent Human–Robot Collaboration Group, ASR Robotics Laboratory

The group is led by Professor Adriana Tapus ([https://perso.ensta-paris.fr/~tapus/eng/](https://perso.ensta-paris.fr/~tapus/eng/)）。). **It researches social robots with learning and cognitive abilities, supporting natural, trustworthy human–robot interaction through verbal, nonverbal, and paralinguistic behavior. By expressing personality, emotion, and cognition, robots aim to assist people with physical or cognitive disabilities, older adults living alone, and children with autism.** Professor Tapus is dean of the doctoral school at IP Paris. Her interests include human–robot interaction, robot learning, and intelligent assistive robotics, and she has been named among the world's 25 leading women in robotics.

Professor Adriana Tapus and the Meka Robot

**The group has led or participated in major French National Research Agency and EU projects, including Horizon 2020's ENRICHME and the ROMEO robot project. It collaborates with leading research organizations such as USC, EPFL, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, CNRS, and CEA, and industrial R&D groups. Its advanced interaction platforms include Meka, Pepper, NAO, and TIAGo robots, together with substantial GPU computing resources.**
