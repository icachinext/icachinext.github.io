## Chinese CHI 2022 Art Gallery

The conference's Digital Art Exhibition **received 246 works** from recommendations of outstanding entries in the China Creative and National Digital Art Design Competition and the Future Designer Competition, direct Chinese CHI 2022 exhibition submissions, and Chinese CHI 2022 Student Competition submissions.

**We especially thank the organizing committees of the China Creative and National Digital Art Design Competition and the Future Designer Competition for their strong support!**

- Authors of shortlisted works must first **register for the conference, as registration is required to exhibit.** Exhibited works will be archived in the Chinese CHI 2022 Digital Art Exhibition yearbook, and authors will receive an ICACHI exhibition award certificate.

## Submit Information

- The **online exhibition** and **physical exhibition** will run concurrently with rotating displays.
- **(1) Online Exhibition Material Submission Guide:**
1. Complete the artist information in **Attachment 1: Soul Scene—Artist Information Form** and attach an ID-style portrait. Each author should submit a separate form.
1. Complete **Attachment 2: Soul Scene—Digital Collectible Artwork Information** and provide all attachments specified in the form.
1. Complete **Attachment 4: Consolidated Author Information.**
- **(2) Physical Exhibition Material Submission Guide:**
1. **Display panel:** Use **Attachment 3, the design template**, and submit both AI and exported JPG files. The JPG must not exceed 10 MB. Include:
1. - Basic information about the work
1. - Your perspective on the design challenge
1. - A brief description of the solution
1. - A clear presentation of the proposed solution's key information
1. Complete **Attachment 4: Consolidated Author Information**
1. **If the work includes a video, provide it too:** teams should submit an MP4 video of no more than ten minutes and 200 MB.
1. For physical installations, an author representative should come to the first- and second-floor exhibition halls of the School of Art and Design, Guangdong University of Technology, between October 13 and 18, 2022. Address: first- and second-floor exhibition halls, School of Art and Design, Guangdong University of Technology, 729 Dongfeng East Road, Yuexiu District, Guangzhou 510090. Physical works may be shipped, but authors must collect them themselves; organizers cannot receive or store shipments. Authors exhibiting installations should make their own installation arrangements.
- **Attachment download link:**

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2022-10-02-chinesechi2022-art-05.png)

- **Compress the materials required under (1) and (2) into a .ZIP file named Online and Physical Exhibition + Work Title + Lead Contact Name + Phone Number, and email it to the following address before October 7, 2022, at 23:59:**
- **artgallery_chichi@163.com**

## Copyright Notice

- **The conference has the right to display and publicize all shortlisted and award-winning works for noncommercial purposes.** All displays and publicity will credit the authors as named in the submission.
- **If you do not consent to display or publicity of your work, notify the committee before October 7, 2022, at 23:59:**
- **artgallery_chichi@163.com**
- **Alternatively, scan the QR code to join the group and contact the committee on WeChat.** The committee will withdraw the work's exhibition eligibility and award eligibility.
- For all shortlisted and award-winning works, **the authors bear responsibility for any copyright disputes.**
