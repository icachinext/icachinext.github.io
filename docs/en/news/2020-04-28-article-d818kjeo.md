**What Is human–computer interaction?**

human–computer interaction (Human-Computer Interaction, HCI) is, as its name suggests, the discipline connecting people with technology. human–computer interaction concerns machines, but even more fundamentally concerns people, making it closely connected to computer science and psychology. human–computer interaction also attracts contributions from cognitive science, industrial design, electrical and electronic engineering, mechanical engineering, anthropology, cultural studies, sociology, digital media, communication, and philosophy. It is a broad interdisciplinary field combining theory and practice.

## Background of human–computer interaction

Since modern computers emerged in the 1940s, scientists have considered how people relate to computers and how their application and wider use could improve social efficiency and organization. Before personal computers existed, pioneers including Douglas Carl Engelbart envisioned future computing and a computerized society. Their ideas produced concepts and tools such as the mouse, hypertext, and graphical interfaces, influencing computing and society and opening the way for human–computer interaction.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2020-04-28-article-d818kjeo-02.jpg)

Douglas Carl Engelbart (image source: Internet)

In 1984, consumer electronics exemplified by Apple's Macintosh computer with its graphical interface reached the market. Commercial success boosted human–computer interaction researchers' confidence and showed AI scientists tangible results from combining computing with usability. Growing human–computer interaction research across platforms subsequently made major contributions to innovation and transformation in information technology and consumer electronics, while human–computer interaction gradually matured.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2020-04-28-article-d818kjeo-03.jpg)

1984 Apple Macintosh (image source: Internet)

human–computer interaction is a frontier technology field closely connected to social life. Familiar technologies including the mouse, user interfaces, multitouch, virtual reality, and IoT owe much to human–computer interaction design and research. Historically, major computing transformations—including remote collaboration, personal computers, and mobile devices—also grew from rethinking human–computer interaction paradigms. From the establishment of its academic community in 1980 to its present position as one of the computing association's most influential communities, human–computer interaction has developed into a force that, alongside AI and other ideas and technologies, will shape the future.

## International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction

On June 28, 2019, Chinese CHI 2019, the International Chinese human–computer interaction Conference, opened at Xiamen University's Science and Art Center, alongside the release of China's first blue book on human–computer interaction development.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2020-04-28-article-d818kjeo-05.png)

Chinese CHI 2019 poster (image source: ICACHI)

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2020-04-28-article-d818kjeo-06.png)

Association logo (image source: ICACHI)

China's human–computer interaction research has advanced rapidly in recent years, driven by universities, businesses, and funding, with impressive results. Gaps remain relative to countries and regions with more established human–computer interaction communities, particularly in considering the broader technological landscape and proposing interaction concepts. Nevertheless, China's human–computer interaction potential is substantial: student numbers, educational capacity, market scale, and policy support all indicate a promising future for human–computer interaction.

As the first global professional scientific association of overseas Chinese scholars, ICACHI has accumulated academic achievements and expanded the influence of both the association and Chinese CHI over its eight-year history. By preparing and publishing this blue book, it hopes to contribute its voice and efforts to the future development of human–computer interaction in China.

## Blue Book on human–computer interaction Development

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2020-04-28-article-d818kjeo-07.png)

Blue book cover (image source: ICACHI)

The blue book also outlines the association's roadmap for the next three to five years in academia, education, and industry:

- **human–computer interaction expert think tanks and exchange mechanisms**
- **Integrating resources to establish a Chinese human–computer interaction society**
- **Developing and building human–computer interaction course clusters**
- **Collaborative research mechanisms for human–computer interaction laboratories**
- **Training interdisciplinary, innovative human–computer interaction talent**
- **Commercialization and incubation mechanisms for human–computer interaction research**

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2020-04-28-article-d818kjeo-08.png)

Call to action (image source: ICACHI)

Through its platform, ICACHI hopes to bring together more researchers in China and overseas, support human–computer interaction schools and departments and research commercialization in Chinese universities, and connect industry effectively with talent and projects.

The preparation and publication of this blue book also owe much to China Association for Science and Technology's enduring support and encouragement of ICACHI. We thank the board members who contributed and the experts in China and abroad who reviewed it. Through this blue book, we call on research and educational institutions, corporate R&D, product and design departments, and government organizations to recognize the essential nature and potential of human–computer interaction as both science and a practical tool. We hope to advance China's human–computer interaction education and industry, bring its human–computer interaction research to the international forefront, and enable Chinese human–computer interaction research and commercial and consumer products to improve human life and the future.

International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction
