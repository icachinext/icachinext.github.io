Dear Members,

**As the Lunar New Year approaches, we extend sincere greetings to ICACHI members on behalf of the International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction Board of Directors!**

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-02-11-membership-01.png)

**We successfully held the first Chinese CHI international forum in France in 2013.** ICACHI formally registered as a nonprofit professional association in Toronto in 2014. **That year's second Chinese CHI conference in Toronto had its proceedings included in the ACM Digital Library,** making papers discoverable in major research indexes. **In 2015 and 2016, Chinese CHI cooperated with ACM SIGCHI and became an official part of CHI's program while preserving its own identity.** Chinese CHI first came to China in 2017 at Guangdong University of Technology, and its 2018 edition was successfully held in Montreal. **Chinese CHI 2019Xiamen University took place with ** the theme Happiness, Culture, and Innovation. **Its opening marked the release of China's first Blue Book on human–computer interaction Development in the history of human–computer interaction,** offering recommendations for human–computer interaction's development. **Chinese CHI has become ICACHI's international flagship conference, growing from just over 100 participants to 700 in 2019.**

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-02-11-membership-02.jpg)

**In 2020,** we experienced a once-in-a-century pandemic and profound global changes. Chinese human–computer interaction scholars, visiting researchers, and international students worldwide faced unprecedented challenges. With the pandemic preventing international travel and academic exchange, the association sought new opportunities amid the crisis, **maintaining closer connections with members through online platforms and livestreams.**

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-02-11-membership-03.png)

**In early 2020,** as society and the global economy slowly restarted after the pandemic's pause, we sought to confront humanity's future challenges and reconsider technology's role. We envisioned more forward-looking, inclusive human–computer interaction models, applications and solutions to unknown problems, reflection on progress, and stronger crisis response. **The association therefore proposed Human-Engaged Computing and Technology for Good, calling for joint scientific and technological efforts against the pandemic** and protection of our shared home. We launched four related online forums, including Chinese CHI 2020, featuring talks and paper presentations connecting members worldwide and reaching more students and practitioners. **The four forums accumulated over 10,000 views and connected with a broad community of students and scholars in China and overseas.**

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-02-11-membership-04.jpg)

**After eight years, the association's members span Europe, the Americas, Asia, and Australia.** Though geographically distant, we remain closely connected. Our recent election attracted more board candidates than previous terms, demonstrating growing internal and external influence. **The fifth Board of Directors has now begun its term,** with enthusiastic members committed to our founding mission. Drawing on inclusive global expertise, we will offer visionary ideas for human–computer synergy, connect international companies' future needs and R&D capabilities, create commercialization opportunities, and link interdisciplinary academic and educational resources worldwide to support members' sustained development. Through a shared aspiration for a desirable human future, we will build a global human–computer interaction academic community. **We believe that, through the efforts of the board and all members, human–computer interaction has an even brighter future!**

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-02-11-membership-05.png)

To seize new opportunities, the association has established committees for journals and textbooks, academic activities, specialized conferences, and strategic development, strengthened secretariat services, and formed a youth committee for researchers and students. These provide support across disciplines and levels. We welcome new members to advance the association together. **Upcoming events include the jointly organized IWHEC 2021 on March 19–20** and Humanity, Technology, and the Future: Reflections on the Post-AI Era on March 21. **Further academic events, including Chinese CHI 2021, will follow in the second half of the year. Please follow conference and event updates on our website ([http://www.icachi.org/](http://www.icachi.org/)）。**

**Wishing you and your family health, happiness, and fulfillment in the Year of the Ox! We look forward to meeting again.**
