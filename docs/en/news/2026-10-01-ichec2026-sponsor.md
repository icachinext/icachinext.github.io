## Conference Overview

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), the International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing **(ICHEC 2026)** will be hosted by **City University of Macau** and held **November 23–26, 2026, at Wynn Palace in Macau, China.** The themes are Inclusivity, Experience, and Innovation.

We invite **universities, research institutes, and companies** and **related organizations** to become **ICHEC 2026 sponsorship partners** and help build an internationally influential HCI conference.

Sponsors will connect with human–computer interaction scholars, students, practitioners, and industry representatives worldwide, **increase their visibility in the HCI community, and promote deeper dialogue and cooperation between academic research and industrial practice.**
