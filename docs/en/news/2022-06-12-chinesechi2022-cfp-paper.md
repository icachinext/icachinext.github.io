## human–computer interaction Conference: Call for Papers

Chinese CHI 2022, organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), **will take place in Guangzhou, China, on October 22–23, 2022, in a hybrid online and in-person format.** Its theme is **Inclusion, Resilience, and New Normal.**

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), this academic conference has become an important meeting point for Chinese HCI scholars, academia, and industry worldwide. Since 2012, Chinese CHI has been successfully held in Paris, Toronto, Seoul, Silicon Valley, Guangzhou, Montreal, and Xiamen. **Papers from previous conferences are included in the ACM Digital Library and submitted for EI indexing.**

As the global pandemic continues, humanity again stands at a historical crossroads, while economic globalization shows renewed resilience. In response to this evolving global challenge, **this conference seeks to explore the value, significance, and social contribution of human–computer interaction in the post-pandemic era.**

## Submission Information

Including, but not limited to:

- User Experience and Usability
- Specific Application Areas
- Health, Accessibility and Aging
- Privacy, Security and Visualization
- Interaction Beyond the Individual
- Games and Play
- Design
- Interaction Techniques, Devices and Modalities
- Understanding People: Theory, Concepts, Methods
- Engineering Interactive Systems and Technologies
- Emergency Management

The **conference also welcomes work on human–computer interaction in Chinese cultural contexts,** including, but not limited to:

- Technology Use or Development in the Chinese Contexy
- Understanding Chinese User
- Interaction Techniques Tailored to Chinese Users
- Community Engagement in China

## Submission Information

### Deadlines

Submissions are welcome from the publication of this call. There are two tracks: full Papers and Late-Breaking Work, with the following deadlines:

- **Full Papers deadlines:**
- Abstracts: June 20, 2022, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth
- Full papers: July 31, 2022, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth
- **Late-Breaking Work deadline:**
- August 20, 2022, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth

### Submission Requirements

1. This conference **accepts submissions in English only.**
1. **Use the ACM Master Article template.**
1. Download the template:
1. [https://www.acm.org/publications/taps/word-template-workflow](https://www.acm.org/publications/taps/word-template-workflow)
1. Length limits: **full papers, up to 20 pages** excluding references; **Late-Breaking Work, up to 8 pages** excluding references.
1. Papers submitted to Chinese CHI 2022 must be **previously unpublished and must not simultaneously be under review at another conference or journal.**
1. Authors must **observe academic integrity.** The committee will uphold rigorous quality standards and investigate misconduct. Authors bear responsibility for any violations, and the association reserves the right to hold them accountable.

### Submission

Submit manuscripts online through the EasyChair conference system:

[https://easychair.org/my/conference?conf=chinesechi2022](https://easychair.org/my/conference?conf=chinesechi2022)

For detailed requirements and formatting information, see the call for papers on the conference website:

[http://chchi2022.icachi.org/index.html](http://chchi2022.icachi.org/index.html)

### Acceptance

Both full papers and Late-Breaking Work undergo blinded peer review by experienced human–computer interaction researchers.

Acceptance Notification Dates:

- Full papers: September 10, 2022
- Late-Breaking Work: September 20, 2022

Supported by academic and industry partners, the conference features talks and workshops led by senior scholars from internationally renowned institutions and companies. Accepted papers will be presented as talks or video introductions, giving participants opportunities to exchange ideas with human–computer interaction scholars worldwide. **At least one author of each accepted, peer-reviewed paper must pay the conference registration fee.** For registration details, visit chchi2022.icachi.org.

**We warmly welcome your submissions!**
