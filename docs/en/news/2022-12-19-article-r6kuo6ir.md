## I. Competition Overview

**The Huawei Smart Mobility Innovation Competition** is an innovative design competition for intelligent devices inside and outside vehicles, organized by Huawei Technologies and ICACHI and hosted by Guangdong University of Technology. **Huawei's vision is to integrate software and hardware across these devices to deliver safe, comfortable, efficient driving, audiovisual entertainment, and mobile working experiences.** We invite talented university participants to **explore innovations connecting smart devices and mobility and build a better future of intelligent travel together**.

## II. Competition Challenge Themes

**1. Intelligent Driving:** Connect phones, vehicles, and more smart devices to provide drivers with safer, more convenient, and more efficient driving experiences.

**2. The Third Living Space:** Integrate more smart devices into vehicles to provide comfortable, immersive audiovisual entertainment or efficient and comfortable mobile working experiences.

**3. All-Scenario Connectivity:** Connect smart devices inside and outside vehicles, placing users at the center and enriching intelligent travel experiences.

## III. Awards

One first prize: RMB 50,000

Two second prizes: RMB 20,000 each

Three third prizes: RMB 10,000 each

## IV. Competition Schedule

1) Preliminary registration and submission: December 20, 2022–February 12, 2023

2) Finalist announcement: February 19, 2023

3) Software and hardware resources distributed: February 19, 2023

4) Final round: February 19–April 10, 2023

5) Final assessment and awards: April 10–30, 2023
