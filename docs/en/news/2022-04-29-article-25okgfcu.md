## Joseph A. Konstan

## University of Minnesota

Joseph A.Konstan (https://konstan.umn.edu) is Distinguished McKnight University Professor and Distinguished University Teaching Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Minnesota where he also serves as Associate Dean for Research in the College of Science and Engineering. He is a Fellow of the ACM, IEEE, and AAAS and a member of the CHI Academy. He is best known for his work in recommender systems, social computing, and human-centered computing. He currently serves as co-Chair of the ACM Publications Board and is a candidate for ACM President in the 2022 ACM Elections.

## Lecture Topic

This talk looks at human-centered artificial intelligence research within the context of recommender systems and systems for improving organizational communications, reviewing recent work from the GroupLens Laboratory at the University ofMinnesota on personalization technology and studies ofhuman-technology systems. Prof. Konstan will also talkabout his involvement in professional service with ACM and SIGCHI and the waysin which students and scholars can become involved in and make use of suchactivities.
