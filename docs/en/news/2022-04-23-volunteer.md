## 01 | Background

Founded in Austin in spring 2012, International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI) has reached its tenth anniversary. A year-long program will review the association's history and a decade of academic and industrial human–computer interaction development, while considering its contributions to industry, individuals, and society over the next decade. Distinguished guests will join online forums, symposia, invited articles, and interviews. Through these events, ICACHI hopes to support human–computer interaction in China and establish directions and shared understanding for human–computer interaction advances that benefit everyone's lives.

## 02 | Purpose and Responsibilities

To organize these anniversary events efficiently and effectively, the association seeks volunteers passionate about human–computer interaction and its development to work with the committee and invited speakers. Applications are now open for the ICACHI 10th Anniversary Events Organizing Committee. Join us!

Volunteer Responsibilities:

- Plan the event content and schedule with association leaders and manage publicity and promotion.
- Help arrange guest contributions and interviews, including preparation and postproduction.
- Conduct or assist interviews with leading scholars and edit and proofread news and interview documents.
- Coordinate academic forums, support livestreams, and follow Q&A sessions.
- Develop dedicated WeChat and website sections and publish content representing ICACHI across platforms.

## 03 | Applicants

**Undergraduates, master's and doctoral students, and young scholars** or **working professionals** in human–computer interaction, media, design, and related fields.

## 04 | Number of Positions

10 people

## 05 | Requirements

**Communication and collaboration:** strong teamwork and communication skills

**Time availability:** sufficient discretionary time

**Execution:** ability to attend online team meetings punctually and complete assigned tasks
