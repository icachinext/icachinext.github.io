## Call for Digital Artworks

Chinese CHI 2022, organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), **will be held in Guangzhou, China, on October 22–23, 2022, in a hybrid online and in-person format.** The theme is **Inclusion, Resilience, and New Normal.**

**We invite practitioners and researchers in art and design**, as well as **students**, to **submit digital artwork proposals** to the Chinese CHI 2022 human–computer interaction conference's Digital Art Exhibition. The exhibition will take place both on-site and online.

The Chinese CHI 2022 Digital Art Exhibition aims to create a highly inclusive interdisciplinary space showcasing new media art, novel interaction technologies, original conceptual designs, smart product demonstrations, experimental audiovisual works, explorations of materiality, games, and other interactive experiences. Within the exhibition theme, **our goal is to foster meaningful exchanges among exhibitors that enrich, extend, or reframe relationships between art, design, and technology.**

A committee of experienced specialists in relevant fields will review all submissions; its membership will be announced later. **Selected works will be archived in the Chinese CHI 2022 Digital Art Exhibition yearbook, and their authors will receive an ICACHI exhibition award certificate.**

## Important Dates

- Proposal deadline: August 30, 2022, 23:59 AoE
- Review notification: September 30, 2022, 23:59 AoE

## Submission Guidelines

Submission Guidelines

1. **Initial submissions must contain the following files:**
1. **- Exhibition proposal** (required): an MS Word document of up to four A4 pages describing the project and exhibition plan, excluding references and bibliography.
1. **- Two still images of the project** (required; under 8 MB)
1. **- A supplementary video of the project** (optional)
1. **Accepted works must provide the following materials for publicity and archiving:**
1. - A project description prepared using an InDesign template, which will be sent to selected authors later
1. - Two to five high-resolution still images
1. - A 60-second demonstration video
1. Compress your submission materials into a **.ZIP file** and **email it to:**
1. **artgallery_chichi@163.com**

Note

The submitted .ZIP file must contain no identifying information about the authors. Include your identity and affiliation only in the email.

State whether you wish to participate on-site or online only. Explain how you will provide a good experience for visitors to the physical or online exhibition.

Authors of accepted Chinese CHI 2022 papers are also invited to submit prototypes or demonstrations to the exhibition. Winners of the Student Competition will automatically be invited to the exhibition track.

**Art Gallery: Call for Exhibition**

**We invite practitioners, researchers, and students in art and design-related fields to submit proposals for on-site or online exhibitions at the Chinese CHI 2022 Art Gallery track.** The theme of this year's hybrid Art Gallery is Inclusiveness, Tenacity, and New Normal.

The Art Gallery track of Chinese CHI is intended to be a highly inclusive, interdisciplinary venue for presenting new media art projects, novel interactive techniques, original concept designs, smart product demonstrations, experimental audio-visual compositions, materiality explorations, games, or other forms of interactive experience. **The aim is to provoke meaningful conversations that enrich, extend or reframe the relationships between art, design, and technology, in light of this year’s theme.**

The final selection of exhibitions will be juried by a committee of experienced experts in relevant domains (to be announced later). **Accepted exhibition works will be archived in the Chinese CHI Art Gallery Year Book, and their exhibitors will receive a Chinese CHI Art Gallery Award Certificate from ICACHI.**

## Submission Guidelines

1. **Each submission consists of the following components:**
1. - [required] **Exhibition proposal:** up to 4 A4 pages long MS Word document describing the exhibited project and your plan for exhibition (not including references)
1. - [required] **2 still images of the exhibited projects** (less than 8 MB in total)
1. - [optional] **A supplementary video about the exhibited project**
1. **Accepted submissions will be asked to provide additional mandatory components for publicity and archiving：**
1. - A camera-ready project description in an InDesign template (sent to exhibitor later)
1. - 2-5 High-res still images
1. - A video preview (60-second trailer)
1. Please compress your submission materials into a **.ZIP file** and **send it to the following email address:**
1. **artgallery_chichi@163.com**

## Notice

The submitted .ZIP file MUST NOT include any information that discloses the identity of the exhibitors. Instead, please and only indicate your identity and affiliation in the email.

In the exhibition proposal, please indicate whether you would like to participate in the on-site exhibition or online exhibition only. And please detail your considerations about how you would make the on-site/online exhibition a great experience for the audience.

We also invite authors of accepted papers from the full paper or late-breaking work tracks of Chinese CHI 2022 to submit your prototypes/demos to Art Gallery. Meanwhile, winners of the student competition track will automatically get invited for the Art Gallery track.
