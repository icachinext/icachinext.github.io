## Haipeng Mi's Team

## Creates Kinetic Suzhou Embroidery Work Splendid Orchid Butterflies

## Featured on CCTV

Video from Intangible Cultural Heritage in China

Haipeng Mi's team collaborated with Xue Zhang's team, inheritors of the Xue school of Suzhou embroidery, to create Splendid Orchid Butterflies. Using human–computer interaction technologies such as temperature sensing and shape-changing interfaces, they embedded specially designed shape-memory alloy wires as a framework within the embroidery. Precisely controlled current makes the embroidered butterflies flap their wings and the orchids bloom.

The work was invited to the Jiangsu episode of CCTV's major cultural program Intangible Cultural Heritage in China, broadcast during the 2023 May Day holiday. Guests Beining Sa, Jixiang Shan, and Tongshu Yang personally experienced the interaction.
