## Introduction:

The North American international market is an excellent destination for businesses promoting technology products and services. Chinese CHI 2017 warmly invites you to its industry panel series to examine today's marketing environment and explore strategies. We can also help develop business plans for your organization. The forum enables businesses interested in entering North America to learn more about products and technologies and connect with peers. Invited specialists bring expertise in establishing accelerators, business consulting, university administration, product design, and promotion.

## introduction：

The North America is a huge and responsive market for both imported technology productsand IT services. Whether you are doing, growing your business, or perhaps youare thinking about 'taking the leap' to the North America Chinese CHI 2017 invites you to this seminar and panel discussion series to help you understandthe market, plan your approach, and suggest practical methods and alternativesfor implementing your business and marketing plans. This event is designed tobe a valuable offering to the Chinese entrepreneur community providing information on timely and relevant topics for business executives who isinterested in entering the North America market. The seminar and paneldiscussion series consist of inviting active business leaders in sectors suchas famed business accelerator, business consulting, university administrators,marketing and the lending industry to present useful tools and information.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-03-25-chinesechi2017-forum-panel-02.jpg)

## Topics Include:

(1) Global trends in human–computer interaction, user experience, and artificial intelligence;

(2) Establishing a US-based business incubator;

(3) Effectively using information resources provided by North American universities;

(4) Addressing cross-border issues such as US travel and immigration, establishing a US business, tax filing, currency exchange, and relevant legal matters;

(5) Understanding logistical issues, including using local resources to establish a US business entity;

(6) Sales and promotional strategies, including market outlook, foundational approaches, and lessons learned.

## Key topics include:

(1) Global development trend of human-computer interaction, userexperience and AI.

(2) Setup of U.S. based incubators who can provide you with guidance, expertise, and resources to help your business grow; seed money and valuable connections to investors.

(3) Intellectual resources provided by NorthAmerica based educational entities.

(4)Dealing with “border issues”, such as U.S. travel and immigration planning,U.S. business planning, taxation, currency exchange and legal considerations;

(5) Understanding “logistics issues”, such as establishing a USA business identity and fulfillment using local resources;

(6) “Sales/marketing strategies”, including market overview, sales/marketing fundamentals, lessons and pitfalls.

## Benefits:

Participants benefit from experts' experience and knowledge. Through discussions and interactive Q&A, you can speak directly with specialists and receive immediate feedback. With an international outlook and renowned entrepreneurs and educational leaders from China and abroad, this business exchange forum offers Chinese entrepreneurs an excellent opportunity to broaden their perspectives, discover business opportunities, and prepare for international markets.

## Benefits:

Attendees benefit from the insight of professionals who are substantively involved in the technology industry and maintain up-to-the-minute knowledge about trends and developments affecting your future decision. These events are structured in an interactive format which encourages questions from attendees and allows for feedback. An international entrepreneur-to-entrepreneur business net working will help Chinese entrepreneurs to recognize the potential that lies indialoguing and collaborating with other international entrepreneurs who havegained success both domestically and internationally in the technology industry.
