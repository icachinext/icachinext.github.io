Psychology was a general subject at the Imperial University of Peking's teacher-training institution from its founding in 1902. A psychology laboratory followed in 1920, a department in 1980, and China's first university School of Psychology in 2001. Beijing Normal University's School of Psychology has distinguished scholars, strong research capabilities, and consistently leads national disciplinary rankings.

In 2015, Beijing Normal University's School of Psychology established China's first user experience (UX) specialization in its Master of Applied Psychology (MAP) program. Its first students enrolled in September 2016. The program trains applied, practice-oriented, interdisciplinary professionals who understand cognitive psychology and can use psychological experiments and analysis to solve HCI/UX problems. Drawing on leading domestic and international resources, it is building a world-class faculty and inviting experienced industry mentors. Project-based teaching develops practical skills: students participate in complete development projects alongside coursework. Cooperation with leading universities and businesses includes shared experimental platforms, curriculum development, international exchange, and joint research.
