## ICACHI Young Scholars Committee

### Yun Wang

Yun Wang earned postgraduate degrees from the Product Design and Innovation Laboratory at France's ENSAM in 2009 and ENSCI in 2013. Since 2016, she has been a lecturer in industrial design at Beihang University's School of Mechanical Engineering and Automation. She is now a doctoral student at Tsinghua University's Academy of Arts and Design and Tsinghua University's Future Laboratory, researching perceptual experience design and interdisciplinary design thinking education.

Tsinghua University Future Laboratory

Tsinghua University's Future Laboratory was established on December 15, 2017. **Its creation was a major step in Tsinghua University's research-system reform and interdisciplinary development.** As Tsinghua University's future-oriented technology incubator, think tank, and explorer of future science, it pursues innovation, new frontiers, and disciplinary integration. Through interdisciplinary research and exchange, it seeks major original breakthroughs and advances disciplinary development. **Its vision brings computing, communication, media, and art together, using original, interdisciplinary, disruptive, boundary-free innovation to transform cognition, interaction, and logic and advance an integrated human–machine–object society. Culture and entrepreneurship help drive leapfrog industrial development.**

## The Laboratory's Updated 2021 Introduction Video:

The Future Laboratory is an international interdisciplinary platform. **It has more than 100 master's and doctoral students, postdoctoral researchers, researchers, and interns; over 60% have studied overseas, and they represent more than 40 disciplinary backgrounds**, including mathematics, chemical engineering, computing, electronics, mechanical engineering, philosophy, education, psychology, film and animation, information art and design, industrial design, environmental design, and visual communication. **Research teams include centers for future materials design, aging user experience and service systems (AeX), perception and consciousness, cultural and creative design, and a lifelong learning laboratory.** Activities throughout the year include workshops, symposia, and public lectures.
