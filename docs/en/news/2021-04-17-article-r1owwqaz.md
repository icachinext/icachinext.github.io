## Congratulations

Professor Xiangshi Ren was elected a foreign member of the Engineering Academy of Japan in February 2021 and a fellow of the Information Processing Society of Japan in April 2021.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-04-17-article-r1owwqaz-01.png)

**Xiangshi Ren** is a tenured professor in the School of Information at Kochi University of Technology, director of its Human-Engaged Computing institute, and lifetime honorary president of International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI). He has been a visiting researcher at IBM Almaden and a visiting professor at the University of Toronto and several Chinese universities, and is a senior member of ACM and IEEE. With nearly 30 years of fundamental human–computer interaction research and education, he is recognized as a pioneer. His work has received awards from ACM, IEEE, CCF, and IPSJ-related conferences and journals. He has trained numerous PhD graduates and postdoctoral researchers, including students from more than ten countries, now active across Asia, Africa, Europe, and Oceania. His research, academic service, and social engagement connect Eastern and Western scientific communities. Considering humanity's future and ideal technological relationships, he has proposed Human-Engaged Computing (Human-Engaged Computing), grounded in Eastern balanced moderation, and is advancing this new research area.

**Keywords:** human–computer interaction; user interface and interaction design; mobile computing; human performance models; fundamental pen, gesture, eye-tracking, game, haptic, and mindfulness interaction; soft-skill development; human–computer interaction for older adults; Human-Engaged Computing

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-04-17-article-r1owwqaz-02.png)

**The Engineering Academy of Japan** was founded in 1987. Its members are university, industry, and government leaders who have made distinguished engineering, science, and technology contributions. It joined the International Council of Academies of Engineering and Technological Sciences (CAETS) in 1990.

**Website:** [https://www.eaj.or.jp/](https://www.eaj.or.jp/)

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2021-04-17-article-r1owwqaz-03.png)

**The Information Processing Society of Japan** is Japan's largest information processing academic society, principally covering computing and communications. Founded in Tokyo on April 22, 1960, it is a full member of the International Federation for Information Processing.

**Website:** [https://www.ipsj.or.jp/index.html](https://www.ipsj.or.jp/index.html)
