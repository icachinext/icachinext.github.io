## Call for Student Volunteers

Chinese CHI 2022, organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), **will be held in Guangzhou, China, on October 22–23, 2022, in a hybrid online and in-person format.** The theme is **Inclusion, Resilience, and New Normal.**

We are looking for friendly, enthusiastic volunteers to help organize Chinese CHI 2022 efficiently and ensure its success. Because the conference will use a hybrid format, **we welcome applications from both online and on-site student volunteers.**

## Recruitment Information

## Applicants

- **Undergraduate, master's, or doctoral students** in human–computer interaction, media, design, or related fields who have been enrolled for at least one semester during the academic year before Chinese CHI 2022. Relevant work experience is preferred.

## Student Volunteer Responsibilities

- **All accepted student volunteers will serve between August 15 and November 15,** according to their working groups' schedules.
- **Volunteers should attend training on August 20, with the date to be confirmed.** Multiple sessions will accommodate different working groups.
- Volunteers should carry out the duties required by their working groups.
- Specific group requirements are listed below, subject to final arrangements:
- **Finance:** 2–3 volunteers
- **Publicity:** 2–3 volunteers
- **Volunteer Coordination:** 10 volunteers, primarily coordinating volunteer work across groups and maintaining order on-site
- **Workshops:** 2 volunteers, primarily preparing, storing, and managing workshop materials and physical resources
- **Secretariat:** 26 volunteers
- Document preparation, organization, and archiving: 2
- Software and hardware support: 5
- Conference document translation: 3
- Participant reception: 10
- Supplies management and venue coordination: 3
- Distribution of conference materials: 3

***** Applicants should have writing skills, strong English, good communication and teamwork skills, and attention to detail.

**Social Media:** 9 volunteers

- Writing and publishing pre-conference press releases: 2
- Media invitations: 2
- Conference note-taking: 2
- Writing and publishing conference news releases: 1
- Media liaison and public opinion monitoring: 2

**Sponsorship:** 4 volunteers

- Delivering sponsor benefits and coordinating with other groups: 2
- Overall communication and coordination, submission of relevant conference documents, and reimbursements: 2

**Exhibition:** 15–20 volunteers

- Online promotion, news, photography, and video: 1–2
- Exhibition installation and dismantling: 15
- Poster and display-panel design: 1–2

**Conference Organization:** 2 volunteers

***** Good English and proficiency with Excel and Word are required.

## Application

- To apply as a student volunteer, **email your CV, named with your name followed by Volunteer Application CV, to:**
- **sv_chichi2022@163.com**
- **Use the email subject Chinese CHI 2022 Student Volunteer Application and state your preferred working group**, optionally ranking your choices.

### Deadlines

Responsibilities

- **Application deadline**: August 15, 2022, 23:59 AoE
- Note: **Shortlisted applicants will be contacted by email within 2–3 working days after recruitment closes.** Applicants not shortlisted will receive no additional email notification.
- Chinese CHI 2022 volunteers may receive event souvenirs and have opportunities to interact closely with conference participants and organizers!

## Student Volunteer Recruitment

**The Chinese CHI 2022 conference** organized by the International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (“ICACHI”) **will be held in Guangzhou, China from October 22 to 23, 2022.** It will be held online and offline. The theme of this conference is: Inclusion, Resilience, New Normal.

**We are looking for friendly and enthusiastic volunteers to help us ensure the success of Chinese CHI 2022!** Given that this conference will be held in a hybrid format **, we would like to recruit both online and off-line student volunteers.**

## Student Volunteer Recruitment

- **Undergraduate, master or doctoral students** in related fields such as human-computer interaction, media and design
