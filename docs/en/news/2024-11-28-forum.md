Forum Group Photo

Through future healthcare, intelligent manufacturing and robotics, autonomous driving and the low-altitude economy, and research commercialization and global expansion, the forum explored how technological innovation, efficiency improvements, and research-to-industry transformation can drive business growth. 'Starting from current industry research and achievements at SUSTech's School of Design, and drawing on Shenzhen's rich industrial and supply-chain resources, we use universities as bridges and strong catalysts for research, industry, and application,' said Xiaohua Sun, chair professor at Southern University of Science and Technology's School of Design and deputy director of its Robotics Institute.

## Presentation Recap

The forum united academic and industry representatives: Academician of Chinese Academy of Sciences and Southern University of Science and Technology medical school dean Songlin Wang; Intel Labs China deputy director and Digital Health Laboratory director Peng Wang; Luoxi Hao of Tongji University's College of Architecture and Urban Planning; AgiBot co-founder and Shanghai Jiao Tong University professor Weixin Yan; Beijing Benz's Menghua Song; BOGE CEO Langxi Lin; Great Wall Motor smart space product director Baoyong Zhang; The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) Low-Altitude Economy Research Institute project lead Dengbo He; HKUST Shenzhen Blue Bay Incubator and Collaborative Innovation Research Institute executive director Lingyun Gao; China Galaxy Securities chief investment adviser and Shanghai institutional business president Jingyuan Liu; and BGI Innovation Center and BGI Pets CEO Liang Liu. They shared research, frontier technologies, successful cases, and practical experience on topics including future digital-intelligent and homeostatic medicine, generative AI for integrated healthcare, and AGI-enabled general-purpose robots. Their analyses of innovation opportunities and trends offered businesses inspiration and applicable approaches for competitive breakthroughs and growth.

## Signing the R&D and Education Cooperation Agreement

During the conference, Professor Xiaohua Sun of SUSTech's School of Design, Intel Labs China deputy director Peng Wang, and Professor Luoxi Hao of Tongji University witnessed the launch of the Comprehensive Health Research Consortium. SUSTech's School of Design, its Education and Training Center, and BOGE Group signed an R&D and education cooperation agreement. A SUSTech-led group standard for ICV intelligent cockpit and driving HMI design and evaluation was previewed.

## Closed-Door Discussion Recap

Beijing Benz's presentation on digitalizing MPS production-line management inspired manufacturing companies. Universities discussed collaboration on MPS lean-management training, while the company advised on research into process optimization in digital production environments and pledged ongoing support. Companies and universities explored research partnerships on humanoid robot dexterous hands and gait control and signed agreements on indoor service robot development and robotics education. Universities will help improve robot usability through LLMs and AIGC and connect robotics education products with users. Several companies agreed on joint R&D intentions and follow-up schedules with universities; businesses explored multiple forms of cooperation with one another. Students also learned from guests through lively discussions.
