The fourth Chinese CHI 2016 workshop, co-located with CHI 2016, will take place in San Jose, USA, on May 8, 2016. The submission deadline is January 6, 2016.

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction, Chinese CHI is an academic–industry workshop for Chinese human–computer interaction designers and researchers worldwide. We warmly welcome submissions and participation.
