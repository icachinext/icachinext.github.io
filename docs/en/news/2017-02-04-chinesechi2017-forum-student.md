Chinese CHI 2017, taking place in Guangzhou, China, on June 8–9, 2017 ([http://chchi2017.icachi.org](http://chchi2017.icachi.org)), invites doctoral, master's, and undergraduate students from different areas of HCI to its Student Forum (SF). Organized as a workshop, SF enables students to present research to HCI and user experience experts and learn together. It offers constructive feedback on thesis work, career guidance, and exchanges with peers in related fields. Its topics match those of Chinese CHI 2017; see [http://chchi2017.icachi.org](http://chchi2017.icachi.org). Students from user experience, interaction design, computer science, cognitive science, psychology, sociology, and other disciplines are welcome, as are all conference participants.

Students (doctoral, master, and bachelor) within different areas of HCI are invited to take part in the Student Forum (SF) at Chinese CHI 2017 (http://chchi2017.icachi.org). The SF will take the form of a workshop where the students have the possibility to present and discuss their research with some of the leading HCI/UX researchers. The purpose of the SF is for the students to receive constructive feedback on their (thesis) work, get advice about future developments, and share ideas and experiences with peer students in related areas. The research areas covered by the SF are the same as the Chinese CHI 2017 conference generally and can be found at [http://chchi2017.icachi.org](http://chchi2017.icachi.org) Students may come from different academic disciplines, such as user experience, interaction design, computer science, cognitive science, psychology, or sociology. All participants from the conference are welcome to join.

## Submission

Apply by emailing the SF chair a proposal of no more than two A4 pages containing: (1) the research title and author name; (2) research objectives, questions, and methods; (3) key results to date; and (4) remaining work. Also submit a personal statement of at most one A4 page describing your background, completed and planned research, relevant experience, and motivation and expectations for attending SF. Submissions need not be anonymous. Accepted applicants must attend.

## Submission

To apply, email the SF chair a concise proposal of at most 2 A4 pages, including: 1) title of your research and your name, 2) research goals, questions and approach, 3) studies conducted so far including key results, 4) remaining research efforts. Additionally, write a biographical note of at most 1 A4 page describing your background, completed and planned studies, relevant work experience, and a paragraph of your expectations and motivation to attend the SF. Please note that the submissions should not be anonymized. Upon acceptance, you have to attend the SF at the conference.

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-02-04-chinesechi2017-forum-student-05.jpg)

## Review

Proposals will be assessed by a panel comprising the SF chair and Chinese CHI experts. Around twelve speakers will be selected based on their anticipated contribution to the breadth and depth of discussion.

## Review

The proposals will be evaluated by a judging panel consisting of the SF chairs and additional senior Chinese CHI experts. Approximately 12 presenters will be selected to participate in the SF. Presenters will be selected based on their anticipated contributions to the breadth and depth of the intellectual discussions of the forum.
