Chinese CHI ([http://chchi2017.icachi.org](http://chchi2017.icachi.org)) will take place at Guangzhou Baiyun International Convention Center on June 7–9. To introduce participants to HCI skills and current topics, the conference offers workshops by expert instructors on foundational methods, product and service design, payments and motion design, and experience-goal management.

UXPA China is honored to collaborate with the international Chinese human–computer interaction conference, pooling resources for a high-quality professional gathering. Join Chinese researchers and practitioners from around the world on June 7–9 for academic–industry exchange.

Workshops 1–5 provide three-hour introductory training for entry-level user researchers, designers, and related professionals, taught by instructors with six to ten years of experience.

Workshops 6–12 offer specialized and advanced content for practitioners with relevant experience.

**Category:** Introductory Workshop

### Speaker

User experience strategist and specialist in user research and service design

More than eight years of user experience consulting experience

## Workshop 6: Eye Tracker Principles and Use

**Category:** Specialist workshop

### Speaker

![Article image](https://media.icachi.org/news/2017-03-16-workshop-06.png)

### Zhenwei Zhang

CEO

iView Technology

**Language:** Chinese

**Time:** Wednesday, June 7, 2017, 14:00–17:00

### Background:

User experience should be improved through data rather than intuition. Compared with other information, gaze is direct and natural, providing useful feedback on experience. Eye tracking and eye trackers study gaze by detecting and recording changes in parameters as people observe the world, producing intuitive visual behavior data. We have used eye tracking to improve UX in several areas. If you want to enhance experience through qualitative and quantitative data, join the discussion. Beyond optimizing existing experiences, we may discover new applications for eye tracking.

### Course Outline:

1. Introduction to eye tracking and eye tracker assessment principles

2. Building experience models and eye-tracking assessment and analysis plans

3. Cases of assessing and improving UX through eye tracking

4. Experimental design and practice

### Participant Benefits:

- Understand basic eye-tracking principles and applications

- Understand practical applications of eye-tracking assessment systems in important scenarios

- Try developing eye-tracking assessment plans to evaluate and improve selected targets

### Intended Audience:

- User experience designers

- Product managers

- People seeking to improve human–computer interaction experiences
