## About the Lecture

Chinese typewriters may seem like short-lived, insignificant technological curiosities, a sideshow to Chinese computing's real story. In fact, they underpin China's contemporary achievements in digital computing and new media and laid foundations for today's IT giants. Predictive text, text corpora, adaptive databases, and even digital graphics were pioneered in mechanical Chinese typewriting. Above all, these supposedly impossible machines showed that modernization did not depend on abolishing Chinese characters, as Lu Xun and others assumed. As one early engineer put it, Chinese characters are innocent.

Chinese typewriters might seem like short-lived, insignificant technological curiosities—a sideshow to the real story of Chinese computing. In fact, they underpin China's contemporary achievements in digital computing and new media and laid foundations for today's Chinese IT juggernaut. Predictive text, text corpora, adaptive databases, and even digital graphics were pioneered in mechanical Chinese typewriting. Above all, Chinese typewriters, machines many assumed impossible to build, showed that Chinese modernization did not, as Lu Xun and others assumed, depend on abolishing character-based Chinese script. As one early engineer phrased it, "Chinese characters are innocent."
