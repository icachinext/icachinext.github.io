Both developed and developing countries face population aging. By 2050, people over 65 are projected to comprise 27% of the population in developed countries and 15% in developing countries. Yet the rapid digital revolution has not adequately benefited older adults. Although their share of the population is increasing, mainstream HCI research remains focused on young people, gradually excluding older adults from the opportunities of the digital age.

**ITAP 2016** will take place on **October 21, 2016** in **Kochi, Japan**, focusing on coordinated development of **technologies for older adults based on human–computer interaction**. More than 100 world-class researchers will be invited to exchange ideas and advance HCI.

**Submissions are now invited**. Accepted papers will appear in a special issue of ***Interacting with Computers***.

## Submission Categories

ITAP 2016 welcomes contributions in Human Computer Interaction, Human Factors, Interaction Design, and related fields.
