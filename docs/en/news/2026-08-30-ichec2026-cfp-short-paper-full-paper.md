## Conference Overview

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), the International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing **(ICHEC 2026)** will be hosted by **City University of Macau** on **November 23–26, 2026** at Wynn Macau in Macau, China. The themes are Inclusivity, Experience, and Innovation.

In 2025, Chinese CHI was officially renamed ICHEC to reflect broader inclusion and international participation. In 2026, we invite human–computer interaction scholars, researchers, and industry leaders worldwide to share findings and experience and explore future possibilities for human–computer interaction.
