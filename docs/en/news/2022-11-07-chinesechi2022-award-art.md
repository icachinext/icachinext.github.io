Inclusion . Resilience . New Normal

**The Future Intelligence Digital Art and Design Exhibition at the 2022 International human–computer interaction Conference, Chinese CHI 2022, received 246 works**. Sources included recommended outstanding entries from the China Creative and National Digital Art Design Competition and Future Designer Competition, Chinese CHI 2022 exhibition submissions, and Student Competition submissions. **We especially thank both competitions' organizing committees for their support!**

An experienced expert panel conducted the reviews. **In the first round, 18 reviewers fairly assessed 246 works, shortlisting 72; 54 were selected for the online 3D virtual gallery and physical interactive gallery. After blinded second-round review by five renowned experts, congratulations to the following 15 award-winning works. The results are:**

## Third Prize

Eight awards in total

- **Work:** Window
- **Authors:** Borou Yu, Jiajian Min, and Mengying Zeng (Harvard University)
- **Work:** Glasses-Free 3D: A Dimension-Breaking Mystery Box
- **Author:** Zesheng Chen (University of Macau)
- **Work:** Digital Cells
- **Author:** Lei Tian (Nanjing University of the Arts)
- **Work:** An Introductory Art Education Platform for Children Through Traditional Art
- **Authors:** Mingyu Han, Yi Ji, Yihui Cai, and Beier Hu (Guangdong University of Technology)
- **Work:** Visualizing the Art of Tai Chi Kung Fu
- **Authors:** Ye Wang, Mingxuan Zhang, and Tiantian Chen (Tongji University and Shanghai Theatre Academy)
- **Work:** Undercurrent
- **Authors:** Zaiwei Liu, Kejia Zhang, Yunhua Zhang, and Zerong Hong (Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts)
- **Work:** Blackbird Collection: An NFT Digital Platform for Artists
- **Authors:** Xiaoyu Zhou and Ziwei Pan (Shandong University)
- **Work:** LIGHTracer
- **Authors:** Ziqian Bai, Yimin Wang, and Jiawei Guo (Southern University of Science and Technology)

## Second Prize

Four awards in total

- **Work:** Seeking Sound / Meet Intimate
- **Authors:** Yufei Liu, Qiaoru Xu, and Junyao Fan (Hunan University)
- **Work:** One Shenzhen Bay Ecological Environment Makes the Beauty
- **Authors:** Hao Li, Yanming Sheng, and Jianwei Zheng (City University of Hong Kong)
- **Work:** The Consumer Cocoon
- **Author:** Jingjing Wang (East China University of Science and Technology)
- **Work:** New Weird Tales
- **Authors:** Yong Wang and Yunxiao Chu (Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts)

## First Prize

Two awards in total

- **Work:** Character Beast Paradise
- **Author:** Tian Huang (Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts)
- **Work:** Travel Mystery Box
- **Authors:** Fan Yuan and Lingran Wang (Huazhong University of Science and Technology)

## Diamond Prize

One award in total

- **Work:** PHYGITAL DISTRIBUTED CITY
- **Authors:** Anqi Wang, Ze Gao, and Yue Hua (The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and University College London)

**The teams behind the award-winning works above will receive cash prizes sponsored by Guangzhou Tianyi Electronics Co., Ltd., and Wucai Huafu cultural and creative gift boxes sponsored by Guangzhou 37 Interactive Entertainment Technology Co., Ltd.**

The cash prize for each award-winning work is:

- Diamond Prize: RMB 5,000 per work
- First Prize: RMB 2,500 per work
- Second Prize: RMB 1,250 per work
- Third Prize: RMB 625 per work

**We sincerely thank Guangzhou Tianyi Electronics Co., Ltd., and Guangzhou 37 Interactive Entertainment Technology Co., Ltd., for their title sponsorship of this competition.**

**Congratulations again to all award-winning works!**
