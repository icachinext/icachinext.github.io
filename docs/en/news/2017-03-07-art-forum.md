## Art and Technology Forum

## Art and Technology Forum

## Introduction:

Interdisciplinary exchange is increasingly important for scientists, artists, and other practitioners. Technology extends the body and senses, while art can rebalance perception and foster a holistic awareness. To explore this, the Art and Technology Forum invites representatives from both communities to share their work. Encounters between apparently unrelated fields may expand each other's thinking. The foundation also intends to support long-term collaboration, with this forum laying the groundwork. Under the theme Interaction · Ecology, discussions encompass interactive moving images, design technologies, digital products, media installations, software, games, and other creative interactive work. Interaction now permeates life, entertainment, and work, profoundly affecting contemporary design and creativity.

## Introduction:

Nowadays, the intersection of subjects is becoming more and more important in the science and art or even in any other filed. Technology is the extension of human sense and the art, in a way, rectifies the proportion of all kinds of senses, which will ultimately reach an “Integral Consciousness”. In order to reach the “Integral Consciousness”, Art and Technology Forum will invite the representatives from scientific and technological circles and artist to share their research spheres. The confrontation of ideas may be totally unrelated, but it could probably widen our thinking border. The Foundation will also promote the the communication and cooperation of art and technology for a long time, expecting that the forum would lay a solid foundation for further crossover cultivation. With the theme of “interaction·ecology”, the forum will have a deep exchange on every whit of the crossover of art and technology, including, interactive image works, design techniques, digital products, media diverse, art works, software, games and other fruits of creative interactive. Interaction has integrated everywhere in our life, entertainment and work, which is also exerting a significant impact on the design and creative work.

## Submission Guidelines

## Format and Participation Requirements

Proposed artworks can include many different forms including sound, vision, performance, dance and theatre, as well as works that are not so easily categorized. Leonardo Transactions is concerned with art, so we will not accept design papers or pure HCI, the papers can be about HCI and art (interactive art) for example or any other science/technology art connection.

The submission should include the following:

- Description of the work: including written texts, images, sound, video
- Artist's statement: giving the background to the ideas and creation process Inspiration, sources, materials and any research related to the artwork
- Artist Profile: short bio, track record including previous exhibitions
- Papers must be in the Leonardo paper format and it must be no longer than 2 pages
- Please upload the file under the name of your project
