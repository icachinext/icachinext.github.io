## Conference Overview

Organized by International Chinese Association of Computer Human Interaction (ICACHI), the **International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing** (ICHEC 2025, formerly Chinese CHI) will take place **November 21–23, 2025** at **Singapore Management University** in Singapore. The themes are **Inclusion**, **Intelligence**, and **Connection**.

## Early-Bird Registration

Conference registration is now open. **The early-bird deadline is October 29, 2025.**

Standard and student registration rates will rise after this date. Register early to enjoy discounted prices!

Note:

- **All registration deadlines are at the end of the stated day in Singapore time (GMT+8).**
- All fees are listed in **Singapore dollars (S$)** and **include Singapore's 9% Goods and Services Tax (GST).**

| Type | Register by Oct 29 | Register by Nov 05 | Register from Nov 06 |
| --- | --- | --- | ---

|
| Standard | S$688 | S$788 | S$888 |
| Student | S$288 | S$388 | S$488 |
